# Atlas Whistleblower and Non-Retaliation Policy v1.0 (POL-WHISTLE-v1.0)

**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Co-owner:** Maya Schwartz (VP People)
**Effective:** 2028-01-01

## Purpose

Provides every Atlas employee, contractor, and partner with a safe channel to raise concerns about suspected misconduct — including violations of POL-CONDUCT-v3.1, POL-DATA-v3.1, POL-ABAC-v2.0, securities-law concerns relating to Atlas Ledger and SOX §404, and any concern affecting customer safety (e.g., a regression similar to INC-2028-0019 at Cresta Health Network).

## Channels

- Manager (where appropriate).
- HR (Maya Schwartz) for employment matters.
- Compliance (Dr Sara Vance) for regulatory, financial, or customer-safety matters.
- Anonymous third-party hotline (24x7, all regions).
- Audit Committee of the Board (for senior-leader concerns).

## Anti-retaliation

Atlas prohibits any adverse employment action — termination, demotion, compensation impact, exclusion, harassment — taken because a person raised a good-faith concern. Retaliation is itself a terminable violation of POL-CONDUCT-v3.1.

## Process

1. Report received.
2. Triage by Compliance (Dr Sara Vance) within 5 business days.
3. Investigator assigned. Conflicts checked.
4. Investigation concluded with documented findings.
5. Closure communication to reporter where permissible.

## Confidentiality

Identity of the reporter is protected to the maximum extent permitted by law, with exceptions only for genuine necessity (e.g., a regulator subpoena).

## Statistics

Annual aggregate statistics on report volume, categories, and closure are published to the Atlas Compliance Council and summarised to the Board Audit Committee.
