# RFC — Atlas Regulator Engagement Framework

**Status:** Draft for Atlas Compliance Council review
**Author:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)
**Target decision date:** 2029-Q1

## Problem

Atlas interacts with multiple regulators across regions and regimes — U.S. HHS for HIPAA-relevant matters at Cresta Health Network, EU national DPAs for GDPR matters across EMEA customers, U.S. SEC indirectly via Meridian Bank's SOX §404 reliance, future EU AI Office for any AI Act enforcement, U.S. BIS for export-controls matters. Each engagement currently happens through ad-hoc legal-and-compliance coordination.

## Proposal

Establish a Regulator Engagement Framework covering:

1. **Single point of contact per regulator regime.** CCO (Dr Sara Vance) for HIPAA/GDPR/AI Act/HHS; CFO for SEC/SOX; General Counsel for litigation-adjacent matters; CCO + General Counsel jointly for export controls.

2. **Incident-triggered engagement playbooks.** When an incident has potential regulatory implications (Cresta INC-2028-0019 is the canonical example), a pre-rehearsed playbook engages.

3. **Proactive engagement registry.** Atlas attends industry-regulator working groups where eligible (e.g., EU AI Office stakeholder forums on building-automation AI applications).

4. **Audit support for customer regulators.** When Meridian Bank's external SOX auditor or Cresta Health Network's HIPAA auditor requests Atlas evidence, a standard package is pre-staged for fast delivery.

## Customer impact

- Customers benefit from a credible "Atlas can support your regulator interactions" narrative at sales cycle and renewal.
- Reduces ad-hoc burden on Customer Success (Aisha Okonkwo) and Engineering (Priya Anand, Marcus Ortiz) during regulator-active periods.
