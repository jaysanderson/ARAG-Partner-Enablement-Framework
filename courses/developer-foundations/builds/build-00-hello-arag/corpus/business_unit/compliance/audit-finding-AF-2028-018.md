# Audit Finding AF-2028-018 · Atlas Ledger Quarterly Access Review Cadence Slip

**Auditor:** External SOC 2 auditor (NORAM)
**Audit cycle:** Q2 2028 SOC 2 Type II window
**Status:** Closed
**Severity:** Major
**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO — Atlas Ledger product owner)

## Finding

The Atlas SOX §404 position commits Atlas to quarterly access review for every Atlas Ledger customer deployment. The Q1 2028 review for the Meridian Bank Atlas Ledger production deployment was completed 14 calendar days past the policy-defined window due to a handoff issue between the Compliance access-review team and Meridian Bank's IT liaison.

No inappropriate access was identified in the review itself, but the cadence slip is a deviation from POL-SECBASE-v2.0 control AC-2 and from the SOX §404 position.

## Affected customers

- Meridian Bank (sole Atlas Ledger flagship at present).

## Remediation

- Q2 2028 review completed on schedule.
- Calendar invitations now sent 30 days in advance with explicit IT-liaison ack.
- Compliance dashboard now tracks Atlas Ledger access-review timeliness as a Tier 1 KPI.

## Closure criteria

- Q2 2028 review on time: Yes.
- Calendar automation in place: Yes.
- KPI live: Yes.

## Closed

2028-07-15 — Dr Sara Vance accepted closure with Meridian Bank's compliance officer present.
