# Audit Finding AF-2028-040 · GDPR DPIA Refresh for Cresta BuildingHub

**Auditor:** Atlas internal audit (EMEA Compliance)
**Audit cycle:** Q3 2028
**Status:** Open — remediation in flight
**Severity:** Minor
**Owner:** Aisha Okonkwo (Director of Customer Success)
**Co-owner:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)

## Finding

The Atlas BuildingHub deployment at Cresta Health Network has a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) on file dated 2026-Q3. GDPR Article 35 requires DPIAs to be reviewed when there is a change in the risk profile. The 2028 firmware regression (INC-2028-0019, AF-2028-007) and the subsequent HIPAA-impact assessment process constitute a material risk-profile change that should trigger DPIA refresh.

## Affected customers

- Cresta Health Network (EMEA sites only — NORAM Cresta sites have a separate HIPAA-only assessment).

## Remediation plan

- DPIA refresh scheduled for 2028-Q4 with Cresta's DPO co-signing.
- DPIA-refresh trigger criteria added to POL-DATA-v3.1 quarterly review checklist (updated language to include "any audit_finding of Major severity affecting Regulated data for an EMEA customer").

## Target closure

2028-Q4.
