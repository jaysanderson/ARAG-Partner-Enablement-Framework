# Atlas Records Management and Legal Hold Policy v1.0 (POL-RECORDS-v1.0)

**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Effective:** 2028-01-15
**Review:** Annual

## Purpose

Establishes the records taxonomy and the legal-hold process used to suspend deletion when records become relevant to anticipated or active legal proceedings, regulatory inquiry, or material customer disputes.

## Records taxonomy

| Record class | Retention | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Board minutes and resolutions | Permanent | General Counsel |
| Customer contracts | 7 years post-termination | Sales Operations |
| Customer audit reports (incl. AF-2028-001, AF-2028-007) | 10 years | Compliance |
| Engineering RFCs and design docs | 7 years | Engineering (Marcus Ortiz / Priya Anand) |
| Incident reports (INC-2027-0142, INC-2027-0188, INC-2028-0019, INC-2028-0034) | 7 years | Engineering + Compliance |
| HR personnel files | 7 years post-separation | HR (Maya Schwartz) |
| Financial ledger records | 7 years | Finance |
| Customer-data per POL-RETENTION-v1.0 | Per schedule | Per product owner |

## Legal hold

A legal hold is initiated by the General Counsel with written notice to Dr Sara Vance and the affected record owners. On receipt:

1. All scheduled deletion for in-scope record classes is suspended for the hold scope.
2. A custodian list is created and notified within 48 hours.
3. Hold acknowledgements are tracked.
4. The hold is reviewed every 6 months for continuing applicability.
