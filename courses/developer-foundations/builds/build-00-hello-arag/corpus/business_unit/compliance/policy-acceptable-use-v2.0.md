# Atlas Acceptable Use Policy v2.0 (POL-AUP-v2.0)

**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Co-owner:** Maya Schwartz (VP People)
**Effective:** 2028-01-01

## Purpose

Defines acceptable use of Atlas-managed IT resources — laptops, network access, email, collaboration tools, source control, and customer systems Atlas employees access in the course of work.

## Scope

All Atlas employees, contractors, and partners with Atlas-issued credentials.

## Permitted use

- Work product directly tied to an Atlas business unit deliverable.
- Reasonable incidental personal use that does not consume material resources or compromise security.
- Approved professional development including certification study aligned to role.

## Prohibited use

- Storing or processing Regulated data (HIPAA, GDPR-personal-data, SOX-financial-data) outside Atlas-approved systems. INC-2028-0019 (BuildingHub firmware) reinforced this control; Cresta Health Network HVAC alert handling is the canonical reference.
- Disabling endpoint protection.
- Bypassing the approved-tools registry — particularly for LLM use (see POL-AIUSE-v1.0).
- Sharing credentials, including between Atlas employees.
- Use of personally-owned removable media on Atlas endpoints.

## Customer system access

When accessing customer environments (e.g. Norvale Energy SCADA, Meridian Bank Atlas Ledger admin plane, Cresta Health Network BuildingHub controllers, Halcyon Logistics routing engine, Talos Steelworks FieldOps installation), employees must:

- Use the customer's approved access path.
- Log activity per the customer's runbook (e.g., RB-E220-Cooling-001, RB-Logix-MemLeak-002, RB-BldHub-Firmware-003, RB-FieldOps-Sync-004).
- Never copy customer data outside the customer environment except via the documented support-data extraction process.

## Enforcement

Violations are reviewed by HR (Maya Schwartz) and Compliance (Dr Sara Vance) jointly. Findings under POL-CONDUCT-v3.1 apply.
