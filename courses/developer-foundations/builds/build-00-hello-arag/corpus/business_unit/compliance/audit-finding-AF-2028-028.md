# Audit Finding AF-2028-028 · Cresta HIPAA Incident Notification Template Drift

**Auditor:** Atlas internal audit (EMEA Compliance, follow-up to AF-2028-007)
**Audit cycle:** Q3 2028
**Status:** Closed
**Severity:** Minor
**Owner:** Aisha Okonkwo (Director of Customer Success)
**Co-owner:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)

## Finding

AF-2028-007 closure committed Atlas to a standardised HIPAA-relevant customer notification template. The Q3 follow-up audit found two notifications issued during Q2 2028 to Cresta Health Network used a non-current draft of the template that omitted the field tracking the §164.308(a)(8) periodic technical evaluation reference.

No notification was deficient under HIPAA Breach Notification Rule (the notifications were not breach notifications; they were impact-update notifications during a non-breach incident). However, internal-policy drift was substantiated.

## Affected customers

- Cresta Health Network — Aisha Okonkwo personally confirmed full context was delivered out-of-band.

## Remediation

- Template canonicalised in the Atlas Compliance docs portal; older versions removed.
- Customer Success training refreshed.
- Quarterly template-audit added to AF-2028-007 follow-up plan; first audit clean.

## Closure criteria

- Template canonical: Yes.
- Training delivered: Yes.
- One follow-up audit clean: Yes.

## Closed

2028-09-28 — Dr Sara Vance accepted closure.
