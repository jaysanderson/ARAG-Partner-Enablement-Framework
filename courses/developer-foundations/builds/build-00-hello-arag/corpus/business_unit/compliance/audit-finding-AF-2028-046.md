# Audit Finding AF-2028-046 · Atlas Ledger Penetration Test Recommendation Backlog

**Auditor:** External SOC 2 auditor (NORAM)
**Audit cycle:** Q4 2028
**Status:** Open — remediation in flight
**Severity:** Major
**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)
**Co-owner:** Marcus Ortiz (Principal Architect)

## Finding

The 2028 Atlas Ledger penetration test (a SOX §404 deliverable to Meridian Bank) returned 11 recommendations across Informational, Low, and Medium severity. Seven recommendations remained open at the time of audit, past the policy commitment of 90-day remediation for Medium-severity findings.

No Critical or High findings were identified — the program remains healthy — but the backlog itself is a control deviation given Atlas Ledger's SOX status.

## Affected customers

- Meridian Bank (Atlas Ledger flagship).

## Remediation plan

- Backlog burndown plan: 3 of 7 closed by end of 2028; remaining 4 closed by 2029-Q1.
- Pen-test recommendations now appear in the Compliance dashboard with explicit SLA timers.
- Quarterly pen-test recommendation review added to the Atlas Compliance Council agenda.

## Target closure

2029-Q1.
