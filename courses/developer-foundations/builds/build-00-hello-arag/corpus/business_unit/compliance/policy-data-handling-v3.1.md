# Atlas Data Handling Policy v3.1 (POL-DATA-v3.1)

**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Version:** 3.1
**Effective date:** 2027-09-15
**Review cadence:** Annual

## Scope

This policy governs the handling of all data Atlas Global Industries processes, generates, or stores — both internal data (HR records, engineering source, sales pipeline) and customer data (Atlas product telemetry, customer-supplied configuration, customer support data).

## Applies to

All Atlas employees, contractors, and partners. Every Atlas business unit — HR, Engineering, Sales, Customer Services, Compliance.

## Core principles

1. Classification. All data is classified Public, Internal, Confidential, or Regulated. Regulated data includes HIPAA-relevant data (e.g., Cresta Health Network HVAC alerts), GDPR-personal-data, and SOX-financial-data.
2. Minimum-necessary. Employees may access only the data needed for their role.
3. Encryption at rest and in transit. All Confidential and Regulated data.
4. Audit trail. All access to Regulated data is logged. Logs retained 7 years.

## Customer-data handling

- Customer data is the property of the customer. Atlas processes it under our standard customer-data agreement (controller-processor delineation per GDPR).
- Customer-data exfiltration outside the contractual boundary is grounds for immediate dismissal.
- INC-2027-0142 (Norvale Energy) — production data accessed only by Priya Anand's incident response team. Access logged.
- INC-2028-0019 (Cresta Health Network) — HIPAA-relevant alerts handled per the HIPAA security rule.

## Customer obligations under contract

Atlas's customer-data agreement (signed by every customer including Norvale Energy, Halcyon Logistics, Meridian Bank, Cresta Health Network, Talos Steelworks) commits Atlas to:

- Process data only as instructed.
- Notify of any breach within 72 hours.
- Provide reasonable assistance with customer compliance obligations (GDPR data-subject requests, HIPAA reporting, etc.).
