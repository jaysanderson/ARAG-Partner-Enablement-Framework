# Atlas Export Controls Policy v1.0 (POL-EXPORT-v1.0)

**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Effective:** 2028-04-15
**Review:** Annual

## Purpose

Sets out Atlas's obligations under U.S. export controls (EAR), the EU dual-use regime (2021/821), and applicable APAC controls — and operationalises them for product shipment, software access, and technical-data exchange.

## Restricted-party screening

Every Atlas customer (current: Norvale Energy, Halcyon Logistics, Meridian Bank, Cresta Health Network, Talos Steelworks) is screened at onboarding and re-screened quarterly against the U.S. Consolidated Screening List, the EU Consolidated Sanctions list, and the UK OFSI list.

## Technology transfer

Engineering hand-offs that cross national borders (e.g., Priya Anand's EMEA team sharing turbine firmware source with Marcus Ortiz's NORAM team) follow the standard intra-Atlas transfer log; no further license is required for EAR99 / non-controlled work product.

## Training

All Atlas Engineering staff (Priya Anand's and Marcus Ortiz's organisations), Sales, and Customer Services with cross-border duties complete annual export-controls training. Maya Schwartz tracks completion.

## Violations

Suspected violations are reported to the CCO immediately and to U.S. BIS / EU national authority within statutory timelines.
