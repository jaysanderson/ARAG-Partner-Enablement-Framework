# GDPR Controller-Processor Delineation · Customer Agreement Annex

**Author:** Dr Sara Vance
**Status:** Active across all EMEA customer agreements

## Summary

Atlas's customer-data agreement specifies Atlas as processor and the customer as controller for all personal data Atlas processes on the customer's behalf.

## Applicability

- All EMEA customers — mandatory.
- All NORAM customers handling EMEA-resident data — mandatory.
- Other customers — optional but recommended.

## Specific commitments

- Process customer personal data only as instructed in writing.
- Notify customer of any data breach within 24 hours.
- Atlas notifies DPA within 72 hours.
- Provide reasonable assistance with data-subject rights requests within 14 days.
- Assist with DPIAs where Atlas is the processor.
- Sub-processor notification: Atlas notifies customer at least 30 days before adding any new sub-processor.
