# Proposal — Atlas Compliance Managed Service for Norvale Energy

**Prepared by:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)
**Customer sponsor:** Norvale Energy CCO
**Atlas commercial sponsor:** VP Sales (Atlas Pulse account)
**Date:** 2028-Q4

## Background

Norvale Energy operates Atlas Pulse under EU AI Act Limited Risk obligations and is increasingly subject to NIS2 supply-chain expectations as an essential energy-sector entity. Norvale's internal compliance team is lean and has signalled appetite for a managed-service overlay from Atlas.

## What we propose

A 12-month Compliance Managed Service engagement covering:

1. **Quarterly applicability refresh.** Atlas refreshes the Pulse-specific EU AI Act applicability statement and any NIS2 supply-chain attestations.

2. **In-flight audit-finding management.** Atlas tracks AF-2028-015 (Pulse transparency) and any subsequent findings on Norvale's behalf through to closure.

3. **DPIA support.** Atlas provides DPIA template and review for Norvale's controller-side documentation.

4. **Incident co-response rehearsal.** Annual joint rehearsal of the customer-breach-notification runbook covering a Pulse-relevant scenario.

5. **Regulator engagement support.** Atlas provides documentation packaging if Norvale faces NIS2 or AI Act regulator inquiry related to the Pulse deployment.

## Commercial terms

Reference SOW; details in the deal addendum. Engagement priced as a fixed annual subscription plus optional regulator-support hours at a documented rate. Atlas Pulse subscription renewal is independent of this engagement.

## What this proposal does NOT include

- Norvale-internal compliance program development outside the Atlas Pulse footprint.
- Legal advice (Atlas is not Norvale's counsel; recommendation only).
- Atlas E-220 turbine compliance — that footprint is governed by Norvale's own NIST 800-53 r5 obligations and the Atlas E-220 NIST deployment guide.
