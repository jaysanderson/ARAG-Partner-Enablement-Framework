# Atlas Pricing & Discount Policy v4.0 (POL-PRICING-v4.0)

**Owner:** CFO + Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Version:** 4.0
**Effective:** 2027-12-01

## Scope

This policy governs Atlas list pricing, discount thresholds, contract terms, and approval workflows globally.

## Sections

### §1 List pricing

Set per region per product line. Reviewed annually (Q4) by the Pricing Committee.

### §2 Discount approval matrix

- 0-15%: Account Executive.
- 15-22%: Sales Director.
- 22-28%: VP Sales + CFO sign-off.
- 28%+: VP Sales + CFO + Engineering executive (Priya Anand or Marcus Ortiz depending on product).

### §3 Strategic-customer special handling

Strategic customers (Norvale Energy, Halcyon Logistics, Meridian Bank, Cresta Health Network, Talos Steelworks) may operate under bespoke pricing agreements. AE consults the customer file before quoting.

### §4 Pilot pricing

Pilot deployments may carry up to 30% off list, with mandatory expansion clause requiring customer commitment to full-price expansion conditional on pilot success criteria.

### §5 Service credits

Service credits authorised by Customer Success leadership (Aisha Okonkwo or Liam Hayashi per customer). Drawn from customer-success budget, not the sales discount pool. Reference incidents: INC-2027-0142, INC-2027-0188, INC-2028-0019, INC-2028-0034.

### §6 Public-sector pricing

Government and defence customers route through Dr Sara Vance for compliance applicability before pricing is set. NIST 800-53 r5 applicability is mandatory.

### §7 Annual escalation

3% standard. 1.5% floor for strategic customers. Not negotiable below 1.5%.

## Approval logs

All discount approvals are logged. Audit trail retained 7 years per Atlas Data Handling Policy (POL-DATA-v3.1).
