# Case Study · Cresta Health Network BuildingHub

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Region:** EMEA (UK + Ireland hospitals)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo (Director of Customer Success)
**Years as Atlas customer:** 5

## Background

Cresta Health Network operates 14 acute-care hospitals across the UK and Ireland. Atlas BuildingHub manages HVAC, lighting, and access-control systems across all 14 sites. Cresta is Atlas's flagship HIPAA-relevant deployment (HVAC zones are monitored alongside patient-care zones — anomalies generate HIPAA-tracked alerts).

## What Atlas does for Cresta

- 14 × Atlas BuildingHub site-controllers (one per hospital).
- 384 × zone-extenders across all sites.
- 24x7 monitoring contract.

## Recent operational events

- INC-2028-0019 (Q1 2028) — BuildingHub firmware 5.2.1 regression caused unexpected HVAC zone activations during off-hours. Six Cresta sites generated HIPAA-relevant facility alerts (no patient-data impact). Rolled back to firmware 5.1.8 via runbook RB-BldHub-Firmware-003. Customer facilities teams spent 2-4 hours per site addressing the alert backlog.

## Why Cresta stays with Atlas

1. Aisha Okonkwo's relationship with the Cresta head of facilities engineering — direct line.
2. Atlas BuildingHub satisfies HIPAA §164.308(a)(8) (Periodic Technical Evaluation) requirements out of the box — significant procurement advantage.
3. No competitive product offers the integrated HVAC + access-control + patient-zone monitoring story.

## Expansion roadmap

- Atlas Pulse for hospital backup-generation energy management — under evaluation.
- Atlas FieldOps for hospital maintenance teams — early conversation.

## Compliance posture

- HIPAA — primary regulatory framework. Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer) joins the Cresta annual compliance review.
- Audit finding AF-2028-007 (related to INC-2028-0019) closed Q2 2028.
