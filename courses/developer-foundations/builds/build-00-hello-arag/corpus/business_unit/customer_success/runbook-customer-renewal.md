# Customer Success Runbook · Customer Renewal Cycle

**Owner:** Aisha Okonkwo (Director of Customer Success)
**Region:** Global
**Applies to:** All Atlas customer renewals (annual or multi-year)

## Phase 1 — Pre-renewal health check (T-180 days)

1. CSM pulls full customer-success health pack: NPS trend, incident history, deployment milestones, expansion pipeline, open issues.
2. CSM identifies any open relationship risk and triages.
3. Customer-side executive sponsor sounding: informal conversation to surface any renewal hesitation.

## Phase 2 — Renewal positioning (T-120 days)

1. CSM authors a 1-page renewal-positioning memo for internal review.
2. Atlas commercial team confirms pricing posture for the renewal year.
3. Any contractual changes (e.g., service-credit framework refresh, compliance addenda) are scoped.

## Phase 3 — Customer-facing renewal conversation (T-90 days)

1. CSM initiates the renewal conversation in person or via video.
2. No surprises. Any pricing changes, contractual changes, or expansion conversations are surfaced openly.
3. Customer-side procurement counterpart engaged in parallel (typically through Atlas commercial).

## Phase 4 — Contract close (T-30 to T-0)

1. Final contract circulated for signature.
2. Renewal-cycle artefacts captured (refreshed compliance attestations, refreshed service-credit framework, etc.).
3. Customer-side executive sponsor receives a personal thank-you from the CSM upon signature.

## Multi-year renewals

For multi-year commitments (typically Norvale, Halcyon, Meridian):

- Annual mid-term health-check meetings, scheduled at signing.
- Annual compliance posture refresh.
- Annual service-credit framework review.

## Renewal risk indicators

- NPS drop of ≥10 points in the last 4 quarters.
- Open P0 or P1 incident.
- Customer-side executive sponsor turnover.
- Procurement-side vendor-risk framework refresh (see `escalation-meridian-grid-phase2-procurement.md`).

Any single indicator → CSM authors a risk-mitigation memo within 14 days.

## Customer-side executive sponsor turnover

When a customer-side executive sponsor turns over:

1. CSM schedules a relationship-introduction meeting with the new sponsor within 30 days.
2. Refresh the relationship history briefing for the new sponsor.
3. Confirm the renewal posture in writing once the new sponsor has had 60 days in role.

## Anti-patterns

- Pricing-only renewal conversations. Renewals are relationship inflection-points.
- Late renewal conversations (T-60 or later). Customer-side procurement workflows take time.
- Skipping the personal thank-you. A 2-minute email can be the most-remembered moment of the renewal.
