# Escalation · Halcyon EMEA Cutover Readiness Concerns

**Customer:** Halcyon Logistics
**Product:** Atlas Logix (Phase 3 EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Marcus Ortiz
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Inge Larsson, Halcyon EMEA Operations Director
**Status:** Open as of 2028-06-01; on track to close Q3 2028

## Background

Following the successful Phase 2 (APAC) cutover, Halcyon committed to Phase 3 (EMEA) for 2028-08-15. Inge Larsson — newly appointed EMEA Operations Director — raised concerns that the EMEA deployment was being pre-planned solely from Toronto, without sufficient EMEA-side ops input.

## What Marcus did

Marcus Ortiz acknowledged the concern as legitimate inside 48 hours. He:

1. Restructured the Phase 3 working group to be co-chaired by Halcyon EMEA + Atlas EMEA.
2. Brought Priya Anand (EMEA-based VP Engineering) into the steering committee in addition to himself.
3. Committed Marcus's own personal presence in Amsterdam during cutover week.
4. Offered the customer the option to slip the cutover by 30 days at zero commercial impact.

## Status

Inge Larsson confirmed in writing on 2028-05-29 that the restructured working group is operating effectively. The 2028-08-15 cutover date is retained.

## Open items

- Pre-cutover joint table-top exercise scheduled for 2028-07-18 in Amsterdam.
- 30-day parallel run window agreed (same as Phase 2).
- Daily Inge-to-Marcus standup during stabilisation.

## Why this matters

Inge Larsson's relationship maturity with Atlas is in formation. How this Phase 3 lands will define her Atlas posture for years. Marcus's instinctive willingness to restructure the working group on her say-so has built early trust.
