# Deployment · Cresta BuildingHub Original 14-Site Rollout

Historical deployment record for Cresta Health Network's original 14-hospital Atlas BuildingHub rollout. Authored at the time by Aisha Okonkwo. This document is the contract-of-record evidence for the original Cresta deployment, predating INC-2028-0019.

## Customer profile

- **Customer:** Cresta Health Network
- **Region:** EMEA
- **CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
- **Contract date:** 2024-06-30
- **Rollout commencement:** 2024-09-01
- **Final site go-live:** 2025-08-15

## Compliance posture (at original rollout)

- HIPAA-equivalent UK NHS data-handling controls applied uniformly.
- Joint compliance review with Dr Sara Vance pre-rollout and annually thereafter.
- Patient-care monitoring alert pipeline integration validated per site.

## Customer-side leadership at original rollout

- Eleanor Brightwell — Director of Facilities Engineering.
- Marcus Whitfield — CISO (joined Cresta 2025).

- 2027 — first major firmware upgrade, clean.
