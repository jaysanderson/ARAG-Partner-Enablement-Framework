# Escalation · Talos Steelworks FieldOps Japanese Language Pack

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Product:** Atlas FieldOps
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Yui Nakajima, Director of Plant Operations
**Status:** Closed — language pack shipped 2028-04-29

## Trigger

Following INC-2028-0034 (FieldOps offline-sync corruption), the Talos plant maintenance team's adoption of FieldOps dropped sharply. Root cause was not the incident itself — that had been cleanly remediated via RB-FieldOps-Sync-004 — but the fact that Talos technicians had found the English-only error messages during remediation impossible to action without Liam's translation help on the phone.

## Escalation

Yui Nakajima formally requested complete Japanese localisation of the FieldOps technician application within 90 days, citing the incident response experience as proof that English-only UX was now a safety issue at her sites.

## What Liam Hayashi did

- Validated the request was reasonable and product-line-strategic.
- Brought it to Priya Anand's product roadmap review — got Japanese-language pack moved from Q4 2028 to Q2 2028.
- Personally project-managed Talos as the lead translation-review partner.
- Negotiated USD 0 cost for the language pack (Talos would not be charged for being the partner that pushed the feature).

## Delivery

- 2028-02-12: Escalation logged.
- 2028-03-04: Language pack design review complete (Talos technicians joined).
- 2028-04-15: Language pack beta delivered at 3 Talos sites.
- 2028-04-29: General availability across all Talos APAC sites + 7 other Atlas customers.

## Outcomes

- Talos NPS jumped from 73 to 84 (Q2 2028 measurement).
- Talos confirmed Atlas Pulse Q3 2028 pilot (8 units) and Atlas Grid evaluation onward.
- Yui Nakajima quoted in the Talos internal review: "Liam-san treated our safety concern as Atlas's safety concern."

## Lessons

- Customer-language localisation is a customer-success retention lever, not just a product feature.
- POL-INCIDENT-v2.0 incident-aftermath review should explicitly call out non-English customer experience.
