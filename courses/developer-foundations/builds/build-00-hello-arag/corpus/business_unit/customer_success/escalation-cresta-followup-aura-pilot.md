# Escalation · Cresta Aura Pilot Hesitation Post-INC-2028-0019

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Product:** Atlas Aura (proposed pilot)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Eleanor Brightwell, Cresta Director of Facilities Engineering
**Status:** Closed — pilot approved with extended runbook commitments

## Background

Cresta had been in late-stage conversations about an Atlas Aura pilot at four flagship sites. Following INC-2028-0019 (BuildingHub firmware regression), Eleanor Brightwell asked Aisha Okonkwo to formally pause the Aura conversation pending evidence of changed release-engineering practice.

## What Aisha did

Aisha treated this as a relationship-preservation escalation, not a sales opportunity to salvage. The action plan:

1. Acknowledge the hesitation in writing within 48 hours.
2. Surface Priya Anand's revised release-engineering policy (referenced in POL-INCIDENT-v2.0 v2.0 release notes).
3. Commit to a Cresta-specific Aura pilot runbook with explicit firmware-pin language.
4. Offer to drop the pilot entirely if Eleanor's confidence didn't return.

## Resolution

Following a series of monthly facilities-team check-ins (per the Q1 2028 customer-health action plan), Eleanor Brightwell reopened the Aura conversation in April 2028. The pilot was approved 2028-05-22 with:

- 4 sites (Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Edinburgh — same blast-radius as INC-2028-0019, intentionally chosen).
- A dedicated Cresta-Atlas firmware-gating sub-process documented separately.
- Quarterly executive standup chaired by Aisha Okonkwo with Eleanor Brightwell and Priya Anand.

## Outcomes

- Aura pilot approved.
- Cresta NPS measurement in Q2 2028 rebounded from 52 to 64.
- Eleanor's quote: "Aisha didn't sell — she listened. That's why I'm in this room."
