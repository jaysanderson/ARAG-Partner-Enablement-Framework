# Customer Success Runbook · Executive Sponsor Engagement

**Owner:** Aisha Okonkwo (Director of Customer Success)
**Region:** Global
**Applies to:** Customer-side executive sponsor relationships (VP, C-level, or equivalent)

## Cadence by customer tier

- Tier-1: quarterly in-person executive standup. Atlas-side: CSM + the appropriate Atlas executive (Priya Anand for engineering-led, Dr Sara Vance for compliance-led, etc.).
- Tier-2: semi-annual in-person; quarterly video.
- Tier-3: annual in-person.

## What the standup is for

- Strategic alignment on roadmap.
- Surfacing any executive-level concerns.
- Confirming the relationship posture.
- Tabling any expansion conversations on the customer's timeline, not Atlas's.

## What the standup is NOT for

- Slide presentations.
- Atlas-led pitch on new products.
- Operational status (those belong in the operational standup, not the executive standup).

## Sponsor turnover protocol

When a customer-side executive sponsor changes:

1. CSM coordinates a relationship-introduction meeting within 30 days.
2. Refresh the relationship-history briefing for the new sponsor.
3. New sponsor receives a personal note from the Atlas-side executive partner.
4. Confirm the next quarterly standup is scheduled.

## Recent sponsor turnover events

- Halcyon: Inge Larsson appointed EMEA Operations Director in early 2028. Atlas-side relationship-introduction completed within the Phase 3 cutover-readiness escalation (`escalation-halcyon-emea-cutover-readiness.md`).
- No other Tier-1 sponsor turnover in 2027 or 2028.

## Anti-patterns

- Letting executive standups slip due to operational urgency. The standup is the relationship spine.
- Asking the executive sponsor to attend operational meetings. Different cadence, different conversation.
- Failing to refresh the relationship briefing when sponsors change. A new sponsor inheriting an opaque relationship is a renewal risk.
