# Escalation · Meridian Bank Grid Phase 2 Procurement Hold

**Customer:** Meridian Bank
**Product:** Atlas Grid (Phase 2)
**Primary CSM:** Dr Sara Vance
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Carlos Reyna, Chief Procurement Officer
**Status:** Closed — procurement approved 2028-03-14

## Trigger

Meridian's procurement team paused Atlas Grid Phase 2 in late February 2028 citing a new internal vendor-risk framework. The framework demanded fresh SOC 2 Type II attestation, a refreshed model inventory under EU AI Act criteria (even for NORAM-only deployments), and a documented third-party-AI usage statement.

## Why it landed with Customer Success

Although the trigger was procurement-side, the relationship risk was real: a 6-week procurement hold would slip the Grid Phase 2 hardware order into a different fiscal quarter, materially affecting both sides' planning.

## Escalation handling

Dr Sara Vance held the customer-facing escalation, pulling in:

- Aisha Okonkwo for executive-relationship support
- Marcus Ortiz for product-of-record technical responses
- Legal for the contract addendum work
