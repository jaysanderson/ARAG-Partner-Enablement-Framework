# Deployment · Cresta Aura 4-Site Pilot

Customer-specific deployment record for Cresta Health Network's Atlas Aura pilot at 4 flagship sites. Owned by Aisha Okonkwo.

## Customer profile

- **Customer:** Cresta Health Network
- **Region:** EMEA
- **CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
- **Pilot approval date:** 2028-05-22 (per `escalation-cresta-followup-aura-pilot.md`)
- **Pilot install commencement:** 2028-07-01 (planned)

## Sites in scope

- Birmingham (Cresta's flagship hospital).
- Manchester.
- Leeds.
- Edinburgh.

These four sites were intentionally chosen to match the geographic blast-radius of INC-2028-0019 — Eleanor Brightwell's logic was: "If Aura succeeds here, the rest of the estate will trust it."

## Dedicated firmware-gating sub-process

In recognition of Cresta's INC-2028-0019 experience, a Cresta-specific firmware-gating sub-process is in effect:

- All Aura firmware updates at Cresta sites are explicitly approved by Cresta's facilities engineering team prior to deployment.
- Atlas commits to a 30-day customer-side validation window per firmware release before staged rollout.
- Quarterly executive standup with Eleanor Brightwell and Priya Anand to review the firmware-pinning posture.

## Pilot governance

- Aisha Okonkwo coordinates weekly status with Eleanor Brightwell.
- Priya Anand joins the quarterly executive standup.
- Dr Sara Vance is the standing HIPAA-review participant.

## Customer-side executive sponsors

- Eleanor Brightwell — Director of Facilities Engineering.
- Marcus Whitfield — CISO.

## Pilot decision criteria (documented prior to start)

- Zero unplanned site outages attributable to Aura over the 12-week pilot window.
- Zero patient-care-monitoring alert mis-routings.
- Cresta facilities-team adoption metric > 80% within 90 days of go-live.
