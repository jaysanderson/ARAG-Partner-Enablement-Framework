# Case Study · Meridian Bank Atlas Ledger + Grid

**Customer:** Meridian Bank
**Region:** NORAM
**Primary CSM:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer dual-hatting as CSM)
**Atlas tenure:** 3 years

## Background

Meridian Bank is a Tier-1 NORAM financial-services institution. Atlas's relationship with Meridian is unusually compliance-led — Dr Sara Vance owns the customer-of-record CSM role, reflecting both the regulatory weight of Meridian's Atlas Ledger deployment and the strategic emphasis Meridian places on vendor compliance posture.

- **Atlas Ledger** in production since 2026-04-12 (currently version 2028.5).
- **Atlas Grid** Phase 1 in production since 2028-01-30; Phase 2 PO issued 2028-03-14.
- Integration with Meridian's IBM Z mainframe core banking platform.
- Integration with Meridian's audit-evidence repository.

1. Compliance-first relationship. Dr Sara Vance is the customer-facing executive, not a salesperson. Meridian's audit and risk teams trust the compliance conversation.
2. Atlas Ledger SOX §404 attestation track-record: clean every year since 2026.
3. Joint compliance review cadence: quarterly, with the right people in the room.
