# Case Study · Tier-1 Expansion Conversion 2028

**Customers in scope:** All 5 Tier-1 (Norvale, Halcyon, Meridian, Cresta, Talos)
**Region:** Global
**Programme:** 2028 expansion conversion across the Tier-1 base

## The headline number

In H1 2028, 5 of 5 Tier-1 customers committed to an expansion event despite a quarter that included 4 P0 incidents. Aggregate Tier-1 ARR expansion commitment for 2028-2029: USD 14.2M.

## Expansion-by-customer

| Customer | Expansion event | ARR commitment | Sourced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Norvale Energy | Pulse 4-unit pilot to 12-unit expansion (2029) + Grid pilot | USD 4.8M | Priya Anand |
| Halcyon Logistics | Logix Phase 3 EMEA | USD 3.4M | Marcus Ortiz |
| Meridian Bank | Grid Phase 2 | USD 2.1M | Dr Sara Vance |
| Cresta Health Network | Aura 4-site pilot | USD 0.9M | Aisha Okonkwo |
| Talos Steelworks | Pulse 8-unit pilot (delayed to Q4) | USD 3.0M | Liam Hayashi |

## What drove the conversion

1. **Engineering accountability during the incidents.** Every CSM in the Tier-1 base brought engineering leadership to the customer table.
2. **Service credits issued without negotiation.** Atlas's posture was: if you were impacted, you receive credits. No fight.
3. **Customer-controlled cadence for recovery.** No CSM imposed a "let's hurry this past" approach.
4. **Product evolution rooted in customer commitments.** The MQTT broker HA architecture; the Japanese language pack; the firmware-gating sub-process; the enhanced control-evidence emission.

## Cross-cutting observations

- **CSM model fit matters.** Priya's engineering-led model fit Norvale; Dr Vance's compliance-led model fit Meridian; the operations-led model fit Halcyon, Cresta, and Talos. No customer was forced into the wrong model.
- **Service credits are an investment, not a loss.** Aggregate service credits issued across the 4 incidents: approximately USD 1.8M. Aggregate ARR expansion: USD 14.2M.
- **Trust is built in stress.** All 5 Tier-1 expansion decisions happened in the quarter following the worst incident-density period in Atlas's recent history.

## Customer-success function commitments for H2 2028

- Convert the 5 expansion commitments into signed contracts (4 already in flight as of Q3 2028).
- Embed engineering leadership into the highest-stakes cutovers (Halcyon EMEA Phase 3, Talos Pulse pilot, Norvale Grid pilot).
- Maintain the customer-controlled cadence discipline.
