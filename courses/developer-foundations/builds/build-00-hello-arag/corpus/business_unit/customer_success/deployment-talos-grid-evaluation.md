# Deployment · Talos Steelworks Atlas Grid Evaluation

Customer-specific deployment record for the Atlas Grid evaluation phase at Talos Steelworks. Owned by Liam Hayashi (CSM) and Marcus Ortiz (product). Evaluation in flight as of Q3 2028; decision window Q4 2028.

## Customer profile

- **Customer:** Talos Steelworks
- **Region:** APAC (Japan)
- **CSM:** Liam Hayashi
- **Evaluation start:** 2028-04-08

## Scope

- 1 Atlas Grid unit installed at the Yokohama plant's incoming distribution substation.
- SCADA integration with Talos's plant electrical-engineering platform.
- Distribution-side optimisation for Talos's furnace loads.

## Data-residency commitment

Per `escalation-talos-grid-evaluation-data-residency.md`, the Atlas Grid deployment at Talos operates on an APAC-resident telemetry option (shipped 2028-06-12 in direct response to Talos's escalation). All evaluation telemetry stays in-region.

## Milestone log

- 2028-04-08: Evaluation unit installed at Yokohama.
- 2028-05-04: SCADA integration validated.
- 2028-06-12: APAC-resident telemetry option go-live.
- 2028-06-18: Kenji Sato confirms data-residency posture acceptable.
- Q3 2028: Pilot operations and metric collection.
- Q4 2028: Evaluation decision.
