# Customer Success Runbook · New Customer Onboarding

**Owner:** Aisha Okonkwo (Director of Customer Success)
**Region:** Global
**Applies to:** All Atlas net-new customer contracts

## Pre-signing handoff (T-30 to T-0)

1. Sales lead transfers customer context to the assigned CSM during the final 30 days of the sales cycle.
2. CSM joins the final two customer-side meetings before contract signing.
3. CSM authors the customer-onboarding plan in draft form prior to contract signing.

## Day 0 — Contract signing

1. CSM sends a personal welcome message within 24 hours of contract signing.
2. CSM schedules the kickoff meeting for T+7 to T+14.

## Kickoff meeting (T+7 to T+14)

| Time | Section |
|---|---|
| 0-15 min | Atlas customer-success function introduction (CSM, leadership, escalation paths) |
| 15-30 min | Product deployment plan walk-through |
| 30-45 min | Customer-side executive sponsor and operational counterparts confirmed |
| 45-60 min | Compliance scoping (Dr Sara Vance joins for regulated customers) |

## Onboarding plan structure

The CSM authors a customer-specific onboarding plan covering:

1. Site survey schedule (per product deployment guide).
2. Customer-side training schedule.
3. Pilot decision criteria (where applicable).
4. Quarterly business review cadence.
5. Escalation contacts on both sides.

## Compliance scoping

- For HIPAA-regulated customers: Dr Sara Vance joins kickoff, owns the HIPAA applicability statement.
- For SOX-regulated customers: Dr Sara Vance + Atlas Compliance team join the kickoff.
- For EU AI Act-regulated customers: Dr Sara Vance owns the applicability statement.
- For GDPR-regulated customers: Dr Sara Vance owns the controller-processor delineation refresh.

## Choosing the CSM model

Refer to:

- Engineering-led model (`case-study-engineering-led-csm-model.md`) — for customers where engineering is the relationship vector.
- Compliance-led model (`case-study-meridian-compliance-led-csm.md`) — for compliance-dominant customers.
- Operations-led model — default for most customers.
- Field-ops-led model — for fleet-deployment customers (FieldOps-heavy).

## Anti-patterns

- Generic onboarding plans. Each customer deserves a customer-specific plan.
- Late CSM engagement. The CSM should be visible to the customer before contract signing.
- Skipping compliance scoping. Even non-regulated customers should have a documented compliance-scoping decision on file.
