# Case Study · Cross-Customer Trust Arc 2027-2028

**Customers in scope:** Norvale Energy, Halcyon Logistics, Cresta Health Network, Talos Steelworks, Meridian Bank
**Programme:** Atlas customer-success trust arc across 4 major incidents in 7 months

## The arc

Between Q4 2027 and Q2 2028, Atlas faced 4 separate Tier-1 incidents across 4 different products and 4 different customers:

| Incident | Product | Customer | CSM |
|---|---|---|---|
| INC-2027-0142 | Atlas E-220 | Norvale Energy (and Talos) | Priya Anand |
| INC-2027-0188 | Atlas Logix | Halcyon Logistics | Marcus Ortiz |
| INC-2028-0019 | Atlas BuildingHub | Cresta Health Network | Aisha Okonkwo |
| INC-2028-0034 | Atlas FieldOps | Halcyon (and Talos) | Liam Hayashi |

This concentration of incidents could have caused systemic customer-confidence damage. It didn't. Aggregate NPS across the 5 Tier-1 customers moved from 71 (Q1 2027 measurement) to 68 (Q2 2028 measurement) — a 3-point dip during a period that included 4 P0 incidents.

## What the customer success function did right

1. **Engineering-led escalation handling.** Every incident was met with engineering leadership at the customer table within 48 hours.
2. **No spin.** Each incident was named in writing without defensive framing.
3. **Product-side accountability.** Each incident generated a runbook and a release-engineering posture change.
4. **Service credits without negotiation.** Atlas issued service credits proactively in every case where customer-side impact was measurable.
5. **Customer-led recovery cadence.** Monthly check-ins offered, not imposed.
6. **Cross-CSM coordination.** When INC-2028-0034 hit both Halcyon and Talos, Marcus and Liam coordinated their customer communications.

## What the incident data taught the product organisation

- The need for a unified post-mortem framework (POL-INCIDENT-v2.0 v2.0).
- The need for a single firmware-gating sub-process for healthcare customers.
- The need for an MQTT broker high-availability reference architecture.
- The need for Japanese-language UX in FieldOps before Q4 2028.

## Quote attributed to Aisha Okonkwo at the 2028 Atlas customer success offsite

> "Our job is not to prevent incidents. Our job is to make sure that when incidents happen, customers feel safer — not less safe — at the end."
