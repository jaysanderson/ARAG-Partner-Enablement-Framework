# Deployment · Meridian Bank Atlas Grid Phase 1

Customer-specific deployment record for Meridian Bank's first phase of Atlas Grid deployment. Owned by Dr Sara Vance (CSM-of-record) with Marcus Ortiz as the product owner.

## Customer profile

- **Customer:** Meridian Bank
- **Region:** NORAM
- **CSM:** Dr Sara Vance
- **Phase 1 contract date:** 2027-08-15
- **Phase 1 production date:** 2028-01-30

## Scope

- 2 Atlas Grid units installed at the Meridian primary data centre's incoming distribution substation.
- SCADA integration with the data centre facilities-engineering platform.
- Power-flow optimisation for Meridian's data-centre electrical loads.

## Recent operational events

- 2028-01-30: Phase 1 production go-live (on schedule).
- 2028-02-15: Phase 2 expansion discussions opened.
- 2028-02-21 to 2028-03-14: Procurement hold during Meridian's vendor-risk framework refresh (see `escalation-meridian-grid-phase2-procurement.md`).
- 2028-03-14: Phase 2 PO issued.
