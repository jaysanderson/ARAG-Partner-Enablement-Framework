# Case Study · Meridian's Compliance-Led CSM Model

**Customer:** Meridian Bank
**Region:** NORAM
**Primary CSM:** Dr Sara Vance (also Chief Compliance Officer)
**Programme:** Compliance-led CSM relationship model

## Why Meridian is unusual

Most Atlas Tier-1 customers are CSM-led: a dedicated customer-success manager runs the relationship, with compliance pulled in as needed. Meridian inverts this. Dr Sara Vance — Atlas's Chief Compliance Officer — is the customer-of-record CSM for Meridian. The compliance conversation is the relationship.

## How it works in practice

- Quarterly business reviews are co-led by Dr Sara Vance and Janelle Pham (Meridian Chief Auditor).
- Atlas Ledger release management is gated through a Meridian-specific compliance-review board prior to deployment.
- Janelle Pham receives a written quarterly compliance-posture summary.
- Product changes that touch SOX-relevant code paths are explicitly evidenced.

## What this unlocks

1. **Faster procurement.** Meridian's procurement holds in early 2028 cleared in 21 days because Atlas already had the evidence pack ready.
2. **Smaller audit surface.** Meridian's external auditor accepted Atlas's evidence pack without revision in 2028.
3. **Trust at the C-suite level.** Carlos Reyna (CPO) expressed in writing his confidence in the compliance posture.

## Risks of the model

- It depends on the CCO's bandwidth. Dr Sara Vance has time only because the model is exceptional — it doesn't scale to every customer.
- It can be hard to expand into product conversations: when Atlas wanted to discuss Atlas Grid, the compliance-led relationship had to be deliberately bridged into a product dialogue.

## When to use this model elsewhere

- Financial services, healthcare, and regulated-utility customers where the compliance posture itself is the primary business risk.
- NOT for customers where the relationship is fundamentally operational (e.g., Halcyon, Talos).

## Quote attributed to Janelle Pham (in her internal Meridian audit report)

> "Atlas's customer-success function and compliance function operate as a single team. That is rare and valuable."
