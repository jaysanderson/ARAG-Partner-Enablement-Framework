# Escalation · Norvale Grid Pilot Pre-Requisite Conditionality

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Product:** Atlas Grid (proposed pilot)
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Helga Vandermark, VP Generation Assets
**Status:** Closed — pilot greenlit conditional on Pulse pilot outcome

## Background

Norvale's planned Atlas Grid pilot for Q3 2028 was contractually conditional on the Atlas Pulse 4-unit pilot completing within a defined success envelope. In April 2028, the Pulse pilot encountered a telemetry-gap escalation (see `escalation-norvale-pulse-pilot-stall.md`). Norvale's procurement team paused the Grid Phase 1 PO pending Pulse stability re-confirmation.

## What Priya did

Priya Anand owns this customer end-to-end as an engineering-led CSM relationship. She:

1. Acknowledged the procurement pause within 24 hours, accepting Norvale's risk-management decision without pushback.
2. Submitted a 30-day Pulse stability report to Helga Vandermark on 2028-05-17.
3. Hosted a joint Atlas-Norvale engineering technical-deep-dive in Rotterdam on 2028-05-22.
4. Offered to extend the Grid pilot evaluation window by one quarter at no commercial impact.

## Resolution

The Grid pilot was greenlit by Norvale's investment committee on 2028-06-04 with:

- Q3 2028 install of two Grid units at Norvale's Rotterdam substation.
- Quarterly engineering steering committee continuing with Priya.
- Service-credit protections explicitly carried over from the Pulse pilot service agreement.

## Outcomes

- Grid pilot retained on the originally planned calendar (Q3 2028 install, Q4 2028 evaluation).
- Norvale's investment-committee chair (CFO) sent a personal note to Priya thanking her for "treating our caution as wise, not insulting."
