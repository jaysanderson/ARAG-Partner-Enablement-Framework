# Deployment · Talos Steelworks E-220 Yokohama

Customer-specific deployment record for Talos Steelworks' Atlas E-220 turbine installation at the Yokohama main plant. Owned by Liam Hayashi.

## Customer profile

- **Customer:** Talos Steelworks
- **Region:** APAC (Japan)
- **CSM:** Liam Hayashi
- **Engineering counterpart:** Priya Anand (annual visit + remote)
- **Initial fleet contract:** 2021-07-30
- **Current fleet:** 12 Atlas E-220 turbines at Yokohama

## Site context

The Yokohama plant operates on a tightly-coupled generation profile. The Atlas E-220 fleet provides combined-cycle generation feeding the plant's electric-arc furnaces. Generation downtime translates directly to furnace-side opportunity cost.

## Recent operational events

- INC-2027-0142 (Q4 2027) — turbine cooling regression. One Yokohama unit affected (the others sat outside the regression's environmental envelope). Remediated via RB-E220-Cooling-001 on 2028-01-22.
- 2028-03-30 — annual maintenance complete (all 12 units).

## Service contract

- Full-service maintenance contract.
- Japanese-language maintenance documentation maintained throughout (Liam Hayashi's standing requirement).
- Repair-for-Life cost model.
