# Case Study · Engineering-Led CSM Model (Priya Anand / Norvale)

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Region:** EMEA
**CSM-of-record:** Priya Anand (also VP Engineering)
**Programme:** Engineering-led customer-success relationship model

## Why this model exists

Atlas's customer-success function operates on a multi-modal model. Some customers (e.g., Halcyon, Talos) are operations-led — the CSM is an operations-and-relationship specialist. Some (e.g., Meridian) are compliance-led — the CCO holds the relationship. And some are engineering-led — the VP Engineering is the customer-facing executive.

Norvale Energy is Atlas's flagship engineering-led account.

## How it works in practice

- Priya Anand is the customer-of-record CSM.
- Joint engineering steering committee chaired by Priya quarterly.
- Norvale's plant engineering leadership trusts the technical conversation.
- Sales relationship handed off to Priya at contract signing; no transactional account-management layer in between.

## What this unlocks

1. **Faster decision-making.** When Norvale's investment committee paused Grid Phase 1 in May 2028, Priya's response within 24 hours was credible because she was the customer-of-record technical voice already.
2. **Visible accountability during stress.** Priya turning up at Rotterdam Day 2 of the telemetry-gap escalation shaped Norvale's expansion decision.
3. **Product evolution as a customer-success lever.** The MQTT broker high-availability reference architecture was Priya's commitment to Helga Vandermark — and it became the product standard.

## Risks of the model

- It depends on the VP Engineering's bandwidth.
- It can be hard to expand if the customer relationship grows beyond engineering scope (Norvale's FieldOps conversation — `escalation-norvale-fieldops-rollout-q3.md` — required Priya to bring Liam Hayashi in alongside her).

## When to use this model elsewhere

- Industrial generation customers where the engineering team owns the operational decision.
- Customers with strong in-house engineering culture (typically utilities, heavy industry).
- NOT for customers where compliance or operations is the dominant relationship vector.

## Quote attributed to Aaron Kim (Norvale Senior Plant Operations Manager)

> "Priya could have argued with me. Instead she asked questions, listened, and made the call. That is who I want as my customer success partner."
