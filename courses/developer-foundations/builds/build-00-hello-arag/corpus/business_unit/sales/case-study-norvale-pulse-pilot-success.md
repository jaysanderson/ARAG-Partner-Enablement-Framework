# Case Study · Norvale Pulse Pilot Success

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Industry:** Utilities (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Atlas products:** Atlas Pulse (pilot), Atlas E-220 (production)

## The headline

The Norvale Atlas Pulse pilot (4 units, three EMEA sites, Q3 2027 - Q2 2028) is exceeding contracted performance criteria by 4 percentage points. Converting at month 9 rather than month 12 (proposal-norvale-pulse-expansion.md), with the conversion offer for 12 production Pulse units already in negotiation.

## The pilot's key results

- Availability: 99.6% (contracted 99.0%).
- Storage-cycle efficiency: 4 points above the contracted threshold.
- Zero severity-1 incidents during the pilot window.
- One steady-state service event handled without service-credit issuance.

## What made the pilot win

1. Priya Anand as primary CSM AND engineering executive sponsor — the dual hat compressed escalation cycles.
2. The 30% pilot-pricing discount, extended to 8 units on the strategic-customer override (vs the standard 4-unit cap) — let Norvale spread the pilot across enough sites for meaningful operational validation.
3. The Atlas E-220 fleet's existing 99.94% uptime gave the technical buyer confidence in Atlas reliability before Pulse was deployed.

## Why this matters for the broader Pulse motion

This pilot is the reference for selling Atlas Pulse to existing E-220 customers. The same playbook is in motion at Talos (case-study-talos-pulse-pilot-conversion.md). The next-best-fit candidates in the pipeline are utilities customers in EMEA with multi-site E-220 deployments.

## Compliance angle

EU AI Act applicability assessed by Dr Sara Vance. Pulse smart-grid optimisation features documented in the limited-risk tier. The clean compliance posture removed a potential blocker that competitors expected to slow Atlas down.

## The Grid POC carry-on

The Pulse pilot's success unlocked Norvale's appetite for a Grid POC (proposal-norvale-grid-poc.md). Marcus Ortiz now consults on the Grid architecture side of the relationship; Priya Anand retains the executive relationship.
