# Proposal · Norvale Energy Atlas Pulse Expansion

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Industry:** Utilities (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028, expected close Q3.

## Executive summary

The Atlas Pulse pilot at Norvale (4 units across three EMEA sites) is exceeding the agreed performance criteria. This proposal converts the pilot into a full deployment and adds 12 production Pulse units across five sites.

- Pilot started Q3 2027, originally chartered for 12 months; converting at month 9.
- 4 pilot Pulse units delivered availability and storage-cycle efficiency 4 percentage points above the contracted threshold.
- One service event during the pilot (non-INC, handled within steady-state operations).
