# Sales Runbook · Strategic-Customer Renewal Cycle

**Audience:** AEs and CSMs handling a strategic-customer renewal.
**Scope:** Norvale, Halcyon, Meridian, Cresta, Talos.

This runbook codifies the standardised approach Atlas takes to strategic-customer renewals. Drawn from the FY28 cycle (Halcyon FieldOps, Talos FieldOps) and the in-flight Norvale MSA refresh.

## T-180 days

1. Pull the customer's running bundle tier from pricing-strategic-customers-2028.md.
2. Pull the customer's incident history (cross-reference INC-2027-0142, INC-2027-0188, INC-2028-0019, INC-2028-0034 as applicable).
3. Pull the customer's most recent independent savings/uptime figures (case-study-halcyon-fieldops-fy27-savings.md style).
4. Open a cross-functional renewal-prep call: AE + CSM + engineering executive owner + relevant compliance contact.

## T-120 days

1. Draft the proposal scope. Honour the strategic-customer overrides.
2. Confirm engineering-executive scope sign-off (Priya Anand / Marcus Ortiz / Aisha Okonkwo / Liam Hayashi / Dr Sara Vance per product mix).
3. Confirm any pending service-credit treatment is closed and reflected in the proposal context.

## T-90 days

1. First customer-side touch on the renewal scope. CSM-led.
2. Surface the annual escalator value explicitly per pricing-renewal-uplift-policy.md.
3. If customer raises new-product cross-sell, route into a separate proposal track. Do not bundle into the renewal scope unless engineering and CSM both sign off.

## T-60 days

1. Draft proposal complete.
2. Route through deal desk per the standard or strategic-customer lane (rfc-deal-desk-redesign.md once approved).
3. Engineering-executive endorsement complete in writing.

## T-30 days

1. Final pricing reconciliation.
2. Sales-director / VP-sales approval per authority matrix.
3. Customer-side legal review begins (allow 6-9 weeks for strategic customers' procurement legal cycles).

## T-0

1. Signature.
2. Renewal logged in the deal-review system.
3. CSM begins year-1 of new term operational planning.

## Post-renewal

1. T+30: customer success kick-off call.
2. T+90: first quarterly check-in.
3. T+365: next renewal-prep call.

## Anti-patterns

- Starting the cycle at T-90 instead of T-180.
- Confusing renewal scope with expansion scope.
- Negotiating below the strategic-customer-override floor without VP-Sales engagement.
- Issuing service credits as renewal-side pricing concessions (always CS budget per pricing-service-credit-framework.md).
