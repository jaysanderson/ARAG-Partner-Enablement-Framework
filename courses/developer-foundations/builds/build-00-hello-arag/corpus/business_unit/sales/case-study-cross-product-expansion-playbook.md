# Case Study · Cross-Product Expansion Playbook

**Author:** Atlas Sales Strategy team
**Audience:** All Atlas account executives

## The headline

The five Atlas strategic customers (Norvale, Halcyon, Meridian, Cresta, Talos) have all expanded from a single-product entry into multi-product footprints over the past three years. The patterns rhyme. This document codifies the playbook from those five expansion arcs.

## The pattern

| Customer | Entry product | Current footprint | Time to multi-product |
|---|---|---|---|
| Norvale Energy | E-220 | E-220 + Pulse + Grid (POC) + FieldOps (pilot) | 4 years |
| Halcyon Logistics | FieldOps | FieldOps + Logix + BuildingHub (pilot) + Grid (LATAM pilot) | 3 years |
| Meridian Bank | Ledger | Ledger + Grid + Pulse (proposed) + Aura (pilot) | 3 years |
| Cresta Health Network | BuildingHub | BuildingHub + Aura + FieldOps (pilot) + Pulse (pilot) | 3 years |
| Talos Steelworks | E-220 | E-220 + FieldOps + Pulse + Grid (Y2) + Ledger (discovery) | 5 years |

## What recurs

1. **The entry product is sold on a single value lever.** E-220 on Repair-for-Life; Ledger on SOX no-surcharge; FieldOps on integration depth; BuildingHub on HVAC energy.
2. **The expansion is initiated by an event.** Either a positive operational result (Cresta 11%, Halcyon USD 4.1M) or a successful incident response (INC-2027-0142 at Norvale; INC-2027-0188 at Halcyon; INC-2028-0019 at Cresta).
3. **The CSM has owned the relationship throughout.** Priya Anand / Marcus Ortiz / Dr Sara Vance / Aisha Okonkwo / Liam Hayashi have all held their accounts through every expansion. Discontinuity would have cost expansion velocity.
4. **The expansion product is bridged via integration.** Aura → BuildingHub at Cresta. FieldOps → Logix at Halcyon. Pulse → E-220 at Norvale + Talos. Grid → Pulse at Meridian. Ledger → existing core systems at every banking prospect.

## What AEs should do

- At the entry sale, name the cross-sell vector that the integration will enable.
- At the first QBR, surface candidate expansion conversations even if they sit dormant for 6-12 months.
- When an operational event (good or bad) creates an inflection moment, have the expansion proposal already in draft.
- Resist transferring CSM ownership during expansion arcs.

## Compliance lever

Strategic customers' compliance posture (Dr Sara Vance's quiet management of EU AI Act / SOX / GDPR / HIPAA applicability) materially de-risks expansion deals. Sales should treat compliance hygiene as a sales asset.
