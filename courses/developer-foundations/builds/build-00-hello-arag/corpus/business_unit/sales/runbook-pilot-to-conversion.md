# Sales Runbook · Pilot-to-Conversion Cycle

**Audience:** AEs and CSMs running an Atlas pilot through to production conversion.
**Anchored on:** pricing-pilot-program-framework.md.

This runbook codifies the standardised approach Atlas takes to converting pilots into production deals. Reference conversions: Norvale Pulse (Q2 2028), Talos Pulse (Q1 2028).

## Month 0 — pilot start

1. Confirm pilot pricing applied per pricing-pilot-program-framework.md.
2. Confirm engineering platform owner and CSM are both named in the pilot SOW.
3. Confirm conversion-evaluation-review date (month 5) is locked.

## Month 1-4 — operational ramp

1. CSM holds monthly check-ins with customer-side platform owner.
2. AE stays informed but does not pressure the conversion conversation.
3. Engineering platform owner (Priya Anand / Marcus Ortiz / Aisha Okonkwo / Liam Hayashi) tracks operational metrics against the contracted pilot performance criteria.

## Month 5 — conversion-evaluation review

1. Joint customer + Atlas review of pilot operational metrics.
2. CSM + AE present the conversion offer (production-pricing, scope, timeline).
3. Customer signals one of: convert at pilot scope, convert at expanded scope, extend pilot, end pilot.

## Month 6-9 — conversion proposal cycle

If converting (most common):

1. AE drafts the production proposal at standard-discount levels (NOT pilot-discount). This is a hard rule.
2. Engineering-executive scope sign-off complete in writing.
3. Multi-product bundle discount applied if the conversion lands the customer at a new bundle tier (pricing-multi-product-bundles.md).
4. Strategic-customer-override review (pricing-strategic-customers-2028.md) if customer is on the list.
5. Submit proposal through deal desk.

## Month 9-12 — close

1. Customer-side legal review.
2. Signature.
3. Pilot scope absorbed into production scope; pilot-pricing terms expire.

## Reference patterns

- Norvale Pulse: month 9 conversion. proposal-norvale-pulse-expansion.md.
- Talos Pulse: month 9 conversion. proposal-talos-steelworks.md.
- Cresta Aura (pre-strategic): year-2 conversion, slower because Cresta was the original Aura pilot customer and the pilot pricing structure was bespoke.

## Anti-patterns

- Pressuring conversion before month 5.
- Letting pilot-pricing carry into the production deal.
- Conversion proposals that introduce new products not piloted (these should be separate proposals).
- Skipping the engineering platform owner sign-off step.

## Pilot non-conversion handling

- No clawback per pricing-pilot-program-framework.md.
- CSM documents the customer's stated reasons; sales-strategy review the patterns quarterly.
- Customer relationship continues at standard cadence; pilot non-conversion is not a relationship terminator.
