# Atlas APAC Pricing & Discount Framework 2028

Governs APAC list pricing and approved discount thresholds. Anchored on `POL-PRICING-v4.0`.

## Atlas E-220 turbine

- **List price (APAC, EUR):** 440,000 per unit + 18,500 annual maintenance.
- **Typical discount:** 10-14% for orders of 4-10 units. 14-18% for 10+.
- **Volume threshold:** 20+ units — custom pricing, Priya Anand approval.

## Atlas Pulse

- **List price (APAC, EUR):** 185,000 per unit + 12,000 annual maintenance.
- **Typical discount:** 12-17%.
- **Pilot pricing:** 30% off first 4 units in verified pilot.

## Atlas Grid

- **List price (APAC, EUR):** 175,000 per unit + 9,000 annual maintenance.
- **Typical discount:** 14-20% (APAC competition stiffer; tier widened from NORAM/EMEA baseline).
- **Multi-product bundle (Grid + Pulse):** 4-point bundle discount.

## Atlas BuildingHub

- **List price (APAC, EUR):** 28,000 per site-controller + 4,800 per zone-extender.
- **Typical discount:** 12-18%.

## Atlas Aura

- **List price (APAC, EUR):** 190,000 site license + 22,000 annual support.

## Atlas Logix

- **List price (APAC, EUR):** 195,000 base + EUR 0.04 per shipment routed.
- **Typical discount:** 15-22%.

## Atlas FieldOps

- **List price (APAC, EUR):** 240 per device per month.
- **Typical discount:** 10-15% on annual prepay.

## Atlas Ledger

- **List price (APAC, EUR):** 380,000 base + EUR 28,000 per market.

## APAC-specific notes

- Local-import duties and on-site installation logistics are quoted separately per deployment. Liam Hayashi's APAC field-operations team provides the duty-and-logistics calculation.
- Typhoon-season scheduling (Q3) carries a 6-week buffer in implementation services.
- Repair-for-Life programme service-credit treatment matches the EMEA framework.
