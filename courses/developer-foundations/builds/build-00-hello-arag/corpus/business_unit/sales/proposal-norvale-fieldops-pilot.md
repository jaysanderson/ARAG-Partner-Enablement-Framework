# Proposal · Norvale Energy Atlas FieldOps Pilot

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Industry:** Utilities (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Norvale's plant-maintenance teams currently operate on a mix of paper job-cards and an aging vendor mobile system. This proposal scopes a 90-device, two-site Atlas FieldOps pilot to validate the operational fit for the broader Norvale field workforce.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Pilot cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas FieldOps device licenses (90 devices, 6 months) | 90 | 108,000 |
| Implementation services | — | 64,000 |
| Pulse + E-220 telemetry adapters (pilot) | — | 28,000 |

**Pilot total: EUR 200,000.**
**Pilot discount: 25% (pilot pricing per POL-PRICING-v4.0 §4).**
**Final pilot value: EUR 150,000.**

## Implementation timeline

- **Q3 2028 week 1-3:** Device provisioning, adapter integration.
- **Q3 2028 week 4-26:** Pilot run, weekly metrics review.
- **Q1 2029:** Conversion decision.

## Why now

- INC-2028-0034 (FieldOps offline sync) is fully remediated. Norvale's operations director has indicated comfort with the platform's reliability post-fix.
- Norvale already operates Atlas E-220 turbines and Atlas Pulse units; FieldOps closes the operational loop.

## Engineering coordination

- Liam Hayashi (Head of Field Operations) — pilot platform owner.
- Priya Anand — engineering executive sponsor and primary CSM.
- Marcus Ortiz consulted only if Pulse-telemetry-side adapters require deeper integration.

## Risks

- Vendor A is the incumbent vendor mobile system; switching cost could slow adoption. Mitigation: 90-day on-site enablement included in the implementation services line.
