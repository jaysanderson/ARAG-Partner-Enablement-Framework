# Proposal · Halcyon Atlas BuildingHub Warehouse Pilot

**Customer:** Halcyon Logistics
**Industry:** 3PL (NORAM)
**Primary CSM:** Marcus Ortiz
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Pilot of Atlas BuildingHub across two of Halcyon's NORAM cross-dock warehouses. Goal: validate operational savings from integrated HVAC + lighting optimisation tied to Halcyon's Logix shift schedules.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Pilot cost (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas BuildingHub site-controllers | 2 | 64,000 |
| Atlas BuildingHub zone-extenders | 18 | 108,000 |
| Implementation services | — | 84,000 |
| Logix shift-schedule adapter | — | 36,000 |

**Pilot total: USD 292,000.**
**Pilot discount: 22%.**
**Final pilot value: USD 227,760.**

## Why now

- Halcyon's energy spend is now its third-largest opex line.
- The Cresta BuildingHub reference deployment (11% HVAC energy reduction) gives Halcyon's CFO a credible benchmark.
- BuildingHub is currently running the post-INC-2028-0019 stable firmware — RB-BldHub-Firmware-003 included in the pilot operations plan as a precaution.
