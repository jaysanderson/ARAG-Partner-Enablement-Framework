# Atlas Pilot-Pricing Framework

Drawn from POL-PRICING-v4.0 §4. Defines the standardised pilot-pricing offer Atlas extends to qualified pre-conversion customers across all products.

## Eligibility

A pilot qualifies for pilot-pricing if all the following hold:

1. The customer is not already a production customer for the product in question.
2. The pilot is scoped at no more than 12 months.
3. The customer agrees to a conversion-evaluation review at month 5.
4. Atlas has named both the platform owner (engineering side) and the CSM (commercial side) at proposal stage.

## Standard pilot discounts

| Product | Pilot discount | Max pilot units |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas E-220 | 18% | 2 units |
| Atlas Pulse | 30% | 4 units |
| Atlas Grid | 22% | 2 units |
| Atlas BuildingHub | 22% | 4 site-controllers |
| Atlas Aura | 22% | 1 site license |
| Atlas Logix | 25% | per-shipment rate, capped at 250k shipments/month |
| Atlas FieldOps | 22% | 250 devices |
| Atlas Ledger | 18% (paid discovery only — see Talos Ledger discovery proposal) | n/a |

## Conversion economics

- Pilot-pricing carries an explicit conversion-discount expectation: the conversion proposal lands at standard-discount levels (not pilot-discount levels).
- If the customer does not convert, no clawback is triggered. Pilot pricing is sunk cost for Atlas.

## Approval

Pilot pricing within these tables is at sales-director authority. Pilots above these tier ceilings require VP Sales + CFO sign-off.

## Recent live examples

- Norvale Pulse pilot (4 units, satisfied; expanded under pilot-pricing waiver to 8 per the strategic-customer overrides).
- Cresta FieldOps medical-equipment pilot (120 devices, well under the 250 ceiling; standard 23% pricing applied).
- Halcyon LATAM Grid pilot (2 units, at standard 22% pilot pricing).
- Cresta Aura backup-power profile pilot (1 site equivalent; 24% pricing approved as bespoke).

## Service credits

Pilots that experience a service-affecting incident are eligible for service credits drawn from the customer-success budget, on the same basis as production customers. No separate pilot-credit framework exists.
