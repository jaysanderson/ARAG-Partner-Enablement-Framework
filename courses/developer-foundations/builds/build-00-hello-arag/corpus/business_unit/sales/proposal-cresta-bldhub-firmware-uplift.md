# Proposal · Cresta BuildingHub Firmware Uplift SOW

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Industry:** Healthcare (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Status:** Submitted at Cresta's request following the closure of INC-2028-0019.

## Executive summary

This SOW covers Atlas's funded firmware uplift for Cresta's installed Atlas BuildingHub estate (38 site-controllers across 11 campuses) onto the post-incident stable firmware release. No license cost; only services scope.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| Firmware uplift services (per site-controller) | 38 | 0 (Atlas-funded) |
| On-site engineering days | 14 | 56,000 |
| Out-of-hours scheduling premium | — | 18,000 |
| Customer-side training and runbook handover | — | 22,000 |

**Total Atlas-billed value: EUR 96,000 (services only).**

The licensed-product cost of the firmware uplift is waived under the service-credit framework arising from INC-2028-0019.
