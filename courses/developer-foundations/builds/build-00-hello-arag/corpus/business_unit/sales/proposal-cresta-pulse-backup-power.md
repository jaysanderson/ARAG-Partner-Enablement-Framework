# Proposal · Cresta Atlas Pulse Backup-Power Pilot

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Industry:** Healthcare (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Cresta operates emergency back-up generators across its hospital estate. This proposal pilots Atlas Pulse as a clean back-up power solution at two campuses, sized to provide ~6 hours of critical-load coverage before generator handover is required.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Pilot cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas Pulse units | 6 | 1,110,000 |
| Implementation services | — | 220,000 |
| BuildingHub integration (Pulse-aware load shedding) | — | 65,000 |
| 12-month pilot maintenance | — | 72,000 |

**Total before discount: EUR 1,467,000.**
**Pilot discount: 24%.**
**Final pilot value: EUR 1,114,920.**

## Why now

- Cresta's resilience director is under board pressure to reduce diesel-generator dependency.
- The existing BuildingHub estate (post-INC-2028-0019 firmware stable) provides the integration substrate without additional risk.
