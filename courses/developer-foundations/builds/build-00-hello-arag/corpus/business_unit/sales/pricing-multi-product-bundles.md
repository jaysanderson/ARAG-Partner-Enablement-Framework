# Atlas Multi-Product Bundle Discount Framework

Customers who buy across multiple Atlas product lines qualify for cumulative bundle discounts. Per POL-PRICING-v4.0 §5, bundle discounts stack with per-product discounts up to a 30% combined ceiling.

## Bundle definition

A "bundle" exists when a single contract or coordinated set of SOWs lands two or more Atlas products at the same customer within a 12-month window.

## Bundle discount tiers

| Number of distinct products | Bundle add-on discount |
|---|---|
| 2 products | +3 points |
| 3 products | +5 points |
| 4 products | +7 points |
| 5+ products | +9 points (capped) |

## Worked examples

### Norvale Energy (4 products: E-220, Pulse, Grid POC, FieldOps pilot)

- Per-product discounts vary (15% Pulse, 22% Grid POC pilot pricing, etc.).
- Bundle add-on: +7 points.
- Combined caps applied per SOW so no single deal exceeds 30%.

### Halcyon Logistics (4 products: FieldOps, Logix, BuildingHub pilot, Grid LATAM pilot)

- Per-product discounts at the listed tiers.
- Bundle add-on: +7 points.
- The FieldOps renewal's 19% per-product discount + 7-point bundle would breach the cap; the renewal carries the bundle discount inline so per-product was negotiated down accordingly.

### Cresta Health Network (3 products: BuildingHub, Aura, FieldOps pilot)

- Bundle add-on: +5 points.

### Meridian Bank (4 products: Ledger, Grid, Pulse, Aura pilot)

- Bundle add-on: +7 points.
- The Meridian portfolio is the cleanest example of bundle stacking; Dr Sara Vance maintains the running bundle calculation in the account file.

### Talos Steelworks (4 products: E-220, FieldOps, Pulse, Grid Y2)

- Bundle add-on: +7 points.

## Calculation discipline

- Bundle discount is calculated against the line-item subtotal, not net-net.
- Account executives must reference the customer's running bundle tier in every new SOW.
- The customer-list-Q1-2028 file is the source of truth for current bundle tier per strategic customer.

## Approval

Bundle add-on layered onto an already-approved per-product discount does not require fresh approval as long as the combined level remains within the existing approver's authority per POL-PRICING-v4.0.
