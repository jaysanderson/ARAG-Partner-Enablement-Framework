# Sales Runbook · New-Logo Discovery Cycle

**Audience:** AEs prospecting net-new customers in any of Atlas's verticals.
**Companion to:** the 5 deployment guides (deployment-guide-pulse-grid-bundle.md, deployment-guide-fieldops-large-fleet.md, deployment-guide-buildinghub-healthcare.md, deployment-guide-ledger-banking.md, deployment-guide-e220-utilities.md).

This runbook standardises Atlas's new-logo discovery cycle. Reference patterns drawn from the recent strategic-customer entry sales (Meridian 2025, Cresta 2025, Talos 2023).

## Stage 1 — qualification

1. Confirm prospect fits one of Atlas's vertical reference patterns.
2. Confirm prospect's geography is in Atlas-supported region (NORAM / EMEA / APAC / LATAM with the LATAM Ledger exception).
3. Confirm prospect's compliance posture is consistent with the relevant Atlas product set.

## Stage 2 — discovery workshop

1. CSM-led if relationship pre-exists; AE-led if cold.
2. Identify the prospect's single-strongest commercial differentiator candidate (TCO / compliance / integration depth / operational reliability).
3. Map the prospect's existing vendor footprint to Atlas displacement targets (Vendor A or Vendor B per product).

## Stage 3 — value-anchor identification

1. Identify the customer-verifiable outcome the prospect would want to claim 12-24 months post-deployment.
2. Reference the Halcyon USD 4.1M / Cresta 11% / Norvale 99.94% precedents as proof points.
3. Surface the engineering-executive who would own the prospect's deployment (Priya Anand / Marcus Ortiz / Aisha Okonkwo / Liam Hayashi / Dr Sara Vance).

## Stage 4 — pilot or production proposal decision

If pilot: route through runbook-pilot-to-conversion.md.

If production: route through standard proposal cycle.

## Stage 5 — proposal

1. Draft per the relevant deployment guide.
2. Pricing per regional book.
3. Engineering-executive endorsement before AE-signature.

## Stage 6 — close or no-decision

1. If close: enter the new customer into the customer-list file.
2. If no-decision: document the reasons; quarterly sales-strategy review patterns.
3. Atlas does not pressure no-decision outcomes; relationship continuity matters even with a no.

## Anti-patterns

- Pitching Atlas products in geographies they are not currently sold (LATAM Ledger).
- Promising deployment outcomes outside the documented Cresta / Halcyon / Norvale ranges without engineering-executive sign-off.
- Skipping the deployment-guide reference and inventing a bespoke pitch.

## Strategic-customer relevance

New-logo strategy interacts with the strategic-customer override list (pricing-strategic-customers-2028.md) in only one way: a new logo cannot enter the strategic-customer list until at least one production deal is delivered and the customer is past the 12-month post-deployment cycle.
