# Proposal · Halcyon Logistics FieldOps Renewal 2028

**Customer:** Halcyon Logistics
**Industry:** 3PL (NORAM)
**Primary CSM:** Marcus Ortiz
**Status:** Renewal cycle Q2 2028, contract expires 30 September 2028.

## Executive summary

Halcyon Logistics' Atlas FieldOps deployment supports 2,400 dispatcher-issued devices across 14 NORAM hubs. Three-year renewal proposed with an extension to two additional regional hubs and a tightened SLA tied to the Atlas Logix routing integration.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Year 1 cost (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas FieldOps device licenses (annual prepay) | 2,400 | 7,200,000 |
| Additional FieldOps device licenses (2 new hubs) | 480 | 1,440,000 |
| Atlas Logix routing integration upgrade | — | 240,000 |
| Implementation services (2 new hubs) | — | 165,000 |

**Year 1 total before discount: USD 9,045,000.**
**Negotiated discount: 19%.**
**Year 1 contract value: USD 7,326,450.**

Three-year TCV (with 2% annual escalator): USD 22,431,000.

## Why renew (Halcyon perspective)

- FieldOps device fleet performing at 99.7% sync reliability over the trailing 12 months — back above SLA after INC-2028-0034 closed.
- Atlas Logix routing has saved Halcyon an estimated USD 4.1M in fuel and overtime in 2027.
- Single-vendor consolidation reduces Halcyon's IT operational overhead.

## Operational lessons priced in

INC-2028-0034 (FieldOps offline-sync corruption) is closed via RB-FieldOps-Sync-004, but the renewal carries a tighter sync-reliability SLA (99.5% monthly) at customer request. Liam Hayashi has signed off on this commitment from the FieldOps platform side.

- Halcyon's CFO has been vocal about cost pressure in 3PL. The 19% discount is the maximum Atlas should land at; further concession should trigger a rescoping conversation rather than a deeper discount.
- Vendor A continues to circle the FieldOps device line. Atlas wins on Logix integration depth.
