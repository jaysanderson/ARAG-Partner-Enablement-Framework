# Case Study · Cresta BuildingHub Firmware Recovery

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Industry:** Healthcare (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Atlas products:** Atlas BuildingHub, Atlas Aura

## The headline

INC-2028-0019 (BuildingHub firmware rollback) was the most serious incident in Atlas's Cresta relationship. Twelve weeks after the incident closed, Cresta signed a 7-campus Atlas Aura expansion (EUR 2.0M Year 1) and an additional FieldOps medical-equipment pilot. The firmware uplift SOW (Atlas-funded EUR 96k services) was the inflection point.

## The incident

A firmware regression affected BuildingHub site-controllers across multiple customers including Cresta. The fault surfaced as control-plane drift during HVAC load-shedding operations. For Cresta, the operational impact was an elevated risk to clinical-area temperature stability.

Aisha Okonkwo escalated within hours. The engineering response (RB-BldHub-Firmware-003) provided a tested rollback to the previous firmware release, then a staged uplift to the post-incident stable release.

## The Atlas-funded firmware uplift SOW

Rather than booking the firmware uplift as a license uplift to Cresta, Atlas funded the licensed-product cost via the service-credit framework and billed Cresta only for the services component (EUR 96k for 38 site-controllers across 11 campuses).

This is documented in proposal-cresta-bldhub-firmware-uplift.md.

## Why it landed commercially

The Cresta CIO described the Atlas response in the post-incident executive briefing as "the cleanest vendor incident response we have seen in healthcare". This single quote was the trigger for the Aura expansion conversation, which Aisha Okonkwo had been seeding for nine months without prior traction.

## The commercial follow-on

- proposal-cresta-aura-expansion.md (signed, EUR 2.0M Year 1).
- proposal-cresta-fieldops-medical-pilot.md (signed pilot).
- proposal-cresta-pulse-backup-power.md (pilot, in negotiation).

Cumulative incremental contract value attributable to the post-incident recovery arc: approximately EUR 3.5M in Year 1 alone.

## What this case shows

- A funded recovery SOW signals seriousness in a way that a credit on a finance ledger cannot.
- The CSM's persistence on expansion conversations during the calm periods (Aisha Okonkwo's nine months of pre-seeded Aura messaging) was what made the inflection moment convertible.
