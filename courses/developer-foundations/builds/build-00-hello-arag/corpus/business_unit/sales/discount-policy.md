# Atlas Discount Policy Quick Reference

Drawn from POL-PRICING-v4.0. Day-to-day cheat sheet for AE and sales leaders.

## Approval thresholds

| Discount level | Who approves | Approval system |
|---|---|---|
| 0-15% | Account Executive | Self-serve |
| 15-22% | Sales Director | Sales-approval portal |
| 22-28% | VP Sales + CFO sign-off | Approval portal + finance ticket |
| 28%+ | VP Sales + CFO + Engineering executive (Priya Anand for E-220/Pulse/BuildingHub products; Marcus Ortiz for Logix/Grid) | Manual approval, exec meeting |

## Special cases

- **Strategic customers** (Norvale, Halcyon, Meridian, Cresta, Talos) — different thresholds may apply per customer. Check the customer file.
- **Multi-product bundles** — bundle discount applies on top of per-product discount, max combined 30%.
- **Pilot pricing** — separate threshold; see POL-PRICING-v4.0 §4.
- **Public-sector / government** — separate approval chain via Dr Sara Vance (compliance applicability).

## Service credits

Service credits for incidents (e.g., INC-2027-0142 to Norvale; INC-2027-0188 to Halcyon; INC-2028-0019 to Cresta) come out of the customer-success budget, NOT the sales discount pool.

## Annual price escalation

3% annual price increase clause on multi-year deals. Negotiable down to 1.5% for strategic customers; not negotiable below 1.5%.
