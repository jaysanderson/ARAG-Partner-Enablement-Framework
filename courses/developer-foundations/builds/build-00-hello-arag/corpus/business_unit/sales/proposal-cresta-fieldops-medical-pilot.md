# Proposal · Cresta Atlas FieldOps Medical-Equipment Pilot

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Industry:** Healthcare (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Pilot of Atlas FieldOps for Cresta's bio-medical equipment maintenance technicians. 120 devices across four campuses. Goal: validate the fit of FieldOps for clinical-equipment maintenance workflows alongside the existing BuildingHub/Aura estate.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Pilot cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas FieldOps device licenses (120 devices, 6 months) | 120 | 172,800 |
| Implementation services | — | 92,000 |
| BuildingHub adapter (custom for medical-equipment telemetry) | — | 48,000 |

**Pilot total: EUR 312,800.**
**Pilot discount: 23% (pilot pricing).**
**Final pilot value: EUR 240,856.**

## Why now

- Cresta's bio-medical maintenance director is dissatisfied with the incumbent vendor's reliability.
- INC-2028-0034 (FieldOps sync) is closed and the post-fix release has been running cleanly across other accounts for two quarters.

## Engineering coordination

- Liam Hayashi — pilot platform owner.
- Aisha Okonkwo — primary CSM, Cresta relationship lead.
- Priya Anand — engineering executive sponsor.

## Compliance posture

- EU AI Act — no AI features in scope.
- Patient data — explicitly out of scope. The BuildingHub adapter handles equipment telemetry only.

## Risks

- Vendor B is the incumbent and will react. Mitigation: lock in 90-day on-site enablement.
- Clinical change-control adds friction to device rollout. Pilot timeline carries a 4-week buffer for hospital-side approvals.
