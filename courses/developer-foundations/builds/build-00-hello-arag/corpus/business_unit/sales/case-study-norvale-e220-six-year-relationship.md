# Case Study · Norvale Energy Six-Year E-220 Relationship

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Industry:** Utilities (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Atlas products:** Atlas E-220, Atlas Pulse (pilot)

## The headline

Six years into the Atlas-Norvale relationship, Norvale operates a 12-turbine Atlas E-220 fleet across three EMEA generation sites at a trailing-12-month uptime of 99.94%. Atlas remains the default vendor for generation modernisation at Norvale, with the Pulse storage pilot already converting and a Grid POC submitted.

## How we got here

Norvale's original 2022 purchase was four E-220 turbines at a single Karlsruhe site. The Atlas pitch in that competitive process was Repair-for-Life cost of ownership combined with EMEA proximity. The deal was won at standard discount (no special concessions).

Footprint expansions in 2024 (4 units) and 2026 (4 units) brought the fleet to the current 12 across three sites.

## The crisis: INC-2027-0142

Q4 2027 brought a cooling regression in the E-220 fleet (INC-2027-0142). The regression affected all twelve Norvale turbines plus equipment at Talos Steelworks. Priya Anand, VP Engineering, owned the response. RB-E220-Cooling-001 was authored and deployed; the regression was closed in 38 days versus an industry-average remediation of 90+ days.

Atlas issued EUR 1.2M in service credits to Norvale (from the customer-success budget, not from sales discount), and Priya Anand personally led the Norvale executive briefing.

## The recovery

The handling of INC-2027-0142 became the proof point that anchored every subsequent expansion. The Pulse pilot expansion proposal, the Grid POC, the FieldOps pilot, and the Multi-Year MSA Refresh proposal all draw a direct line back to the post-incident relationship recovery.

## What this case shows other AEs

- A well-handled incident, with engineering executive ownership, becomes a long-term commercial asset.
- Service credits funded from the CS budget (not sales) protect the AE's commercial incentives during a recovery.
- Strategic-customer status (per the pricing-strategic-customers-2028 file) creates the policy space to make appropriate commitments without distorting the price book.
