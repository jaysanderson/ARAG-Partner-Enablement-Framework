# Atlas NORAM Pricing & Discount Framework 2028

This document governs NORAM list pricing and approved-discount thresholds for all Atlas products. Governed by Atlas Pricing & Discount Policy `POL-PRICING-v4.0`.

## Atlas E-220 turbine

- **List price (NORAM):** USD 460,000 per unit + USD 20,000 annual maintenance.
- **Typical discount:** 8-12% for orders of 4-10 units. 14-18% for orders of 10+ units.
- **Volume threshold:** Above 20 units, custom pricing — approval from Priya Anand required.

## Atlas Pulse

- **List price (NORAM):** USD 200,000 per unit + USD 13,000 annual maintenance.
- **Typical discount:** 10-15%.
- **Pilot pricing:** 30% off for first 4 units in a verified pilot deployment (max 12 months).

## Atlas Grid

- **List price (NORAM):** USD 185,000 per unit + USD 9,000 annual maintenance.
- **Typical discount:** 12-18%.
- **Multi-product bundle (Grid + Pulse):** an additional 4-point bundle discount applies, max combined 30%.

## Atlas BuildingHub

- **List price (NORAM):** USD 32,000 per site-controller + USD 5,400 per zone-extender.
- **Typical discount:** 12-18%.
- **HIPAA-deployment surcharge:** None. Differentiator vs Vendor B.

## Atlas Aura

- **List price (NORAM):** USD 210,000 site license + USD 24,000 annual support.
- **Typical discount:** 12-16%.

## Atlas Logix

- **List price (NORAM):** USD 220,000 base + USD 0.045 per shipment routed.
- **Typical discount:** 15-22%.
- **Volume threshold:** Above 1M shipments/month, custom pricing (Marcus Ortiz approval).

## Atlas FieldOps

- **List price (NORAM):** USD 280 per device per month.
- **Typical discount:** 10-15% on annual prepay.

## Atlas Ledger

- **List price (NORAM):** USD 420,000 base + USD 32,000 per market.
- **Typical discount:** 12-18%.
- **SOX-compliant deployment:** No surcharge.

## Service credits

Service credits issued for incidents (INC-2027-0188 to Halcyon; INC-2028-0019 to Cresta; INC-2028-0034 to Talos + Norvale) come from the customer-success budget, not the sales discount pool.

## Compliance

All discount approvals logged in the Atlas approval system per `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`.
