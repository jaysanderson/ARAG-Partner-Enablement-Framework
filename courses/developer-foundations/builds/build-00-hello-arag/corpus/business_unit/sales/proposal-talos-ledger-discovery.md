# Proposal · Talos Steelworks Atlas Ledger Discovery Engagement

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Industry:** Heavy Industry (APAC)
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Talos has begun expressing interest in modernising the customer-finance back office that surrounds its industrial-equipment sales business. Atlas Ledger is the closest fit. This proposal scopes a paid discovery engagement that, if successful, anchors a Ledger production proposal for FY29.

## Scope

| Component | Cost (EUR) |
|---|---|
| Discovery workshop series (3 sessions) | 48,000 |
| Current-state architecture review | 36,000 |
| Atlas Ledger proof-of-fit design | 72,000 |
| Final readout + production-deployment roadmap | 24,000 |

**Total: EUR 180,000.**
**Discount: 12%.**
**Final engagement value: EUR 158,400.**

## Engineering coordination

- Dr Sara Vance — Ledger product owner, leads the proof-of-fit design.
- Marcus Ortiz — Ledger architecture consult.
- Liam Hayashi — primary CSM, Talos relationship anchor.

## Compliance posture

- SOX §404 — not directly applicable (Talos is not a US-listed entity), but Atlas Ledger's audit-resilient controls are a feature point.
- ISO 27001 — Talos's existing alignment carries through.

## Why now

- Pulse + FieldOps relationships at Talos have been delivering reliably; CFO trust in Atlas is high.
- Talos has set a Q1 2029 internal target to choose a finance-platform direction. Discovery in Q3 lands the readout inside that window.

## Risks

- Talos's incumbent finance system has multi-year switching cost. Discovery must cover migration sequencing realistically — over-promising would damage credibility.

- Dr Sara Vance, Liam Hayashi — endorsements complete.
