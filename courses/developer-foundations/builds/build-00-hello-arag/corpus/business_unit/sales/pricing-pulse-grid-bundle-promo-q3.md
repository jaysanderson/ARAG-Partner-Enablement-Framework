# Pulse-Grid Bundle Promotion · Q3 2028

Time-bound EMEA-anchored promotion to drive Pulse-Grid attach across the installed Atlas E-220 customer base. Governed by POL-PRICING-v4.0 with sales-VP-approved exception for the promo period.

## Promotion window

- Quote validity: 1 July 2028 - 30 September 2028.
- Order must be booked (signed SOW) by 30 September 2028.
- Implementation services delivery can extend into Q1 2029.

## What's on offer

- Customers buying Atlas Pulse and Atlas Grid in a coordinated SOW receive an additional 3-point bundle discount on top of the standard Pulse-Grid bundle (effectively +6 vs single-product purchase).
- Existing E-220 customers receive an additional 2-point Pulse-Grid loyalty discount, layered.
- Total maximum combined discount: 30%, in line with the POL-PRICING-v4.0 cap.

## Eligibility

- Customer must have at least 4 production Atlas E-220 units.
- Customer must commit to at least 2 Grid units and 4 Pulse units in the SOW.
- Customer must not have an open severity-1 incident against either Pulse or Grid at SOW signing.

- **Norvale Energy** (Priya Anand) — strongest fit; the Norvale Multi-Year MSA Refresh and Pulse Expansion proposals were sequenced explicitly to capture this promotion.
- **Talos Steelworks** (Liam Hayashi) — Pulse Year-1 already in flight; Grid Y2 SOW aligns with the promo window.
- **Meridian Bank** (Dr Sara Vance) — already has Pulse + Grid; outside promo scope (existing footprint).

## Sales motion

- AE leads with the loyalty layer in any conversation involving an existing E-220 customer.
- Engineering reviewer (Marcus Ortiz for Grid; Priya Anand for Pulse) confirms scope before discount stack is applied.
