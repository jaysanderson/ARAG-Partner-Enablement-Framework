# Proposal · Talos Steelworks Atlas Pulse Expansion

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Industry:** Heavy Industry (APAC)
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Drafting:** Account executive (APAC), reviewed by Priya Anand for engineering scope.

## Executive summary

Talos Steelworks operates one of the largest steel-production facilities in APAC. The proposal expands Atlas's existing Atlas E-220 + Atlas FieldOps footprint to include 8 × Atlas Pulse units across two production sites, with optional Atlas Grid pilot in Year 2.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Year 1 cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas Pulse units | 8 | 1,480,000 |
| Atlas Pulse annual maintenance | 8 | 96,000 |
| Implementation services | — | 220,000 |
| Optional Atlas Grid pilot (Year 2) | 4 | (separate SOW) |

**Total Year 1: EUR 1,796,000** (before discount).
**Negotiated discount: 17%.**
**Final Year 1 contract value: EUR 1,490,680.**

## Implementation timeline

- **Q3 2028:** Atlas Pulse units 1-4 delivered to Site A. Implementation services Phase 1.
- **Q4 2028:** Atlas Pulse units 5-8 delivered to Site B. Implementation services Phase 2.
- **Q1 2029:** Full deployment operational. Acceptance testing complete.

## Engineering coordination

Priya Anand (VP Engineering) leads the implementation services team. Marcus Ortiz (Principal Architect) consults on the Pulse-Grid integration design (relevant for the Year 2 Grid pilot expansion).

## Customer Success

Liam Hayashi remains the primary CSM. Quarterly business reviews; annual customer-success plan.

## Why now

- Existing Atlas E-220 deployment at Talos (12 turbines) is performing at 99.91% uptime over the last 12 months.
- Talos Q4 2027 cooling-regression incident (INC-2027-0142, affected one site) closed cleanly via runbook RB-E220-Cooling-001.
- FieldOps deployment at Talos affected by INC-2028-0034 closed in 5 weeks.

## Compliance posture

- No EU AI Act applicability (no AI features in the Pulse units quoted).
- NIST 800-53 r5 applicability waived (Talos is not a US Federal customer).
- Customer's internal compliance posture: ISO 27001 — Atlas's Repair-for-Life programme aligns favourably.

## Risks

- Talos has a competitive process for Pulse-class equipment; primary competitor is Generic-Energy Distributed Storage. Atlas wins on existing-relationship plus Repair-for-Life value.
