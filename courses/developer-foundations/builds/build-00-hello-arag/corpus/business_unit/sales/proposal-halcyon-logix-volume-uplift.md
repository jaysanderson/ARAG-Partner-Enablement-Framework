# Proposal · Halcyon Logix Volume Uplift

**Customer:** Halcyon Logistics
**Industry:** 3PL (NORAM)
**Primary CSM:** Marcus Ortiz
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Halcyon's Atlas Logix routing volume is forecast to exceed 1.6M shipments/month by Q4 2028, breaching the 1M/month volume-pricing threshold. This proposal restructures the contract onto custom volume pricing aligned to Halcyon's growth.

## Scope

| Component | Year 1 cost (USD) |
|---|---|
| Atlas Logix base license | 240,000 |
| Custom volume rate (1.6M shipments/month) | 0.029 / shipment |
| Annual routing-engine support | 84,000 |

**Estimated Year 1 cost at forecast volume: USD 880,800** (vs USD 1,008,000 at list per-shipment rate — net saving USD 127,200 to Halcyon).

## Discount mechanics

Custom volume pricing — Marcus Ortiz's approval engaged per POL-PRICING-v4.0 §3 (custom pricing above 1M shipments/month). Atlas internal economics remain healthy at this tier; routing-engine marginal cost per shipment is below the proposed rate.

## Engineering coordination

- Marcus Ortiz signed off on the routing-engine scalability profile.
- RB-Logix-MemLeak-002 referenced in the support runbook to ensure operations teams remain aware of the historical memory-leak signature post-INC-2027-0188.

## Why now

- Halcyon's volume forecast is tracking ahead of the renewal cycle.
- Lock-in of custom pricing before Q4 2028 reduces the risk of vendor-evaluation friction at the FieldOps renewal touchpoint.

## Risks

- The custom rate is competitive but not below the floor in POL-PRICING-v4.0. Further reduction would require CFO sign-off.
