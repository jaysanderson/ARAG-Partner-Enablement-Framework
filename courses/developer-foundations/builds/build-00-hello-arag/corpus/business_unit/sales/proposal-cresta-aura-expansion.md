# Proposal · Cresta Health Network Atlas Aura Expansion

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Industry:** Healthcare (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Atlas Aura is currently live across four Cresta Health Network hospital campuses, integrated with the existing Atlas BuildingHub estate. This proposal extends Aura to a further six campuses (Manchester, Leeds, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Cardiff, Belfast) plus a regional NHS-trust ambulatory-care site.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Year 1 cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas Aura site licenses | 7 | 1,470,000 |
| Atlas BuildingHub zone-extenders (additional) | 84 | 403,200 |
| Implementation services | — | 360,000 |
| Year 1 managed-service add-on | — | 168,000 |

**Year 1 total before discount: EUR 2,401,200.**
**Negotiated discount: 16%.**
**Final Year 1 contract value: EUR 2,017,008.**

## Compliance posture

- HIPAA — out of scope (no NORAM patient data crosses the deployment).
- EU AI Act — Aura's optimisation features remain in the limited-risk tier; documentation refresh provided.
- BuildingHub firmware: deployed on the post-INC-2028-0019 stable release. RB-BldHub-Firmware-003 referenced in the implementation runbook.

## Engineering coordination

- Aisha Okonkwo — primary CSM and Aura platform owner.
- Priya Anand — engineering executive sponsor.

## Customer success

Quarterly business reviews continue across all 11 campuses post-deployment. Atlas commits two on-site engineering days per campus per year through Liam Hayashi's field-operations team for joint operational drills.

## Why now

- The four-campus reference deployment is delivering a measurable 11% reduction in HVAC energy spend.
- Cresta's CIO has tabled a board-approved estate-modernisation budget that closes 31 December 2028.
- The closed INC-2028-0019 firmware incident gave Cresta executive confidence that Atlas's incident response (POL-INCIDENT-v2.0) is mature.

## Risks

- Vendor B has been actively reaching the Cresta CFO with a lower-cost Aura-equivalent offer. Atlas wins on integrated BuildingHub story and the existing operational relationship — but the discount cannot stretch beyond 16% without exec involvement.
