# Strategic-Customer Pricing Overrides 2028

Per POL-PRICING-v4.0 §6, the following customers operate under bespoke pricing thresholds that supersede regional defaults. Account executives must reference this document before quoting.

## Norvale Energy (EMEA, Priya Anand CSM)

- Sales executive: EMEA AE.
- E-220 cumulative volume discount: 18% across all units once fleet exceeds 12 (currently 12; satisfied).
- Pulse pilot pricing: 30% extended to 8 units (vs standard 4) in recognition of multi-site pilot scope.
- Annual escalator: 1.5% (per the MSA refresh) for the five-year framework.

## Halcyon Logistics (NORAM, Marcus Ortiz CSM)

- Logix custom volume pricing once monthly shipments exceed 1M (currently active per the volume-uplift proposal).
- FieldOps device tier: locked rate of USD 250/device/month for renewals through 31 December 2031.
- Annual escalator: 2.0% on multi-year FieldOps renewals.

## Meridian Bank (NORAM, Dr Sara Vance CSM)

- Ledger multi-market add-on rate: USD 28,000 per market (locked through FY29).
- SOX-compliant deployment: explicit no-surcharge clause.

## Cresta Health Network (EMEA, Aisha Okonkwo CSM)

- BuildingHub HIPAA-style deployment: no surcharge (irrelevant in EMEA but written in for parity with the global cross-customer SOW template).
- Aura site-license cumulative: 16% discount across all sites once estate exceeds 8 (currently 11 post-expansion; satisfied).

## Talos Steelworks (APAC, Liam Hayashi CSM)

- E-220 cumulative volume discount: 16% across all units once fleet exceeds 16 (currently 12 + 8 = 20 post Site C expansion; will be satisfied).
- Pulse multi-product bundle stacks with FieldOps managed-service tier.

## Approval

All strategic-customer override pricing reviewed annually by VP Sales + CFO. Customer-specific exceptions logged with reference to this document and POL-PRICING-v4.0.

Service-credit treatment is uniform across strategic customers. Examples: INC-2027-0142 (Norvale, EUR 1.2M); INC-2027-0188 (Halcyon credits); INC-2028-0019 (Cresta firmware uplift services); INC-2028-0034 (Talos + Norvale FieldOps credits). All from customer-success budget.
