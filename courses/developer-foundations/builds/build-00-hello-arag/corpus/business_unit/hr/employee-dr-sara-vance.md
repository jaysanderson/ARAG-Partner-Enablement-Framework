# Employee Profile — Dr Sara Vance

**Role:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Tenure:** 4 years at Atlas
**Business unit:** Compliance
**Region:** NORAM (based in New York)

## Responsibilities

Sara owns the Compliance organisation. She is the named owner on:

- **EU AI Act** applicability statements (in partnership with Priya Anand for Engineering products).
- **NIST 800-53 r5** posture for all Atlas products sold to US Federal agencies.
- **GDPR** controller obligations across all customer-facing products including Atlas Ledger.
- **SOX §404** internal controls.
- **HIPAA** for Atlas BuildingHub deployments at hospital-system customers like Cresta Health Network.

Sara reports to the Atlas CEO and chairs the Atlas Compliance Council.

## Engagement with Engineering

Sara has weekly office hours with Priya Anand and Marcus Ortiz to review compliance impacts of design-doc changes. The Atlas Code of Conduct (`POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`) and Data Handling Policy (`POL-DATA-v3.1`) are her direct work products.

## Audit findings

Sara is the executive owner of all open audit findings — currently 8 open findings across the portfolio, with assigned remediation owners and deadlines tracked in the Compliance-Trace workflow.

## Customer-facing

Sara joins customer compliance reviews. She has presented Atlas's EU AI Act posture to Norvale Energy, Meridian Bank, and Cresta Health Network in 2027.
