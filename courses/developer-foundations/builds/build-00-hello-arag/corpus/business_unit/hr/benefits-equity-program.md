# Atlas Equity Program 2028

Atlas grants time-vested equity to all employees as part of compensation. This document specifies grant sizing, vesting, refresh cadence and tax handling. Owned by Maya Schwartz with CFO and Compensation Committee oversight.

## Vehicle

- NORAM, APAC, LATAM: Restricted Stock Units (RSUs).
- EMEA: RSUs in most jurisdictions; phantom equity in jurisdictions where RSU treatment is tax-inefficient (FR, IT).

L7+ packages are bespoke and include performance-share components.

## Vesting

Standard: 4-year vest, 25% at month 12 (cliff), then quarterly equal vests for the remaining 3 years.

## Refresh

- L4+: refresh grants every 18 months in good performance standing.
- L1-L3: refresh annually at compensation cycle, sized to mid-band.
- Performance multiplier on refresh: 0.5x to 1.5x based on prior cycle rating.

## Founders' Grant

A small annual pool for senior ICs with >10 years of tenure who continue to deliver at L5+ impact. Marcus Ortiz received an FY27 Founders' Grant in recognition of his architectural lineage on Atlas Logix and Atlas Grid; this is the canonical Founders' Grant precedent.

## Acquisition retention grants

Per `runbook-acquisition-integration-people.md`: 50% of one year's base in RSUs, vesting 50% at 12 months and 50% at 24 months. Maya Schwartz signs off all such grants.

## Sign-on grants

Used selectively to bridge deferred-comp forfeiture for senior external hires. Notable example: Priya Anand's joining package included a sign-on RSU grant to bridge forfeited equity at her prior employer (`case-study-engineering-leader-placement.md`).

## Equity admin

- Carta as system of record.
- Quarterly auto-trade window for vested holdings (10b5-1 plans available L6+).
- Insider-trading restrictions per `code-of-conduct-supplement-conflicts.md`.

## Tax handling

- Atlas does not gross up for tax on RSUs.
- Mobility tax handling: per LTA tax-equalisation policy in `global-mobility-policy.md`, equity is included in the equalisation calculation.
- Equity disposition guidance is offered through a contracted financial planning benefit (BetterUp Finance) for L4+.

## Equity-pay parity audit

Annual review by Dr Sara Vance under `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1` for refresh-grant distribution by demographic. The FY27 audit found no statistically significant disparities; refresh-grant guidelines were tightened anyway to prevent drift.
