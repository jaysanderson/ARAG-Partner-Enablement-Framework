# Proposal — Atlas Manager Coaching Program

Prepared by Maya Schwartz's office for VP-and-above approval. Decision needed: approve a 12-month pilot of executive-coaching access for Atlas L4+ managers.

## Background

Atlas's Manager Essentials curriculum (`manager-essentials-curriculum.md`) is widely seen as effective onboarding for new managers. But it's a one-shot program. There is no ongoing development structure for L4+ people leaders beyond their direct skip-level relationship.

Maya Schwartz's office surveyed the Atlas Managers Community in FY28: 78% of L4+ managers reported wanting "structured external coaching access" within the next 12 months. Cost (and the perception that coaching is "remediation, not development") was cited as the main barrier.

## Proposal

Launch a 12-month pilot offering structured coaching access for all Atlas L4+ people leaders, with curated coaching partners:

- BetterUp (regional coverage, all four regions).
- The Coaching Hub (EMEA-specific).
- TalentLMS Asia Coaching Network (APAC-specific).

### Eligibility

All Atlas L4+ people leaders by default. Self-enrolment, no manager approval required, no public visibility of who is enrolled. This explicitly addresses the "remediation" perception.

### Format

- 6 one-hour sessions per coachee per year.
- Coach selection by coachee from a vetted pool.
- Coaching journal optional, private to coachee.

### Cost

| Item | Year-1 cost |
|---|---|
| Coaching sessions (avg $250/session, ~600 coachees, 6 sessions each) | $900,000 |
| Coaching platform licenses | $80,000 |
| Internal program management (0.5 FTE) | $115,000 |
| Total | $1,095,000 |

Per L4+ manager: ~$1,830/year. Comparable to a single conference attendance.

## Outcomes expected

- Higher engagement scores for direct reports of coached managers.
- Lower involuntary attrition of L4+ managers (FY28 actual 6%, target 4%).
- Stronger PIP success rates as coached managers run better remediation per `runbook-pip.md`.
- More effective handling of difficult-conversation scenarios (Manager Essentials Module 6 case material).

## Risk

- Cost concerning. Mitigated by structured cap (6 sessions/coachee/year) and the per-manager comparison to a conference.
- "Coaching is fluff" perception. Mitigated by Maya Schwartz's office publishing aggregate engagement data after 12 months.
- Coach quality variance. Mitigated by vetted pool and post-session structured feedback.

## Cross-business-unit endorsement

Priya Anand, Marcus Ortiz, Aisha Okonkwo, Liam Hayashi and Dr Sara Vance have all endorsed the pilot. Aisha Okonkwo's Customer Services org would adopt as the lead pilot population given its scale and the CSM/manager mix she's developing.

## Decision sought

Approval of $1,095,000 budget for FY29 (April 2028 - March 2029) pilot with measurement plan for FY30 decision on continuation/expansion.
