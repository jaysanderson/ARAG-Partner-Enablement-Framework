# LATAM Benefits Schedule 2028

Atlas LATAM benefits framework. Country-by-country statutory plus Atlas supplemental. Owned by Maya Schwartz with LATAM People Lead Camila Restrepo.

## Medical

- Mexico: IMSS statutory + Atlas private medical via AXA Keralty (employee fully paid, dependants 60%).
- Brazil: SUS public health + Atlas private medical via Bradesco Saúde.
- Argentina: obra social + Atlas Swiss Medical top-up.
- Colombia: EPS Sanitas with Atlas top-up coverage.

## Pension

- Mexico: SAR statutory.
- Brazil: INSS statutory + Atlas Previdência Privada 4% match.
- Argentina: SIPA statutory.
- Colombia: AFP statutory.

## Life and disability

- Life: 3-4x base depending on jurisdiction availability.
- Long-term income protection where insurable.

## Time off

- Mexico: 12 days PTO year 1, increasing to 22 by year 5.
- Brazil: 30 days PTO (statutory) + 13th-month salary.
- Argentina: 14 days PTO statutory, increasing with tenure.
- Colombia: 15 days PTO.

## Family

- Parental leave per `parental-leave-policy.md` with LATAM statutory floor.
- Childcare allowance in Mexico, Brazil where tax-efficient.

## Mental health

- EAP via Workplace Options LATAM.

## Learning

- Per `learning-budget-policy.md`: USD 1,200 / 2,400 / 3,800 by level.

## LATAM-specific

- Brazil: vale-refeição (meal vouchers) statutory.
- Mexico: vales de despensa where applicable.
- Argentina: inflation indexation on base salary per `compensation-bands-latam.md`.
- Colombia: prima de servicios + cesantías statutory.

## Cost summary (per FTE per year, USD-equivalent, Atlas-funded)

| Component | Approximate Atlas cost |
|---|---|
| Private medical | $1,500 |
| Pension (above statutory) | $1,400 |
| Life / disability | $360 |
| Family programs | $640 |
| Mental health | $240 |
| Learning | $980 |
| Country-specific | $1,200 |
| Total | ~$6,320 |

Plus very substantial statutory employer charges (Brazil ~70% of base, Argentina ~25%, Mexico ~30%, Colombia ~28%). LATAM total cost of employment is dominated by statutory burden; Atlas-funded supplemental remains modest.
