# Atlas Learning Budget Policy

Every Atlas employee receives an annual learning budget calibrated to region and level. The budget is non-cumulative — use it or lose it — but tax-efficient and weighted toward role-relevant development. Owned by Maya Schwartz.

## Budgets

| Region | L1-L3 | L4-L5 | L6+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| NORAM | USD 2,000 | USD 3,500 | USD 5,000 |
| EMEA | EUR 1,500 | EUR 2,800 | EUR 4,200 |
| APAC | USD 1,500 | USD 2,800 | USD 4,200 |
| LATAM | USD 1,200 | USD 2,400 | USD 3,800 |

## Eligible spend

- Courses, certifications, books, conferences, software for learning, professional memberships.
- Examples of routinely approved spend: AWS / GCP / Azure cert tracks for engineering, Pragmatic Marketing certifications for sales engineers, CFA / CPA tracks for finance and compliance team members, IIBA for analysts.

## Manager-approval thresholds

- <USD 500: self-approved.
- USD 500-2,500: manager-approved.
- USD 2,500+: manager + skip-level approval.

## Role-aligned recommendations

| Business unit | Recommended spend categories |
|---|---|
| Engineering (Priya Anand / Marcus Ortiz) | Cloud certifications, distributed systems courses, EU AI Act technical training, hardware-software co-design conferences |
| Customer Services (Aisha Okonkwo / Liam Hayashi) | CSM Practice Network, ITIL v4, regional Atlas product certifications, customer-domain-specific (HIPAA for Cresta Health Network engagements, etc.) |
| Compliance (Dr Sara Vance) | CIPP/E, CIPP/US, ISO 27001 lead auditor, EU AI Act practitioner |
| Sales | MEDDPICC, Challenger, Force Management; vertical-specific (utilities for Norvale, banking for Meridian) |
| HR | SHRM-SCP, CIPD, CCP (compensation) |

## Conferences

Atlas reimburses conference attendance up to the regional budget. Manager approval required for travel-bearing conferences. For multi-conference attendees, Maya Schwartz's office maintains a registry to avoid over-attendance.

## Internal Atlas Academy

The Atlas Academy is a free internal learning track that does not count against budgets. It includes:
- Atlas Product Foundation (each product family, ~8 hours).
- Atlas Code of Conduct + Data Handling refresh (`POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`, `POL-DATA-v3.1`).
- Incident response readiness based on `POL-INCIDENT-v2.0`.
- Manager Essentials track (for first-time managers).

## Carry-over and exceptions

Budgets do not carry over. Maya Schwartz approves exceptions in cases where a multi-year program (e.g., Executive MBA) is approved at the start.
