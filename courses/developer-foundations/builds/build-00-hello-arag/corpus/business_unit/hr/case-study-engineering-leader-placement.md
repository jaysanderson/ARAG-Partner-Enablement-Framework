# Case Study — VP Engineering Placement (Priya Anand)

This case study, used in the Manager Essentials Module 5 (Hiring Decisions), documents the placement that became one of Atlas's most successful executive hires: Priya Anand, now VP Engineering for the EMEA org owning Atlas E-220 and Atlas Pulse.

## Context

In 2020 Atlas was running EMEA engineering through a NORAM-anchored leadership model. Atlas E-220 was a growing product line and the EMEA customer base — including Norvale Energy — was demanding closer engineering presence. CFO and CEO approved a search for a Dublin-based VP Engineering with deep hardware-software co-design experience.

## Search

- External search firm: Heidrick & Struggles.
- Internal back-up candidate: existing Atlas L6 director.
- Brief written by Maya Schwartz with input from the (now former) CTO.
- Target: VP with 10+ years of leadership in industrial systems, EMEA tenure, public-speaking and customer-facing comfort.

## Candidate

Priya Anand was identified through a Heidrick & Struggles introduction. She joined the panel having previously held senior leadership roles at a major European industrial OEM. Strong technical, strong relationship orientation, strong customer-facing presence.

## Panel and decision

- CEO interview.
- Existing engineering principals (including Marcus Ortiz, NORAM Principal Architect) — Marcus's panel feedback was decisive: "Priya thinks at the right altitude and listens at the right altitude. She will not impose; she will integrate."
- Maya Schwartz culture and values interview.
- Customer-perspective interview (an Atlas customer executive sat in by request, with NDA in place).
- Compensation: L7 EMEA band per `compensation-bands-emea.md`, with a multi-year RSU package, sign-on cash, and a guaranteed FY21 bonus to bridge the candidate's deferred compensation forfeited at her prior employer.

## Onboarding

The Engineering Onboarding Deployment Guide (`onboarding-engineering-priya-org.md`) was substantially co-authored by Priya in her first 60 days. The 90-day plan included:
- Visit each EMEA engineering site.
- Two days shadowing Marcus Ortiz in NORAM to absorb the architectural pattern lineage.
- Customer visits at Norvale Energy and three other named EMEA accounts.

## Outcomes

- Atlas Pulse refresh program (FY27) — delivered under Priya's leadership, multi-quarter complex program with strong customer NPS.
- Q4 turbine cooling regression `INC-2027-0142` — Priya's response shaped the working-time-policy tightening for EMEA on-call (`working-time-policy-emea.md`) and the post-mortem `RB-E220-Cooling-001` became the EMEA Engineering culture artefact for "what good looks like" in incident response.
- 95th-percentile retention in her org over FY26-FY28.
- Three L4 promotions and one L5 promotion in her first 24 months — Priya's promotion-committee discipline now serves as the EMEA reference.

## Lessons

1. The candidate's panel feedback from technical peers (Marcus Ortiz) carried more weight than the search-firm brief — and that was correct.
2. Bridge compensation for deferred-comp forfeiture was critical to closing.
3. The first-90-day site visits paid back many times.
4. Letting the new VP co-author the onboarding deployment guide made it usable, not theoretical.
