# HR Runbook — Mass Hiring Event (50+ hires in 90 days)

When a single business unit needs to hire 50+ people in a 90-day window — typically driven by a customer expansion, product launch or geographic build-out — this runbook governs the response. Owned by Maya Schwartz with regional Talent Acquisition leadership.

## When this runbook fires

Examples that have triggered it historically:
- FY26: Aisha Okonkwo's Customer Success org scaling from 90 to 160 CSMs ahead of the Cresta Health Network expansion.
- FY27: Priya Anand's EMEA Engineering org adding 70 engineers for the Atlas Pulse refresh program.
- FY28: Liam Hayashi's APAC Field Operations adding 60 technicians for the Talos Steelworks multi-site rollout.

## Steps

### Step 1: Approval and intake (T-30 days before hiring starts)

- Business case approved by CFO, CEO and VP People (Maya Schwartz).
- Headcount opened in Workday.
- Job descriptions reviewed by HRBP for band alignment per regional bands docs.
- Source mix decided: agency, direct, employee referral, university channels.

### Step 2: TA squad formation (T-21 days)

- Dedicated recruiter pod (5-7 recruiters) assigned for the event.
- Hiring manager bench mobilised. For Engineering: Priya Anand or Marcus Ortiz attends weekly review; for CSM: Aisha Okonkwo; for Field Ops: Liam Hayashi.
- Interview-loop calibration session — every interviewer briefed on the calibrated bar.

### Step 3: Pipeline open (T+0)

- Channels live: Atlas careers page, LinkedIn, agency network, referral surge program (referral bonus doubled for the 90-day window).
- Daily standup with TA, hiring managers, HRBP.

### Step 4: Quality control (continuous)

- Sampled offer audits weekly by Maya Schwartz's office.
- Diversity-of-slate monitoring per requisition.
- Adverse-impact analysis on rejection patterns.

### Step 5: Onboarding capacity (T+30 days)

- Onboarding deployment guides pre-staged. For mass Engineering hiring, `onboarding-engineering-priya-org.md` or `onboarding-engineering-marcus-org.md`.
- Buddy bench expanded.
- Atlas Academy capacity scaled (live modules duplicated where needed).
- Equipment supply-chain pre-positioned with operations.

### Step 6: Wrap and retrospective (T+90 days)

- Hiring KPIs reviewed: time-to-fill, offer-accept rate, 90-day retention, ramp-time-to-productivity.
- Retrospective with hiring managers; learnings folded into the runbook.

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Quality drift under volume pressure | Sampled offer audits, calibration sessions |
| Buddy network burnout | Buddy bench rotation; recognition for active buddies |
| Onboarding throughput failure | Onboarding capacity scaled, Atlas Academy duplicated |
| Compensation off-band | HRBP review of every offer above L4 |

## Coordination with Compliance

If the hiring event touches a regulated product (Atlas BuildingHub for Cresta Health Network → HIPAA awareness, Atlas Ledger for Meridian Bank → SOX context), Dr Sara Vance's office reviews the cohort training plan.

## Anti-patterns

- Lowering the bar under volume pressure.
- Skipping calibration on time-pressured loops.
- Onboarding capacity assumption based on normal-state throughput.
- Forgetting the equipment supply chain.
