# Onboarding Deployment Guide — Sales

Operational guide for HRBPs onboarding into the Atlas Sales org. The org covers all five product families across all four regions, organised by industry vertical and named-account pods.

## Pre-boarding (T-14 to T-0)

- HRBP confirms patch (named-account or geo-led).
- Pre-read: Atlas product datasheets, Atlas Pricing & Discount Policy `POL-PRICING-v4.0`, Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`.
- CRM (Salesforce) license and territory provisioning.
- Customer-specific pre-clearance: financial-services for Meridian Bank pods, HIPAA awareness for Cresta Health Network pods.

## Day 1

- Welcome by regional VP Sales. Sales kickoff buddy assigned.
- `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`, `POL-DATA-v3.1`, `POL-PRICING-v4.0` modules.
- Salesforce walkthrough, MEDDPICC discipline overview.

## Week 1

- Atlas product certifications (level 1) for assigned product families.
- Shadow three customer calls and one internal pricing-desk session.
- Read battlecards for the assigned vertical (utilities, banking, healthcare, logistics, heavy industry).

## Days 30 / 60 / 90

- Day 30: first qualified opportunity in CRM with MEDDPICC fields populated.
- Day 60: independent customer meeting (no shadow). Read the relevant Customer Success engagement notes for active accounts.
- Day 90: first deal advancement past qualification. Quarterly Sales calibration includes the new hire.

## Pricing discipline

Sales has a tight relationship with the Atlas Pricing Desk. Discount approvals beyond standard thresholds in `POL-PRICING-v4.0` route to regional VP Sales, then to CFO for sub-floor discounts. New AEs are calibrated by the Pricing Desk before being allowed to offer discounts.

## Cross-region context

Sales onboarding follows the regional pattern but every AE attends the global Atlas Sales Kickoff once per fiscal year. Maya Schwartz attends global kickoff to brief the Sales org on the People-team priorities.

## Common pitfalls

- Discounting below floor without pre-clearance.
- Pitching outside product certification level.
- Missing the customer-specific clearance (Meridian, Cresta).
- Forgetting Compliance is a stakeholder for Meridian Bank conversations.
