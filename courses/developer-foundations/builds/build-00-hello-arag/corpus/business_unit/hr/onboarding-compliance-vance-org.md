# Onboarding Deployment Guide — Compliance (Dr Sara Vance org)

Operational guide for HRBPs onboarding into Dr Sara Vance's Compliance org. The org owns regulatory positions across NIST 800-53 r5, EU AI Act, GDPR, HIPAA and SOX §404, with cross-business-unit governance authority.

## Pre-boarding (T-14 to T-0)

- HRBP confirms scope (regional regulator focus or cross-cutting policy).
- Pre-read: every Atlas policy in the umbrella set — `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`, `POL-DATA-v3.1`, `POL-INCIDENT-v2.0`, `POL-PRICING-v4.0`.
- Background check at level appropriate to regulator scope.
- Vendor-managed pre-clearance for Meridian Bank and Cresta Health Network engagements if relevant.

## Day 1

- Welcome by Dr Sara Vance personally — she attends every Compliance Day 1.
- Atlas policy umbrella walkthrough (90 minutes).
- Regulator-mapping briefing: which Atlas products fall under which regulation per `anchors.json`. Refresher on Atlas E-220 / Atlas FieldOps → NIST; Atlas BuildingHub / Atlas Logix → EU AI Act; Atlas Ledger / Atlas FieldOps → GDPR; Atlas Ledger → SOX §404; Atlas BuildingHub → HIPAA.

## Week 1

- Atlas product compliance certifications (level 1) for the products in scope.
- Read post-mortems for any incidents touching the regulator scope. EU-AI-Act-track readers read `RB-BldHub-Firmware-003` (`INC-2028-0019`) and the Logix post-mortem `RB-Logix-MemLeak-002` (`INC-2027-0188`).
- Intro with Maya Schwartz (the HR–Compliance interface is dense).

## Days 30 / 60 / 90

- Day 30: own one in-flight audit work-stream.
- Day 60: present in a compliance committee. Dr Sara Vance attends and offers feedback.
- Day 90: lead one regulator-mapping update or policy revision. Compliance promotion-committee track begins from this point.

## Cross-business-unit interactions

Compliance is a connector function. New hires should expect regular working sessions with:
- Priya Anand and Marcus Ortiz on Engineering posture against NIST and EU AI Act.
- Aisha Okonkwo on customer-facing audit responses (Cresta Health Network in particular).
- Liam Hayashi on FieldOps GDPR posture.
- Maya Schwartz on HR data handling supplement and code-of-conduct interpretation.

## Customer audits

Atlas hosts an average of 14 customer-driven audits per year. New Compliance hires shadow two audits before leading one. Norvale Energy, Meridian Bank and Cresta Health Network are the heaviest auditors.

## Common pitfalls

- Treating policy as static — the Atlas policy umbrella is updated 2-3 times per year.
- Underestimating the volume of cross-business-unit coordination.
- Not building the relationship with Maya Schwartz's HR-policy team early.
