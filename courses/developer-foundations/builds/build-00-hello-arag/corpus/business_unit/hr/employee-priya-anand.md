# Employee Profile — Priya Anand

**Role:** VP Engineering
**Tenure:** 8 years at Atlas
**Business unit:** Engineering
**Region:** EMEA (based in Dublin)

## Responsibilities

Priya owns the engineering organisation for **Atlas E-220** (industrial turbine) and **Atlas Pulse** (energy systems). She is also the escalation point for cooling-system incidents on the E-220 line — most recently **INC-2027-0142** (Q4 turbine cooling regression), which was remediated via Runbook **RB-E220-Cooling-001**.

## Customer relationships

Priya is the named technical contact for two of Atlas's five strategic customers:

- **Norvale Energy** — primary E-220 customer; Priya leads the joint engineering steering committee.
- **Talos Steelworks** — Pulse deployment partnership.

## Compliance

Priya signs off on EU AI Act applicability statements for products her group owns. She holds the dotted-line working relationship with **Dr Sara Vance** (Chief Compliance Officer).

## On-call

Priya is on the L3 on-call rotation for any P0 incident affecting E-220 or Pulse. Atlas Incident Response Policy `POL-INCIDENT-v2.0` defines the escalation path.
