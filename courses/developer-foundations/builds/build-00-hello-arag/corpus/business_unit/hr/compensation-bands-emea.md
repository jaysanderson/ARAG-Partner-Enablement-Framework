# EMEA Compensation Bands 2028

This document covers compensation bands for full-time Atlas employees in the EMEA region. Bands are reviewed annually by VP People Maya Schwartz with Compensation Committee oversight.

## Bands

| Level | Role examples | EMEA base band (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate Engineer, Coordinator | 42,000 – 56,000 |
| L2 | Engineer, Specialist | 56,000 – 78,000 |
| L3 | Senior Engineer, Senior Specialist | 78,000 – 105,000 |
| L4 | Staff Engineer, Lead | 105,000 – 138,000 |
| L5 | Principal Engineer (e.g., Marcus Ortiz tier — note: Marcus is in NORAM) | 138,000 – 175,000 |
| L6 | Director, Senior Principal | 175,000 – 222,000 |
| L7 | VP (e.g., Priya Anand) | 222,000 – 295,000 |
| L8 | Senior VP / C-suite | Bespoke |

## Variable compensation

- L1-L3: 10% target annual bonus.
- L4-L5: 15% target.
- L6-L7: 20-30% target + equity refresh.

## Equity

All Atlas roles include time-vested equity. Refresh grants every 18 months for L4+ levels in good performance standing.

## Benefits

- Standard EMEA benefits package: 25 days PTO + national holidays, statutory pension contributions, private medical, learning budget €1,500/year.
- Sabbatical eligibility after 5 years of service.

## Equity-pay parity

Atlas conducts an annual pay-equity audit reviewed by **Dr Sara Vance** (Chief Compliance Officer) under Atlas Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`. Any band breach is investigated and remediated within 90 days.

## Compensation contact

For questions about your specific compensation, contact your manager first. Cross-region transfers or band exceptions go through Maya Schwartz.
