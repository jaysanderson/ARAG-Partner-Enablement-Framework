# Case Study — Norvale Energy Engineering-Led CSM Bridge

Norvale Energy is unusual in the Atlas customer portfolio: the primary CSM relationship is held by Priya Anand (VP Engineering), not by a Customer Success career CSM. This case study documents the people-side decisions that supported this anomaly and the eventual transition design.

## Why the engineering-led model exists

Norvale's engagement is anchored in Atlas E-220 and Atlas Pulse — heavy industrial equipment with deep engineering co-design. When the relationship was originated in 2022, Norvale's CTO insisted on a peer-level engineering relationship rather than a CSM relationship. Priya Anand was the natural lead.

The arrangement has worked: NPS 71 across FY26-FY28, $43m annual revenue, multi-year extension signed in FY28.

## People-side challenges

### Workload on Priya Anand

The Norvale relationship adds an estimated 12% of Priya's calendar time. Without explicit accounting, this would have eroded her engineering leadership capacity. Maya Schwartz's office formalised the workload allocation in FY27 and built it into her objectives.

### Career path for a real CSM

Aisha Okonkwo's Customer Services org has not been able to develop a Norvale-experienced CSM, because Norvale doesn't have one. This is a Senior CSM career-blocking pattern. The transition design addresses it.

### Customer-side succession risk

Norvale's CTO is mid-career and likely to continue for another 5+ years. But if she moves on, the new CTO may not require peer engineering engagement — and Atlas needs CSM continuity.

## Transition design (FY28-FY30)

A staged transition is in flight:

### Year 1 (FY28): shadowing

A senior CSM from Aisha Okonkwo's EMEA team is assigned in shadow capacity. Joins all Norvale calls. Builds relationship with Norvale's facilities, operations, finance counterparts where Priya's engineering altitude doesn't reach.

### Year 2 (FY29): co-lead

The CSM takes operational ownership of commercial conversations, renewal planning, and steering-committee chair rotation. Priya remains the technical-relationship anchor.

### Year 3 (FY30): CSM-led, engineering-anchored

The CSM becomes primary CSM of record. Priya retains executive sponsorship and direct CTO-to-VP relationship. Standard Atlas model resumes.

## People-side learnings

1. Engineering-led customer engagement is sometimes the right answer for the customer but always creates People-side debt. Make the debt explicit and pay it down.
2. The CSM career path is real and must be protected even for "engineering-led" customers — the transition design ensures a Norvale-experienced CSM emerges by FY30.
3. Customer-side succession risk is a People-side concern that the CSM transition mitigates.
4. Maya Schwartz's quarterly check-in with Priya on the Norvale workload prevents the engineering leadership capacity erosion.
