# Atlas Remote & Hybrid Work Policy v3.0

Owned by Maya Schwartz. Reviewed by HR Operations annually and by Dr Sara Vance for compliance with the Atlas Data Handling Policy `POL-DATA-v3.1`.

## Working modes

Atlas supports three working modes. Eligibility is set per role at the requisition stage.

| Mode | Definition | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| In-office | 4-5 days per week on-site | Field Operations dispatchers, lab-bound engineering on Atlas E-220 hardware |
| Hybrid | 2-3 days per week on-site | Most engineering (Marcus Ortiz's NORAM teams), Sales (regional offices), HRBPs |
| Fully remote | <1 day per month on-site | Software engineering on Atlas Logix / Atlas Grid, Customer Success generalists |

## Data-handling implications

Remote workers handling customer or restricted data must:

- Work from an approved location declared in Workday (no co-working spaces, no unsecured residences).
- Use the Atlas-managed laptop only — BYOD is prohibited for restricted data per `POL-DATA-v3.1`.
- Connect through Atlas SSO + ZScaler. Direct VPN-to-customer is prohibited unless the customer (e.g., Norvale Energy, Meridian Bank) has a signed MNDA permitting it.

## Cross-border remote work

Working from a country other than your country of employment requires Workday declaration and is capped at 30 days per year. This is a tax, immigration and `POL-DATA-v3.1` concern, not preference. EMEA-employed staff considering Schengen-area remote work need no extra approval up to the cap.

## Office expectations on hybrid days

- Hybrid employees coordinate team anchor days with their manager. Engineering anchor days are Tue/Thu by global default.
- Travel between offices is reimbursed per the Atlas Travel Policy if required by the role.

## Equipment

- Atlas provides one laptop, one external monitor (purchase up to USD 450), keyboard/mouse and headset.
- Remote-allowance one-time stipend of USD 750 for new hires for desk/chair setup.

## Manager guidance

Managers run a monthly remote-check pulse on engagement. Aisha Okonkwo and Liam Hayashi co-authored the Customer Services version of this pulse for FieldOps and BuildingHub remote CSMs.

## Exceptions

Exceptions route through HRBP + Maya Schwartz. Common approved exceptions: medical accommodations, eldercare relocations, dual-career relocations.
