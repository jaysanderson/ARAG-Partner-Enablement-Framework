# Atlas Performance Review Framework 2028

Atlas runs a calendar-year performance cycle with two formal touchpoints: mid-year (June) and year-end (December). Maya Schwartz owns the framework with input from business-unit People Partners.

## Principles

1. Performance is evaluated against role expectations, not against peers (no stack-ranking).
2. Reviews are evidence-based — manager observations, peer feedback, customer feedback (for customer-facing roles), and self-assessment.
3. Outcomes inform compensation, promotion and development — not termination decisions, which follow a separate documented PIP process.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Definition | Distribution guideline |
|---|---|---|
| Significantly Exceeds | Consistent exceptional impact beyond role | ~10% |
| Exceeds | Performs above role expectations | ~25% |
| Strong | Performs role fully and reliably | ~55% |
| Inconsistent | Some gaps; growth plan required | ~9% |
| Below | Sustained underperformance; PIP triggered | ~1% |

Guidelines are not quotas. Maya Schwartz reviews any business unit whose distribution deviates materially from guideline.

## Inputs

- Self-assessment (written, 60 minutes recommended).
- Manager assessment.
- 3-5 peer feedback responses (anonymous, structured).
- Skip-level review for L4+.
- Customer feedback for customer-facing roles (Sales, Customer Services). Aisha Okonkwo's team gathers Cresta Health Network and Norvale Energy CSAT/voice-of-customer data into the reviews for CSMs on those accounts.

## Promotion process

Promotions are decided by promotion committees, not by direct managers alone. Each business unit runs its own committee.

- Engineering committee chaired by Priya Anand or Marcus Ortiz (alternating).
- Customer Services committee chaired by Aisha Okonkwo with Liam Hayashi as APAC representative.
- Compliance committee chaired by Dr Sara Vance.
- Sales and HR committees chaired by their respective VPs.

L7 promotions require CEO and board involvement.

## Calibration

Calibration happens at the business-unit level before ratings are shared with employees. Maya Schwartz attends Engineering, Customer Services and Compliance calibrations as observer to ensure fairness across business units.

## Compensation cycle

Merit increases and equity refresh decisions are made in February following December reviews, paid April 1.

## PIP process

The Performance Improvement Plan is a separate framework (`process-pip.md`) with 60-day or 90-day plans, clear exit criteria and HRBP support. PIP is never a stealth-firing mechanism — Maya Schwartz audits PIP success rates quarterly.
