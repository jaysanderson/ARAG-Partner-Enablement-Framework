# Atlas Anti-Discrimination & Harassment Policy v2.4

Owned by Maya Schwartz, reviewed annually by Dr Sara Vance as part of the Atlas Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1` umbrella. Applies to every Atlas employee, contractor, vendor and visitor across HR, Engineering, Sales, Customer Services and Compliance.

## Protected classes

Atlas prohibits discrimination on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, veteran status, marital status, pregnancy, caregiver status, genetic information, or political affiliation. Regional supplements add protected classes where local law is broader (e.g., trade-union membership in EMEA).

## What constitutes harassment

Any unwelcome conduct — verbal, written, visual, physical or digital — that creates an intimidating, hostile or offensive environment. Conduct does not need to be intentional to qualify.

## Reporting channels

Atlas offers four independent channels so no employee is forced to report through a manager that may be implicated.

1. Direct to manager or skip-level.
2. HRBP for the employee's business unit.
3. Maya Schwartz (VP People) for any complaint involving leadership L6+.
4. Atlas Ethics Line — anonymous third-party hotline operated by NavexEthics, 24/7, 18 languages.

## Investigation standard

Every report is acknowledged within 48 hours and assigned to a trained investigator within 5 business days. Investigations follow a documented protocol with interview transcripts, evidence preservation, and a written findings memo. The Chief Compliance Officer reviews findings memos quarterly in aggregate; individual findings stay confidential.

## Non-retaliation

Retaliation against a reporter or witness is itself a terminable offense. Maya Schwartz has standing authority to suspend any employee found to have retaliated, pending full investigation.

## Training

All employees complete a 45-minute anti-discrimination module annually, delivered through the Atlas Learning Platform. Managers complete an additional 90-minute bystander-intervention module. New hires complete both within their first 30 days as part of the onboarding handbook.

## Discipline matrix

| Severity | Example | Typical outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Single off-colour comment, first occurrence | Documented coaching, mandatory retraining |
| Moderate | Repeated comments, exclusionary patterns | Formal written warning, PIP, retraining |
| Severe | Discriminatory hiring/firing decisions, hostile-environment patterns | Termination, reportable to authorities where applicable |
| Egregious | Physical or sexual assault, threats | Immediate termination, law-enforcement referral |

## Cross-reference

This policy interacts with the Atlas Data Handling Policy `POL-DATA-v3.1` when complaints involve sensitive personal data. Investigators are bonded under POL-DATA's restricted-data clause.
