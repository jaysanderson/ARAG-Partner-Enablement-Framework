# NORAM Compensation Bands 2028

This document covers compensation bands for full-time Atlas employees in the NORAM region. Bands are reviewed annually by VP People Maya Schwartz with Compensation Committee oversight and benchmarked against Radford TCH-AMER, Mercer US-Tech and three internally selected peer manufacturers.

## Bands

| Level | Role examples | NORAM base band (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate Engineer, Coordinator, Field Tech I | 62,000 – 84,000 |
| L2 | Engineer, Specialist, Field Tech II | 84,000 – 118,000 |
| L3 | Senior Engineer, Senior Specialist | 118,000 – 158,000 |
| L4 | Staff Engineer, Lead, Senior CSM | 158,000 – 205,000 |
| L5 | Principal Engineer (e.g., Marcus Ortiz tier) | 205,000 – 268,000 |
| L6 | Director, Senior Principal (e.g., Aisha Okonkwo at directorship level) | 268,000 – 332,000 |
| L7 | VP (e.g., Maya Schwartz tier) | 332,000 – 425,000 |
| L8 | SVP / C-suite (e.g., Dr Sara Vance, Chief Compliance Officer) | Bespoke |

## Variable compensation

- L1-L3: 12% target annual bonus.
- L4-L5: 18% target.
- L6-L7: 25-35% target + equity refresh.
- L8: 40-60% target, weighted 60% corporate / 40% individual.

## Equity

All NORAM Atlas roles include RSU grants on the standard four-year vest with one-year cliff. Refresh grants every 18 months for L4+ in good performance standing. Founders' Grant pool reserved for principal engineers with >10 years of tenure — Marcus Ortiz received a Founders' Grant in FY27 in recognition of his contribution to the Atlas Logix architecture lineage.

## Benefits

- 20 days PTO + 11 federal holidays, Atlas-paid medical/dental/vision (Cigna OAP + delta dental), 401k 6% match.
- Learning budget USD 2,000/year.
- Sabbatical eligibility after 7 years of service.

## Equity-pay parity

NORAM is the lead region for Atlas's annual pay-equity audit reviewed by Dr Sara Vance under the Atlas Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`. Any band breach is investigated and remediated within 90 days; the FY27 audit flagged 14 micro-breaches across Engineering and Customer Services, all remediated within 41 days.

## Compensation contact

Cross-region transfers or band exceptions route through Maya Schwartz. Mid-cycle adjustments require manager + skip-level + HRBP signoff.
