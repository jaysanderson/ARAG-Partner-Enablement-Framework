# Atlas Separation Policy v2.2

This policy governs all employment separations — voluntary, involuntary, and reorganisation-driven — across the Atlas business units. Owned by Maya Schwartz, with HRBPs operating the day-to-day execution and Dr Sara Vance providing compliance oversight where regulated data is involved.

## Voluntary separations

Atlas welcomes notice. The standard expectation is two weeks; senior contributors (L5+) are asked for 30 days; people leaders (L6+) for 60 days, with knowledge-transfer plans signed off by the next-skip and the business-unit VP (e.g., Priya Anand for departing Engineering leaders, Aisha Okonkwo for Customer Services, Dr Sara Vance for Compliance).

### Exit process

1. Resignation acknowledged in writing by manager within 48h.
2. HRBP triggers Workday separation workflow.
3. Knowledge-transfer plan agreed within 5 business days.
4. Last-day checklist: equipment return, SSO offboarding, customer transitions documented.
5. Optional exit interview with HRBP (and one with VP People Maya Schwartz for L6+).

## Involuntary separations

Triggered only after one of: completed PIP without sustained improvement, serious policy violation, role elimination through reorganisation. Every involuntary separation requires two-person sign-off: the direct manager and the HRBP, with a third sign-off by the business-unit VP for L4+ separations.

### For-cause separations

- Egregious violations (e.g., harassment per anti-discrimination policy, deliberate `POL-DATA-v3.1` breach, theft) terminate immediately with no severance.
- Reportable-conduct cases are referred by Dr Sara Vance to authorities where required (e.g., HIPAA breach for Cresta Health Network data).

### Reorganisation separations

When a role is eliminated, Atlas offers:
- 60 days' notice or pay in lieu (90 for L4+, 120 for L6+).
- Severance: 2 weeks per year of service, minimum 6 weeks, maximum 26 weeks.
- 3 months' continued medical coverage (subject to local jurisdiction).
- Outplacement services with Lee Hecht Harrison.
- Re-hire eligibility preserved.

## Customer-impact transitions

Departures of named CSMs or technical leads with customer relationships (e.g., Liam Hayashi's APAC field leads, the engineers owning the Atlas E-220 deployment at Norvale Energy) require a customer-transition memo signed by the new owner and the leaving employee.

## Data return

Departing employees return all Atlas property and certify destruction of any Atlas data on personal devices (BYOD is restricted under `POL-DATA-v3.1`; this should be rare). Workday triggers SSO de-provisioning at 18:00 local on the last day.

## Non-disparagement and reference

Atlas does not enforce non-disparagement agreements that would gag legitimate workplace concerns. Standard reference is dates of employment + title; managers may give reference letters at their discretion for amicable departures.
