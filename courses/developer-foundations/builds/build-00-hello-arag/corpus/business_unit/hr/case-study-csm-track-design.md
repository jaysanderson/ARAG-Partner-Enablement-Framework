# Case Study — CSM Specialist Track Design (Aisha Okonkwo)

Director of Customer Success Aisha Okonkwo designed the Atlas CSM Specialist Track in FY26 as the customer-services equivalent of the Engineering IC track Marcus Ortiz exemplifies. This case study documents the track's design and outcomes for Maya Schwartz's "Specialist Tracks Beyond Engineering" research.

## Why the track was needed

Aisha's org had a tenure cliff at L4. Strong CSMs were either promoted to management (and often not happy there) or left for competitors. The premise: industrial customer success is a craft, not a stepping stone to management. The track needed to recognise depth.

## Track structure

| Level | Title | Profile |
|---|---|---|
| L3 | Senior CSM | Owns 4-6 accounts |
| L4 | Lead CSM | Owns 2-3 Tier-1 accounts; co-designs playbooks |
| L5 | Principal CSM | Industry-vertical authority (e.g., Healthcare Principal CSM for the Cresta Health Network practice) |
| L6 | Distinguished CSM | Atlas-wide CSM craft leadership; portfolio responsibility for one product family |

## Compensation parity

Per `compensation-bands-noram.md` and the regional variants, L5 Principal CSM and L6 Distinguished CSM bands are compensationally equal to their people-management counterparts at the same level. Equity refresh cadence identical.

## Vertical specialisation

Specialisation is anchored to the Atlas customer base. Current FY28 vertical Principal CSMs:
- Healthcare (Aisha Okonkwo personally — Cresta Health Network anchor).
- Utilities (Atlas Pulse and Atlas E-220 base, including Norvale Energy).
- Financial Services (Meridian Bank anchor).
- Logistics (Halcyon Logistics anchor).
- Heavy Industry (Talos Steelworks anchor; coordinated with Liam Hayashi's APAC field org).

## Adjacent design choices

- Co-designed playbooks shipped from L4 onwards — playbook authorship is recognised work.
- Customer voice in performance reviews per `performance-review-framework.md` — Aisha's CSMs receive structured customer feedback as input.
- Mentor relationships across business units — the Atlas Healthcare Principal CSM mentors Compliance hires onto Cresta Health Network's HIPAA posture in collaboration with Dr Sara Vance.

## Outcomes (24 months in)

- L4-L5 CSM tenure increased from 2.1 years (FY25) to 3.6 years (FY27).
- Customer NPS up across all five Tier-1 accounts (Norvale, Halcyon, Meridian, Cresta, Talos).
- 7 CSMs progressed to L5 Principal CSM (vs 1 promotion to CSM management). The "career means craft, not headcount" message landed.
- Talent Multiplier award to Aisha Okonkwo (FY27) — partly for the track design and partly for promoting four reports into other business units in one year.

## People-side learnings

1. A specialist track only works if compensation is genuinely equal at level.
2. The track needs visible work-products beyond individual account work — playbooks, vertical authority, mentorship.
3. Customer voice in reviews is reinforcing for specialist tracks where direct-manager observation is partial.
4. Maya Schwartz's calibration discipline (cross-org observation of CS calibrations) protected the track from level inflation.

## Replication

The pattern is now extending into Compliance under Dr Sara Vance — Compliance Principal track in design for FY29. Field Operations under Liam Hayashi already has an IC-style senior-field-tech track informally; formalisation is FY29 work.
