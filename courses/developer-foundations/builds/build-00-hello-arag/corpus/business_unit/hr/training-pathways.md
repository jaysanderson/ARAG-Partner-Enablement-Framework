# Atlas Training Pathways

Owned by Maya Schwartz (VP People). Defines training every Atlas employee completes.

## Atlas Code of Conduct (POL-CONDUCT-v3.1)

Mandatory — within 30 days of hire. Refresher annual.

## Data Handling (POL-DATA-v3.1)

Mandatory — within 30 days. Refresher annual. Stricter quarterly refresh for employees touching HIPAA-relevant data (e.g., teams supporting Cresta Health Network deployments).

## Incident Response (POL-INCIDENT-v2.0)

Mandatory for Engineering and Customer Services within 30 days. Other BUs within 60 days.

## Atlas Engineering Architecture Office sessions

Monthly. Marcus Ortiz chairs. Architecture reviews of in-flight engineering work. Open to all engineers.
