# Field Note — Jonah Reyes, Skyline 45L, Continental Divide Trail

Ambassador Jonah Reyes field-tested four prototype iterations of the Aurora Skyline 45L pack during his 2024 Continental Divide Trail thru-hike. His feedback on the hip-belt V-shape, brain-pocket dimensions, and load-lifter angle shaped the production geometry.

On the same thru-hike, Jonah carried the Aurora Quill 850 quilt — his testing across the CDT's desert and mountain phases drove the quilt's magnetic-snap footbox closure. He also carried the Aurora Helios jacket, which he cites as one of the three products (with Skyline 45L and Quill 850) that make up the bulk of his published base-weight breakdown.

Jonah's gear philosophy notes appear in two trail guides that recommend the Skyline 45L: the Tasmania Overland Track guide and the Tour du Mont Blanc guide. Both guides recommend the Skyline 45L for multi-day carries under 13 kg.
