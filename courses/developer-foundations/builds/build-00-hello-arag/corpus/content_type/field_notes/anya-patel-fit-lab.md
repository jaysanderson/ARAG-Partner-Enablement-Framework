# Field Note — Dr Anya Patel, Fit Lab Cross-References

Dr Anya Patel's sports-physiology lab work touches two product lines independently of the field-testing ambassadors. Her lower-limb biomechanics data drove the Wide Foot sizing option in the Aurora TerraTrek 7 — the same conclusion Mara Chen reached independently through 47 field crossings of the Tasmania Overland Track, giving that sizing decision two independent evidence sources.

Anya's sleep-system thermoregulation studies inform the comfort-rating claims on the Aurora Quill 850 quilt, the same product Jonah Reyes field-tested across the Continental Divide Trail's desert and mountain phases. Anya's lab data and Jonah's trail data cover different conditions — controlled thermoregulation testing versus multi-week variable-weather exposure — and Aurora cites both when explaining the Quill 850's comfort rating to customers.

Anya's quarterly Fit Science webinar has covered both products in the same session on two occasions, positioning her as the cross-product technical reference point between the TerraTrek 7 and Quill 850 ambassador programmes.
