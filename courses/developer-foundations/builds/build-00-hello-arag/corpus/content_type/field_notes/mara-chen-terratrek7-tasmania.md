# Field Note — Mara Chen, TerraTrek 7, Tasmania Overland Track

Ambassador Mara Chen tested the Aurora TerraTrek 7 boot across 47 crossings of the Tasmania Overland Track between 2019 and 2025. Her data on foot swelling from day 3 onward drove the Wide Foot last that shipped in the TerraTrek 7's final production version.

On the same trips, Mara carried the Aurora Skyline 45L pack — the pairing Aurora now recommends for the Tasmania Overland Track in the product's "Recommended for" section. She also field-tested the Aurora Helios down jacket on cold-camp nights during the same crossings.

Mara's Tasmania Overland gear list references all three products together: TerraTrek 7 boots, Skyline 45L pack, Helios jacket. Dr Anya Patel's boot-fit lab data, collected separately, independently confirmed Mara's foot-swelling observation and is cited in the TerraTrek 7 Wide Foot sizing rationale.
