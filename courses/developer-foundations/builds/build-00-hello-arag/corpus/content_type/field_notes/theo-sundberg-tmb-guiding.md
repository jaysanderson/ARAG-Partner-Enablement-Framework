# Field Note — Theo Sundberg, Tour du Mont Blanc Guided Groups

Ambassador Theo Sundberg has guided client groups on the Tour du Mont Blanc every summer since 2015 through his Chamonix outfit, Sundberg Alpine. His TMB gear notes — quoted directly in the trail guide — recommend three Aurora products together for the route: the TerraTrek 7 boot, the Skyline 45L pack, and the Helios down jacket for cold refuge nights.

Theo's primary product collaboration with Aurora is the Cumulus 2P tent, tested by his climbing-school teams in 50+ km/h Pyrenean wind events. He does not use the Cumulus 2P on standard TMB guiding trips, where clients sleep hut-to-hut rather than camping — the tent recommendation is specific to his separate alpine-skills courses.

The new visual topo guide for the Tour du Mont Blanc (`tour-du-mont-blanc-topo-guide.pdf`) documents the same three-product kit — TerraTrek 7, Skyline 45L, Helios — across the same ten-day itinerary Theo guides commercially.
