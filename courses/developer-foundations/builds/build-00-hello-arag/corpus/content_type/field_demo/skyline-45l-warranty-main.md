# Aurora Skyline 45L — Warranty Terms

Aurora Outfitters warrants the Skyline 45L pack against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship for a period of **2 years** from the original purchase date, to the original purchaser only.

## What's covered

- Seam failure under normal use
- Hardware failure (buckles, zippers, ladder-locks) under normal use
- Fabric delamination not caused by UV exposure beyond 500 hours

## What's not covered

- Damage from misuse, modification, or normal wear (abrasion, fading)
- Loss or theft
- Damage from improper storage (wet storage, rodent damage)

## How to claim

Bring the pack and proof of purchase to any Aurora Outfitters service depot, or contact support with photos and your order number. Approved claims are repaired free of charge or replaced with the current-season equivalent if repair isn't possible.
