# NORAM Benefits Schedule 2028

The full Atlas NORAM benefits schedule effective 2028. Owned by Maya Schwartz with annual renewal cycle. Costed against Mercer NORAM benefits benchmark.

## Medical

- **Atlas Medical Plus (default)** — Cigna OAP nationwide network. Atlas pays 85% employee premium, 75% dependant. Employee monthly contribution $135 / $310 / $470 by tier.
- **Atlas HSA-Eligible** — high-deductible Cigna with $1,500 Atlas HSA seed for individual, $3,000 family. Employee monthly contribution $42 / $128 / $190.
- **Atlas Kaiser (CA, CO, GA, HI, OR, WA, VA, MD, DC)** — Kaiser Permanente regional. Employee monthly contribution $115 / $268 / $410.

## Dental

- Delta Dental PPO Plus. 100% preventive, 80% basic, 50% major, $2,500 annual maximum.
- Atlas pays 100% employee premium, 60% dependant.

## Vision

- VSP Choice network. Annual eye exam covered, $200 frame/lens allowance, contact lens allowance $150.

## Disability

- Short-term disability 60% replacement up to 26 weeks, Atlas-paid.
- Long-term disability 60% replacement to age 65, Atlas-paid.

## Life and AD&D

- Atlas-paid life: 2x base salary, capped at $1m.
- AD&D: 2x base, capped at $1m.
- Voluntary supplemental life available up to additional 5x base.

## Retirement (401k)

- 6% Atlas match on employee contribution.
- Immediate vesting.
- Roth and traditional pre-tax options.
- Brokerage window available.

## Time off

- 20 days PTO (accrual) for L1-L4, 25 days for L5+, unlimited for L7+.
- 11 federal holidays.
- 4 floating cultural/religious days.
- 5 sick days (separate from PTO).
- Sabbatical eligibility after 7 years of service per `compensation-bands-noram.md`.

## Family

- Parental leave per `parental-leave-policy.md`.
- $5,000 adoption stipend per child.
- Fertility / IVF support up to $25,000 lifetime (via Carrot).
- Backup childcare 10 days/year (Bright Horizons).
- Eldercare backup 5 days/year.

## Mental health

- Atlas EAP via Spring Health. 8 sessions/year per employee + 4 per dependant, Atlas-paid.

## Learning

- Per `learning-budget-policy.md`: USD 2,000 / 3,500 / 5,000 by level.
- Tuition reimbursement up to USD 12,000/year for accredited degree programs.

## Atlas-specific

- Atlas annual all-hands travel reimbursed.
- Field-services premium per `compensation-bands-apac.md` cross-reference for any NORAM employee on extended APAC field rotation.
- Volunteer time-off: 24 hours/year paid.
