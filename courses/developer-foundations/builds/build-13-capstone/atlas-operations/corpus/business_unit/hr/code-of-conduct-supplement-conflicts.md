# Code of Conduct Supplement — Conflicts of Interest

This supplement extends the Atlas Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1` with detailed guidance on conflicts of interest. Owned by Maya Schwartz with Dr Sara Vance as compliance co-signer.

## Why this exists

Atlas operates across five business units and works with customers in regulated industries — Norvale Energy (utilities), Meridian Bank (financial services), Cresta Health Network (healthcare), Halcyon Logistics (3PL), Talos Steelworks (heavy industry). Conflicts of interest can compromise both customer trust and Atlas's own compliance posture under regimes such as SOX §404, HIPAA, GDPR, NIST 800-53 r5 and the EU AI Act.

## Defined conflict scenarios

### Financial

- Owning material equity (>1%) in a customer, supplier or competitor.
- Receiving gifts or hospitality valued over USD 250 per year from a single counterparty.
- Personal use of customer credits, rebates or referral fees.

### Relational

- Reporting to or supervising a family member or romantic partner.
- Being directly involved in a hiring, promotion or compensation decision for a family member or romantic partner.
- Concurrent material relationship with a vendor account team during procurement.

### Engagement

- Outside employment that competes with Atlas products (Atlas E-220, Atlas Logix, Atlas BuildingHub, Atlas FieldOps, Atlas Pulse, Atlas Grid, Atlas Aura, Atlas Ledger).
- Board service on a customer or competitor.
- Public statements or content that materially misrepresents an Atlas product or customer.

## Disclosure mechanism

Every Atlas employee files an annual COI declaration through Workday in January. Material changes require an updated declaration within 30 days of the change. Maya Schwartz and Dr Sara Vance jointly review L6+ declarations.

## Resolution pathways

| Type | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Pre-existing minor (e.g., spouse holds 0.5% in supplier ETF) | Documented disclosure, no action |
| Personnel conflict (reports/supervises family) | Reassignment within 60 days |
| Material financial conflict | Divestment or recusal, witnessed by HRBP |
| Competitive outside employment | Cessation, possible termination if undisclosed |

## Customer-specific addenda

Atlas employees engaged on Meridian Bank deployments face an additional financial-services pre-clearance requirement for personal trading in Meridian-listed securities. CSMs on Cresta Health Network are bound by an additional HIPAA awareness clause.

## Annual training

Conflicts-of-interest scenarios are covered in the 45-minute annual ethics module managed by Maya Schwartz's team.
