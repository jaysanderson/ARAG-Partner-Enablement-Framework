# Atlas Internal Mobility Policy

Atlas believes internal mobility is the strongest signal of culture health. This policy makes moving between teams predictable, fair and welcomed by both losing and receiving managers. Owned by Maya Schwartz.

## Principles

1. Employees own their careers; Atlas provides the rails.
2. Hoarding talent is a managerial anti-pattern — losing managers are recognised for nurturing movers.
3. Movement is non-secretive: notifying your manager is mandatory at application step.
4. Lateral moves at the same level are encouraged equally with promotion-track moves.

## Eligibility

- Minimum 12 months in current role (waivable by current manager + HRBP).
- Performance rated Strong or better in latest cycle.
- No active PIP.

## Process

1. Internal candidate notifies current manager of intent before applying.
2. Submits internal application through Workday Career Hub.
3. Hiring panel (3 interviewers minimum, at least one outside the receiving team).
4. Offer extended; transition window negotiated (default 30 days, up to 90).
5. HRBP coordinates start date, compensation (if change), and team rituals.

## Compensation on internal move

- Lateral move at same level: no automatic compensation change. Receiving manager may propose a market adjustment if justified.
- Promotion via internal move: aligned to band of new role.
- Cross-region move: see Global Mobility Policy. Compensation aligned to destination region bands per Maya Schwartz's framework.

## Notable internal-mobility patterns

- Engineering ↔ Customer Services: technical CSMs moving into Aisha Okonkwo's org from Marcus Ortiz's or Priya Anand's teams account for ~12% of CSM hires.
- Field Ops ↔ Engineering: Liam Hayashi has a documented program for promoting senior field techs into Engineering associate roles after 2 years on FieldOps deployments.
- Compliance attraction track: Dr Sara Vance runs a 6-month exchange program where engineers and CSMs rotate into Compliance for cross-domain depth.

## Manager incentives

Managers who lose talent to internal moves are recognised in their own performance reviews via a Talent Multiplier dimension. Aisha Okonkwo earned the FY27 Atlas Talent Multiplier Award by promoting four reports into other business units in one year.

## Boomerang

Atlas welcomes returning employees ("boomerangs") with no waiting period. Returning hires retain their original Atlas anniversary date for tenure-driven benefits (sabbatical eligibility, parental leave matching).
