# Proposal — Employee Resource Group Funding FY29

Prepared by Maya Schwartz's office for CEO and CFO approval. Decision needed: FY29 funding envelope for Atlas's Employee Resource Groups (ERGs).

## Background

Atlas hosts 11 ERGs globally:

- Atlas Women in Engineering
- Atlas Women in Field Operations (chaired by a senior tech in Liam Hayashi's org)
- Atlas Pride
- Atlas Black Network
- Atlas Latine Network
- Atlas Asian-Pacific Network
- Atlas Veterans
- Atlas Parents
- Atlas Disability Network
- Atlas Caregivers
- Atlas Sustainability (cross-cuts the Carbon-Negative by 2030 plan referenced in `onboarding-handbook.md`)

ERGs are open to all Atlas employees and are voluntary. FY28 funding was USD 320,000 globally.

## Proposal

Increase FY29 ERG funding to USD 540,000 globally with a structured allocation:

| Use | Proposed FY29 budget | FY28 actual |
|---|---|---|
| ERG chair stipend (10 hours/month equivalent, 11 chairs) | $96,000 | $0 (volunteer) |
| Event and program budgets (avg $30k per ERG) | $330,000 | $220,000 |
| Speaker series and external partnership | $60,000 | $40,000 |
| Annual ERG leader summit | $35,000 | $20,000 |
| Mentorship program technology | $19,000 | $40,000 |
| Total | $540,000 | $320,000 |

## Justifications

### Stipend for ERG chairs

The single biggest learning from FY28: ERG chair burnout is real and ERG chairs are typically high-performers also delivering full role. A modest stipend ($800/month) recognises the work and prevents the burnout pattern. Maya Schwartz consulted Aisha Okonkwo (whose Customer Services org houses 3 ERG chairs) and Priya Anand (whose EMEA Engineering houses 2) — both signal that the stipend would materially improve retention of ERG leadership.

### Event budget increase

ERG engagement scaled in FY28: total participants up 41%, events delivered up 33%. The same per-ERG event budget is too small. The increase brings per-ERG to the level Atlas peer companies fund at.

### Annual ERG leader summit

ERGs operate largely in regional silos. An annual global summit, modelled on Liam Hayashi's APAC cross-region rotation pattern, would build the cross-ERG and cross-region connective tissue.

## Outcomes expected

- Higher ERG chair retention (FY28 chair turnover was 36% — too high).
- Higher employee engagement scores in the dimensions ERGs influence (belonging, support, manager development).
- Better signal for the Compliance equity audit, since ERGs surface lived experience that policy alone doesn't.

## Risk

- Stipend introduces a compensation conversation. Mitigated by treating stipend as taxable extra-compensation in Workday, not as a discretionary bonus. Dr Sara Vance's office reviewed.
- Budget could be perceived as performative. Mitigated by transparent reporting of ERG output and impact in Atlas's annual people-report.

## Decision sought

CEO and CFO approval of USD 540,000 envelope, effective FY29 Q1 (April 2028). Maya Schwartz operationalises within the existing People Operations team capacity.
