# HR Runbook — Acquisition Integration (People Side)

When Atlas acquires another company, this runbook governs the people-side integration. Owned by Maya Schwartz with a dedicated M&A People Lead drafted into the integration steerco for each deal.

## Phases

### Phase 0: Due diligence (pre-signing)

- People-side due-diligence package: org chart, headcount by region, comp structure, benefits, attrition history, open complaints / investigations, regulatory posture (works councils, unions, immigration sponsorships).
- Compliance review by Dr Sara Vance's office for any data-handling implications.
- Cultural assessment by external partner (Atlas uses Spencer Stuart Cultural Diagnostic for deals over USD 50m).

### Phase 1: Announcement (T+0)

- All-hands at acquired company within 24 hours of close.
- Maya Schwartz attends.
- Pre-prepared FAQ shared in writing.
- Joint Slack channel opened.

### Phase 2: First 30 days

- Every acquired employee assigned an Atlas buddy.
- Welcome to Atlas onboarding tracks (compressed for context vs full new-hire flow).
- Comp banding mapped. Where bands diverge, mapping documented and adjustments scheduled.
- Workday provisioning at scale.
- Initial culture-alignment sessions.

### Phase 3: Days 30-90

- Functional integration — acquired teams plugged into Atlas business-unit org charts.
- Re-leveling exercises where titles or job content diverged.
- First Atlas performance-cycle inclusion.
- Manager Essentials enrolment for any acquired managers.

### Phase 4: Days 90-180

- Compensation full alignment to Atlas bands (`compensation-bands-noram.md`, `-emea.md`, `-apac.md`, `-latam.md`).
- Retention package mid-point evaluation.
- Skip-level conversations across acquired teams.

### Phase 5: Day 180-365

- Full integration retrospective.
- Attrition tracked against forecast.
- Maya Schwartz signs off integration "closed" status.

## Retention packages

Standard Atlas retention package for key acquired talent:
- 50% of one year's base in restricted stock units, vesting 50% at 12 months, 50% at 24 months.
- Eligibility curated by acquired-company leadership + Atlas business-unit VP (e.g., if acquiring an engineering company, Priya Anand or Marcus Ortiz nominates).
- Maya Schwartz signs off all retention packages.

## Compliance considerations

- Works councils in EMEA jurisdictions (Germany, France, Netherlands) — engagement starts at signing, not at close.
- TUPE / equivalent regulations.
- Pension transfers.
- Data Handling continuity per `POL-DATA-v3.1`.

## Cultural risk mitigation

- "Big-fish-in-small-pond" leaders sometimes struggle in the Atlas matrix. Coaching offered, paired with Atlas mentor (often Aisha Okonkwo or Liam Hayashi who have integrated multiple acquired teams).
- Pace of integration calibrated — too fast erodes acquired identity, too slow leaves the deal value on the table.

## Anti-patterns

- "Atlas way or the highway" tone at first all-hands.
- Skipping cultural diagnostic.
- Delaying compensation mapping past Day 90.
- Treating acquired-leader retention as transactional only.
