# APAC Benefits Schedule 2028

Atlas APAC benefits framework. Country-by-country statutory baseline plus Atlas supplemental. Owned by Maya Schwartz with APAC People Lead Hiroko Tanaka.

## Medical

- Singapore: AXA private medical, Atlas pays 100% employee + 60% dependant.
- Japan: shakai hoken statutory + Atlas private supplemental via AIG.
- Australia: Bupa AU private + statutory Medicare.
- India: ICICI Lombard group medical with INR 5 lakh sum insured, parents included.
- South Korea: NHI statutory + Atlas supplemental via Samsung Fire & Marine.
- Philippines: PhilHealth + Maxicare supplemental.

## Provident fund / superannuation

- Singapore CPF (statutory).
- Japan kosei nenkin (statutory).
- Australia superannuation 11.5% (statutory; Atlas pays above 11% guarantee).
- India EPF (statutory) + Atlas NPS Tier 1 match.
- South Korea NHIS / NPS (statutory).
- Philippines SSS + Atlas group RSP.

## Life and disability

- Life: 4x base, Atlas-paid.
- Long-term income protection where insurable in jurisdiction.

## Time off

- Singapore: 18 days PTO + statutory holidays.
- Tokyo: 18 days PTO + Golden Week.
- Sydney: 20 days PTO + statutory holidays.
- Bangalore: 24 days PTO + 12 holidays + statutory casual leave.
- Seoul: 15-25 days PTO depending on tenure.
- Manila: 15 days PTO + 10 holidays.

## Family

- Parental leave per `parental-leave-policy.md` with APAC statutory floor.
- Childcare allowance in Singapore (CDA-aligned).
- Fertility / IVF support up to USD 15,000 lifetime.

## Field-services premium

Per `compensation-bands-apac.md`: 12% on-call premium for FieldOps technicians carrying primary on-call. Material part of total compensation for Liam Hayashi's org.

## Mental health

- EAP via Workplace Options (regional provider).
- Country-specific augmentation in Tokyo, Singapore and Sydney.

## Learning

- Per `learning-budget-policy.md`: USD 1,500 / 2,800 / 4,200 by level.

## APAC-specific

- Tokyo: housing allowance for expat hires under global mobility.
- Singapore: transport allowance.
- Bangalore: meal allowance, cab return for evening on-call work.
- Manila: rice subsidy where statutory.
