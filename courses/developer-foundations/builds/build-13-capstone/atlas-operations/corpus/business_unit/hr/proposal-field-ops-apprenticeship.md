# Proposal — Field Operations Apprenticeship Program

Co-authored by Maya Schwartz and Liam Hayashi. Decision needed: approve a 3-year pilot of a structured field-operations apprenticeship across APAC and LATAM.

## Background

Liam Hayashi's APAC Field Operations org consistently struggles to source mid-career field technicians with the specific competencies Atlas needs — heavy-industry safety culture, hardware-software hybrid troubleshooting, customer-facing presence in regulated environments. Existing technical schools produce capable graduates but typically without the Atlas-specific depth that takes 18 months to build on the job.

The successful FY28 Talos Steelworks mass hire (`case-study-talos-mass-hire-apac.md`) demonstrated Atlas's ability to scale at speed, but also exposed the medium-term sourcing constraint.

## Proposal

Launch a 3-year Field Operations Apprenticeship Program, initially in APAC (year 1) and LATAM (year 2), with the following design:

### Cohort

- 30 apprentices per cohort, twice yearly.
- Sourced through 5 partner technical institutes (Singapore Polytechnic, Tokyo Tech, KAIST, KMUTT, HCMC University of Technology), expanding to LATAM partners in year 2 (SENAI BR, Tecnológico de Monterrey).
- Apprentices paid 70% of L1 band per `compensation-bands-apac.md` for the 18-month duration.

### Curriculum

- 18 months structured curriculum: 6 months base technical, 6 months Atlas product immersion, 6 months customer-site apprenticeship.
- Atlas FieldOps app mastery, Atlas BuildingHub familiarity, Atlas E-220 safety basics.
- Customer-engagement coaching with Aisha Okonkwo's CSM org partnering on the customer-presence module.
- Safety culture (`safety-policy-field-operations.md`) — Liam Hayashi personally teaches the stop-work module each cohort.

### Conversion

- 80% target conversion to FTE at L1-L2 per the bands.
- Sponsor managers identified at apprenticeship start.

### Cost (3-year program)

- Apprentice compensation: ~$2.4m (90 apprentices, 18 months, 70% L1)
- Curriculum development + delivery: $480,000
- Partner institute fees: $240,000
- Internal sponsor management: 0.5 FTE-equivalent year 1, 1.0 FTE year 2-3
- Total: ~$3.4m over 3 years

## Outcomes expected

- 70-80 long-term Atlas field technicians by end of year 3, sourced through this program.
- Per-hire cost lower than agency-sourced equivalent.
- Stronger Atlas pipeline into hard-to-source regions (LATAM particularly).
- Diversity of slate improved through institute partnerships including women-in-engineering tracks at KAIST and KMUTT.

## Risk

- 18-month commitment from apprentices may have higher attrition than expected. Mitigated by stipend structure and clear conversion pathway.
- LATAM expansion year-2 dependent on year-1 outcomes. Phase gating preserved.
- Operational load on Liam Hayashi's senior bench. Mitigated by Atlas Buddy Bench compensation structure used in the Talos Steelworks mass hire.

## Decision sought

Approval of $3.4m 3-year envelope and 1.0 FTE allocation for FY29. Maya Schwartz operationalises through HR Operations partnering with Liam Hayashi's APAC Field Operations team.
