# Atlas Grievance and Appeals Policy

Owned by Maya Schwartz. Atlas commits to a defined, non-retaliatory process for any employee to raise a grievance or appeal a decision affecting their employment — across HR, Engineering, Sales, Customer Services and Compliance.

## Scope

This policy covers:

- Disagreements with performance ratings under `performance-review-framework.md`.
- Disagreements with PIP placement or PIP outcomes under `runbook-pip.md`.
- Disagreements with compensation decisions outside bands (`compensation-bands-noram.md`, `-emea.md`, `-apac.md`, `-latam.md`).
- Disagreements with internal-mobility outcomes per `internal-mobility-policy.md`.
- Disagreements with separation decisions per `separation-policy.md`.
- General workplace concerns short of formal complaints (formal complaints route through the investigation protocol in `runbook-investigation-protocol.md`).

## Principles

1. Grievances are heard. Even where the outcome is unchanged, the employee receives a substantive written response.
2. The reviewer is independent of the original decision.
3. Timelines are bounded.
4. No retaliation, ever. Same standard as anti-discrimination policy.

## Levels

### Level 1: HRBP review

- File grievance with HRBP within 30 days of decision (or learning of decision).
- HRBP reviews evidence, interviews relevant parties (typically including the original decision-maker, e.g., a hiring panel under Aisha Okonkwo's CSM org or a promotion committee under Marcus Ortiz's Engineering org), and issues written response within 14 calendar days.

### Level 2: Business-unit VP review

- If unsatisfied with HRBP outcome, employee may escalate to the business-unit VP (Priya Anand for EMEA Engineering grievances; Marcus Ortiz for NORAM Engineering; Aisha Okonkwo for Customer Success; Liam Hayashi for Field Operations; Dr Sara Vance for Compliance; the relevant Sales VP for Sales; Maya Schwartz for HR).
- Filed within 14 days of Level 1 response.
- VP issues written decision within 21 calendar days.

### Level 3: VP People review

- Final internal escalation. Maya Schwartz reviews evidence, interviews relevant parties, issues written decision within 30 calendar days.
- Maya Schwartz's decision is final internally. Employees retain any external legal rights independently.

## Sensitive cases

- Grievances against a Level-2 VP route directly from Level 1 to Maya Schwartz.
- Grievances naming Maya Schwartz route to an independent external reviewer (Atlas retains Withers/Mercer for these rare cases).

## Data handling

Grievance files are Highly Restricted per `data-handling-hr-supplement.md`. Investigation files retained 7 years from closure.

## Aggregate review

Maya Schwartz's office reviews grievance trends quarterly. Patterns by business unit, by decision type, by demographic are surfaced. Trends that suggest a systemic issue are reported to the Compliance Office (Dr Sara Vance) for parallel review.

## Anti-patterns

- HRBP rubber-stamping the original decision without genuine review.
- VP delegating Level-2 review back to the original decision-maker.
- Long delays between escalation levels.
- Treating grievance filing as a negative performance signal.
