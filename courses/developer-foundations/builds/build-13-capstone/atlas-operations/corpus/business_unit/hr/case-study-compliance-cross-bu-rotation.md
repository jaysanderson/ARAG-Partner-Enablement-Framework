# Case Study — Compliance Cross-Business-Unit Rotation Program

Dr Sara Vance's 6-month exchange program (mentioned briefly in `internal-mobility-policy.md`) is documented in full here for Maya Schwartz's cross-business-unit talent-development brief.

## Premise

Compliance is most effective when its members understand the businesses they govern. Reading policies isn't enough — Compliance hires benefit from 6 months embedded in Engineering, Customer Services or Sales before settling into a Compliance role.

## Program design

### Eligibility

- Open to L2-L4 employees from any business unit, in good performance standing.
- Six-month duration, fully reversible (rotator returns to original role at original level guaranteed).

### Roles available

- Compliance analyst (NIST or EU AI Act track).
- Compliance program coordinator (audit work-stream).
- Data-handling governance specialist (interfaces with HR Data Handling Supplement).

### Compensation

Same level, same pay, no relocation required. Most rotators stay in their home city.

### Sponsorship

Each rotator is sponsored by their home VP (Priya Anand / Marcus Ortiz / Aisha Okonkwo / Liam Hayashi / Maya Schwartz) and hosted by Dr Sara Vance directly. Maya Schwartz signs off all rotations.

## FY27 cohort outcomes

8 rotators across 4 business units:
- 2 from Priya Anand's EMEA Engineering org (Atlas Pulse, Atlas E-220 backgrounds).
- 2 from Marcus Ortiz's NORAM Engineering org (Atlas Logix, Atlas Grid backgrounds).
- 2 from Aisha Okonkwo's CSM org (Cresta Health Network and Meridian Bank backgrounds).
- 2 from Liam Hayashi's APAC Field Operations (Atlas FieldOps backgrounds).

Of the 8:
- 3 returned to home role with deeper compliance awareness (the expected outcome).
- 4 converted to permanent Compliance roles (above expectation; Dr Sara Vance's track preference at the time was 1-2 conversions per cohort).
- 1 used the rotation as a stepping-stone to a different business unit (a Field Ops technician moved permanently to CSM after seeing the customer-engagement work up close).

## Customer-side outcomes

Several rotators worked on audit responses for their former customer accounts during the rotation:
- The Engineering rotators worked on the NIST 800-53 r5 evidence package for Atlas E-220 deployments at Norvale Energy.
- The CSM rotators worked on the HIPAA addendum framework for Cresta Health Network.
- The Logix rotator co-authored the `RB-Logix-MemLeak-002` compliance annex documenting the regulator-touching response to `INC-2027-0188`.

## People-side learnings

1. Reversibility was decisive — guaranteed-return removed friction in candidate self-selection.
2. The 6-month duration is long enough to do real work, short enough that home teams remain bought-in.
3. The cross-business-unit understanding the rotators built was valuable to their home roles regardless of conversion.
4. Maya Schwartz's calibration discipline ensured rotators were not under- or over-leveled on conversion.

## Replication potential

Aisha Okonkwo is exploring a parallel exchange between Customer Services and Engineering (FY29 design). Priya Anand and Marcus Ortiz have signalled support for hosting CSM rotators into Engineering, particularly for product-management-adjacent work.
