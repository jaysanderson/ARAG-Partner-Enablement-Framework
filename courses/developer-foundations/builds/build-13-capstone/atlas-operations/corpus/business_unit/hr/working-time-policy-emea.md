# EMEA Working Time Policy

This policy implements the EU Working Time Directive and equivalent UK regulations across all Atlas EMEA entities. Owned by Maya Schwartz, with regional HRBPs operating local addenda for Germany, France, Ireland, Netherlands, UK, Spain and Italy.

## Standard working hours

- 40-hour reference week (37.5 hours in jurisdictions where shorter is standard).
- Minimum 11 consecutive hours of rest in each 24-hour period.
- Minimum 24 consecutive hours of weekly rest.
- Maximum 48-hour week averaged over 17 weeks (Atlas opt-out for individuals is not used).

## On-call and Engineering rotations

Priya Anand's EMEA engineering teams cover production on-call for Atlas E-220 and Atlas Pulse. On-call rotations are bounded:

- Maximum 1 week of primary on-call per 6 weeks.
- Compensatory rest mandatory after any on-call activation that interrupts daily rest.
- Activations during scheduled off-hours are paid at 1.5x base hourly equivalent, capped at 20 hours per week.

These bounds were tightened following the Q4 turbine cooling regression `INC-2027-0142` and the published remediation runbook `RB-E220-Cooling-001` which exposed sustained on-call load on a thin team.

## Country addenda

- France: 35-hour reference week, RTT days for cadres.
- Germany: works council co-determination on rotation patterns.
- Netherlands: collective bargaining alignment.
- UK: post-Brexit Working Time Regulations 1998 still apply.

## Time tracking

EMEA L1-L4 employees record time daily in Workday Time Tracking. L5+ employees are exempt unless their country requires recording (Spain, Italy). The Atlas Logix dev team in Dublin pioneered the Slack-to-Workday connector that 60% of EMEA Engineering now uses.

## Sundays and public holidays

Work on Sundays or public holidays requires written approval from the regional HRBP and is paid at 2x.

## Disconnection right

EMEA employees have a formal right to disconnect outside working hours and on rest days. Managers (including Priya Anand and Aisha Okonkwo's EMEA reports) are accountable for respecting this. Repeated breaches trigger HRBP intervention.
