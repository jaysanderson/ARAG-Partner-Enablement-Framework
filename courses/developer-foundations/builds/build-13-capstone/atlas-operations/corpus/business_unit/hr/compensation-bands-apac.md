# APAC Compensation Bands 2028

APAC bands cover full-time Atlas employees in Japan, Singapore, Australia, India, South Korea and the Philippines. Owned by Maya Schwartz, with regional HRBP input from APAC People Lead Hiroko Tanaka (Singapore).

## Local cost-of-labour multipliers

Bands are expressed in USD-equivalent for parity. Local payroll converts at the audited Atlas FX rate (refreshed quarterly).

| Country | Multiplier vs APAC reference |
|---|---|
| Singapore | 1.00 (reference) |
| Tokyo | 1.05 |
| Sydney | 0.98 |
| Seoul | 0.85 |
| Bangalore | 0.55 |
| Manila | 0.50 |

## Field-services premium

APAC Field Operations roles supporting Atlas FieldOps and Talos Steelworks deployments receive a 12% on-call premium above band when carrying primary on-call rotation. This premium was added in FY28 following the FieldOps offline sync corruption incident `INC-2028-0034` to recognise the on-call load Liam Hayashi's team absorbed.

## Variable compensation

- L1-L3: 10% target.
- L4-L5: 15% target.
- L6-L7: 22% target + equity refresh.

## Benefits

- Statutory minima + Atlas top-up to private medical in Singapore, Australia and Japan.
- Learning budget USD 1,500/year.
- Country-specific provident fund / CPF / superannuation contributions per local law.

## Cross-region mobility

Permanent transfers from APAC to NORAM/EMEA are paid at destination bands from day one. Short-term assignments (<12 months) retain home-country compensation with a 25% expat uplift. Maya Schwartz signs off all transfers L4+.
