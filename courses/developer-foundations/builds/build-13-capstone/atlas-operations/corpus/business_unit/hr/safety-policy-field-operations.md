# Atlas Field Operations Safety Policy

Atlas Field Operations technicians work in customer-controlled industrial environments — turbine halls (Norvale Energy with Atlas E-220 and Atlas Pulse), distribution centres (Halcyon Logistics with Atlas Logix), hospital plant rooms (Cresta Health Network with Atlas BuildingHub), and steel works (Talos Steelworks with Atlas FieldOps). Safety is non-negotiable. Owned by Maya Schwartz with Head of Field Operations Liam Hayashi as operational authority.

## Personal protective equipment

| Site type | Mandatory PPE |
|---|---|
| Power generation (Atlas E-220, Atlas Pulse, Atlas Grid) | Arc-flash suit (Cat 2 minimum), hard hat, safety glasses, hearing protection, steel-toe boots |
| Distribution / warehouse (Atlas Logix) | Hi-vis vest, hard hat, steel-toe boots, cut-resistant gloves |
| Building automation (Atlas BuildingHub, Atlas Aura) | Standard PPE; respirator in plant rooms |
| Steel works (Atlas FieldOps at Talos Steelworks) | Heat-resistant outerwear, face shield, FR coveralls, steel-toe boots |

PPE is issued by Atlas. Annual fit-check and condition audit by site.

## Right to stop work

Every Atlas FieldOps technician has unconditional authority to stop work in any environment that appears unsafe. Stop-work decisions are protected — no employee may be retaliated against, even if the stop turns out to be precautionary. This was reaffirmed following the Atlas FieldOps offline sync corruption incident `INC-2028-0034` where a technician's stop-work call prevented a downstream safety event during the rollback procedure documented in `RB-FieldOps-Sync-004`.

## Pre-job safety briefing

Every site visit begins with a documented pre-job safety brief covering customer-site hazards, isolation procedures and emergency contacts. The briefing is logged in Atlas FieldOps app within five minutes of arrival.

## Incident reporting

| Severity | Reporting path |
|---|---|
| Near-miss | Logged in FieldOps app; weekly review by site lead |
| Minor injury (first aid only) | Logged + HRBP notified within 24h |
| Serious injury / lost-time | Liam Hayashi notified within 1h, Maya Schwartz within 4h, regional regulator within 8h |
| Fatality | Crisis-management protocol; CEO, Maya Schwartz, Dr Sara Vance, CISO simultaneously |

## Driving

Atlas does not require driving as a condition of FieldOps employment. Where it is part of the role, defensive-driving certification (Smith System or equivalent) is mandatory annually.

## Working alone

Solo work in customer plant areas is prohibited. The minimum on-site team is two for any Atlas E-220, Atlas Pulse or Talos steel-works engagement. BuildingHub installs may be solo with documented site lock-out / tag-out (LOTO).

## Heat and cold

Liam Hayashi's APAC operations have detailed heat-stress procedures (drink schedules, work-rest cycles) for sites in Singapore, Manila and northern Australia. Cold-weather equivalents exist for Canadian and Nordic deployments.
