# Case Study — Field Ops Cross-Region Rotation (Liam Hayashi)

Liam Hayashi, Head of Field Operations (APAC), runs an annual 90-day APAC-to-LATAM cross-region rotation that has become the model for Atlas's broader cross-region knowledge transfer. This case study documents the program's design and outcomes for Maya Schwartz's Global Mobility briefing pack.

## Context

LATAM is Atlas's youngest region. As Atlas FieldOps deployments scaled in Mexico, Brazil and Argentina, the local field operations bench needed depth in two specific areas: (1) the Atlas FieldOps mobile app and its offline-sync patterns and (2) the customer-engagement playbook for Atlas FieldOps deployments at industrial customers.

Liam's APAC org had developed this depth over the preceding 4 years, including the operational learning from the `INC-2028-0034` FieldOps offline sync corruption incident and the deep familiarity with `RB-FieldOps-Sync-004`.

## Design

### Selection

- 4 APAC FieldOps technicians per cohort, mid-career (L3-L4).
- Selected for technical depth, customer presence, willingness to teach.
- Application + nomination process — never compulsory.

### Duration

90 days, structured as:
- Weeks 1-2: orientation, Spanish/Portuguese language immersion (Berlitz, funded by Mobility).
- Weeks 3-10: paired field work with LATAM technicians at customer sites.
- Weeks 11-12: documented knowledge transfer + LATAM technician shadowing of APAC patterns.

### Compensation and mobility

Per `global-mobility-policy.md` short-term assignment provisions. Home-country payroll plus per diem. Family support optional (not common for 90-day STA).

### LATAM counter-rotation

In parallel, 4 LATAM technicians spend 90 days in APAC. Maya Schwartz's Global Mobility team coordinates the visa logistics.

## Outcomes (FY27 cohort, the second program year)

- LATAM Atlas FieldOps customer NPS rose from 32 to 51 in the 12 months following the rotation completion.
- Three of the four LATAM counter-rotation participants were promoted to L4 within 18 months.
- Zero attrition in the APAC rotators within 24 months — the rotation increased their Atlas commitment.
- Co-developed asset: a Spanish-language quick-reference for the offline-sync stop-work decision tree, now standard in LATAM training packs.

## People-side learnings

1. Cross-region rotation works when both directions are funded equally — counter-rotation matters.
2. Selecting for willingness to teach (not just technical depth) was decisive.
3. Language support is non-optional. 2 weeks of immersion is the floor.
4. Family-friendly design (90 days, not 12 months) widens the candidate pool dramatically vs traditional LTAs.

## Cross-org observations

Aisha Okonkwo's Customer Success org has adopted a parallel pattern for CSM cross-region exchanges. The Atlas Logix deep-pattern exchange between Marcus Ortiz's NORAM org and Priya Anand's EMEA org is also a sibling pattern (engineering-led).

Maya Schwartz cites Liam's program in the FY28 Global Mobility refresh as the reference design for short-term cross-region knowledge transfer.
