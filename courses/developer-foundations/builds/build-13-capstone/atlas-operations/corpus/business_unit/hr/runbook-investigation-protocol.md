# HR Runbook — Workplace Investigation Protocol

This runbook governs the operational execution of an internal investigation under the Atlas Anti-Discrimination & Harassment Policy and the Atlas Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`. Owned by Maya Schwartz with Dr Sara Vance as compliance reviewer.

## When this runbook fires

A formal complaint received through any of the four channels:
1. Manager / skip-level intake.
2. HRBP intake.
3. Direct to Maya Schwartz (typically L6+ allegations).
4. Atlas Ethics Line (NavexEthics) intake.

## Steps

### Step 1: Acknowledge (T+0 to T+48h)

- Investigator acknowledged the report to the complainant within 48 hours.
- Confidentiality reaffirmed.
- Initial categorisation: minor / moderate / severe / egregious per discipline matrix.

### Step 2: Assign (by T+5 business days)

- Investigator selected from the certified investigator pool. Investigator independence verified — no reporting line to either party.
- Sensitive cases (L6+ implicated, or involving Compliance leadership) handled by external counsel coordinated by Dr Sara Vance's office.
- Maya Schwartz approves L6+ cases personally.

### Step 3: Plan (by T+10 business days)

- Investigation plan documented: witnesses to interview, evidence to preserve, expected timeline (target 30-45 days).
- Both parties notified of investigation underway.
- Any required interim measures (separation of duties, temporary reassignment) implemented.

### Step 4: Interview (T+10 to T+30 days)

- Complainant interview (audio recorded with consent).
- Respondent interview (audio recorded with consent).
- Witness interviews.
- Evidence collection: emails, Slack, Workday records, swipe logs as relevant. POL-DATA-v3.1 controls apply.

### Step 5: Findings memo (T+30 to T+45 days)

- Written findings memo: facts established, facts disputed, conclusion against the relevant policy clause, recommended outcome.
- Memo reviewed by HR Legal.
- Maya Schwartz signs off for moderate+ findings.

### Step 6: Outcome (T+45 to T+50 days)

- Outcome communicated to both parties.
- Disciplinary action executed if applicable.
- Complainant offered support resources (EAP, mediation, transfer).

### Step 7: Closure and learning (T+60 days)

- Case closed in NavexEthics with structured tags.
- Aggregate trends reviewed quarterly by Maya Schwartz and Dr Sara Vance.
- Themes feed back into manager training (Manager Essentials Module 6).

## Special situations

### Allegation against L6+ leader (e.g., named anchor employees)

External counsel mandatory. Board notification if conclusion is substantiated for L7+. Maya Schwartz personally informs the CEO.

### Cross-region investigation

GDPR / local data protection adds 5-7 days; EMEA cases route through the regional DPO for SCC-compliant data transfer of investigation materials.

### Customer-overlapping conduct

If alleged conduct occurred at a customer site (e.g., a field engagement at Norvale Energy or Cresta Health Network), customer's site safety / conduct team is informed of the existence of the investigation without disclosing details, unless safety implication mandates more.

## Anti-patterns

- Investigating a complaint that involves your own reporting line.
- Skipping the interim-measures step "because investigation is fast".
- Sharing findings beyond strict need-to-know.
- Failing to follow up with the complainant after closure.
