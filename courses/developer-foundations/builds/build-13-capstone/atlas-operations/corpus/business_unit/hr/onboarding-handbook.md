# Atlas Onboarding Handbook

Welcome to Atlas Global Industries. This handbook applies to all new hires across the five business units: HR, Engineering, Sales, Customer Services, and Compliance.

## Day 1

- Report to your team's primary office (or join the remote-onboarding Zoom link if remote-eligible).
- Meet your hiring manager. For Engineering hires, this is typically **Priya Anand** (VP Engineering) for E-220 / Pulse teams, or **Marcus Ortiz** (Principal Architect) for Logix / Grid teams.
- For Customer Services hires, your hiring manager is **Aisha Okonkwo** (Director of Customer Success) or **Liam Hayashi** (Head of Field Operations).
- Complete the Atlas Code of Conduct training (`POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`) and Data Handling Policy training (`POL-DATA-v3.1`).

## Week 1

- Be assigned a buddy from your business unit. Coffee chats scheduled.
- Read the Atlas Incident Response Policy (`POL-INCIDENT-v2.0`) regardless of business unit. Every Atlas employee is expected to recognise and report an incident.
- For Customer Services hires: shadow your buddy on three customer calls in the first week. Norvale Energy, Halcyon Logistics, or Meridian Bank are typical first-week shadow targets.

## Days 30 / 60 / 90

The 30/60/90 plan is generated per hire by the Onboarding-Path Generator. It synthesises:

- HR module assignments.
- Product overview reading for products owned by your business unit (e.g., Atlas E-220 if you're on Priya's team, Atlas Logix if you're on Marcus's).
- Compliance modules relevant to your region (EU AI Act for EMEA hires, NIST 800-53 r5 for NORAM hires).
- Assigned customer intro calls if you're in Customer Services or Sales.

## Compensation and benefits

Compensation is benchmarked per region. EMEA bands live in `compensation-bands-emea.md`. NORAM bands are in the NORAM equivalent. Talk to **Maya Schwartz** (VP People) for any compensation questions outside the documented bands.

## Repair-for-Life and sustainability

Atlas is enrolled in the Carbon-Negative by 2030 plan. New hires complete a mandatory Sustainability 101 module in their first 30 days.
