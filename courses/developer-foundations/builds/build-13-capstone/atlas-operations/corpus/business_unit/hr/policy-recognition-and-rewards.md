# Atlas Recognition and Rewards Policy

Atlas formal recognition framework. Recognition matters — it shapes culture, drives retention, and signals what good looks like. Owned by Maya Schwartz with annual review.

## Principles

1. Recognition is timely. The closer in time to the deed, the stronger the signal.
2. Recognition is specific. "Great work" recognises nothing; "your post-mortem analysis on `INC-2027-0142` set a new bar for the EMEA Engineering org" recognises a specific contribution.
3. Recognition is multi-channel. Peer-to-peer, manager-to-report, executive, public.
4. Recognition is multi-currency. Money is one part; visibility, opportunity, and time are others.

## Programs

### Peer recognition (Bonusly)

- All employees can recognise peers with small monetary tokens (USD 10-50 equivalents) plus written recognition.
- Quarterly individual cap to prevent gaming.
- Visible on team feeds (with opt-out).

### Manager spot recognition

- Managers have a quarterly discretionary spend (USD 500 per direct report) for spot bonuses, gift cards, or experiences.
- Documented in Workday; visible to skip-level.

### Atlas Quarterly Award

- One award per quarter per business unit, nominated by peers, decided by VP.
- Quarterly: USD 5,000 + RSU top-up + executive visibility.
- Notable recent recipients include the Atlas FieldOps engineer whose stop-work call during `INC-2028-0034` prevented a downstream safety event (recognised Q1 FY28 by Liam Hayashi).

### Talent Multiplier Award

- Annual, recognising managers who developed others — including losing reports to internal mobility per `internal-mobility-policy.md`.
- USD 10,000 + RSU + featured story in Atlas annual people-report.
- Aisha Okonkwo won FY27 for promoting four reports into other business units in one year.

### Founders' Grant

- Senior IC tenure recognition per `benefits-equity-program.md`. Marcus Ortiz received FY27 grant for his architectural lineage contribution.

### Sustained Excellence Award

- Annual, recognising 5+ year sustained outstanding contribution.
- USD 25,000 + sabbatical week + Atlas-funded executive coaching session.

### CEO Award

- Annual, 3 recipients across Atlas, decided by CEO.
- USD 50,000 + RSU + featured profile + executive sponsor for the following year.

## Cross-business-unit recognition

Atlas explicitly recognises cross-business-unit collaboration. The Cross-BU Excellence Award recognises teams that delivered together. The FY28 award went jointly to the Compliance and Engineering teams who responded to the EU AI Act regulator inquiry on Atlas BuildingHub — Dr Sara Vance, Aisha Okonkwo and Priya Anand co-led that response.

## Anti-incentives

The recognition framework also identifies anti-incentives to remove:

- Tenure-based recognition that rewards staying without contributing — Atlas does not award tenure alone (Founders' Grant requires sustained L5+ impact).
- Manager-favourite patterns — peer-recognition data is analysed quarterly for clustering.
- "Recognition theatre" — Maya Schwartz audits award distributions for genuine differentiation.

## Equity

The full recognition program is reviewed by Dr Sara Vance under `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1` for distribution by demographic. FY27 review showed no statistically significant disparities; FY28 in progress.

## Manager training

Manager Essentials Module 9 (Talent Multiplier) covers recognition framing. Per `manager-essentials-curriculum.md` every Atlas first-time manager completes the curriculum within 90 days.
