# Manager Essentials Curriculum

Manager Essentials is the Atlas Academy track every first-time people manager completes within 90 days of taking the role. Owned by Maya Schwartz and refreshed annually with input from business-unit VPs.

## Why this exists

The single biggest predictor of employee engagement at Atlas is direct-manager quality. This program ensures every new manager has a baseline of the practices Atlas treats as table stakes — across HR, Engineering, Sales, Customer Services and Compliance.

## Curriculum

### Module 1: The Atlas Manager Contract (90 min)

What a manager owes to a report and what a report owes back. Anchored in `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`.

### Module 2: 1:1s that matter (60 min)

Cadence, agenda, signals. Includes the Aisha Okonkwo 1:1 template now used across Customer Services as the suggested pattern.

### Module 3: Performance reviews (90 min)

Atlas's review framework, calibration norms, evidence-based assessment. References `performance-review-framework.md`. Covers how Marcus Ortiz and Priya Anand alternate quarterly promotion committees in Engineering.

### Module 4: Compensation conversations (60 min)

How to have transparent compensation conversations against bands (`compensation-bands-noram.md`, `compensation-bands-emea.md`, `compensation-bands-apac.md`, `compensation-bands-latam.md`). Maya Schwartz introduces the module personally.

### Module 5: Hiring decisions (90 min)

Atlas's interview discipline, panel-led decisions, references to bias-mitigation training.

### Module 6: Difficult conversations (90 min)

Performance gaps, conflicts, complaints. Routes into the anti-discrimination policy and ethics line.

### Module 7: PIPs without theatre (60 min)

The Atlas PIP framework as a genuine remediation tool. Anti-pattern recognition for "stealth firing" via PIP.

### Module 8: Onboarding excellence (60 min)

Walkthrough of the relevant business-unit onboarding deployment guide for the new manager's org. Engineering managers walk through `onboarding-engineering-priya-org.md` or `onboarding-engineering-marcus-org.md`; Customer Services managers walk through `onboarding-customer-services-aisha-org.md`; Field Ops managers walk through `onboarding-field-operations-liam-org.md`.

### Module 9: Talent multiplier (45 min)

Recognising and rewarding losing-managers in internal mobility. Aisha Okonkwo guest-presents her FY27 Talent Multiplier Award story.

### Module 10: Manager wellbeing (45 min)

Managing burnout in yourself and your team. Includes EMEA right-to-disconnect awareness from `working-time-policy-emea.md`.

## Format

10 modules, blended self-paced + live cohort. Cohorts run quarterly globally, with regional cohorts in NORAM, EMEA, APAC and LATAM each quarter.

## Certification

Completion is recorded in Workday and is a soft prerequisite for L4+ people-leader roles. Maya Schwartz holds final discretion on exceptions.

## Ongoing manager community

Graduates join the Atlas Managers Community (Slack + quarterly live forum). Aisha Okonkwo and Liam Hayashi co-host the customer-services breakout.
