# LATAM Compensation Bands 2028

LATAM is Atlas's youngest region (formal entity established 2024) and covers Mexico City, São Paulo, Buenos Aires and Bogotá. The Atlas Logix and Atlas FieldOps customer base in LATAM is growing fastest, so most LATAM hiring is in Customer Services and Sales.

## Bands

| Level | Role examples | LATAM reference band (USD-equiv) |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate, Field Tech I | 32,000 – 46,000 |
| L2 | Engineer, Specialist, Field Tech II | 46,000 – 64,000 |
| L3 | Senior Engineer, Senior CSM | 64,000 – 88,000 |
| L4 | Staff, Lead, Regional CSM | 88,000 – 118,000 |
| L5 | Principal, Country Manager | 118,000 – 152,000 |
| L6 | Director | 152,000 – 195,000 |

## Country premiums

- Mexico City: +8% (reflects Atlas regional HQ premium and proximity to NORAM customers spanning the border).
- São Paulo: reference.
- Buenos Aires: indexed quarterly against ARS/USD with a 90-day floor to protect against currency shock.
- Bogotá: -5% reference.

## Variable compensation

- L1-L3: 10% target.
- L4-L5: 15% target.
- L6: 22% target.

## Benefits

- Mexico: IMSS + Atlas top-up private medical (AXA Keralty).
- Brazil: CLT-compliant package, 30 days PTO, 13th-month salary.
- Argentina: monotributo and full-CLT options for senior hires.
- Colombia: standard EPS + Atlas top-up.

## Field-services note

LATAM FieldOps deployments lean on the Liam Hayashi APAC playbooks heavily, given the customer profile overlap. The LATAM on-call rotation runs against the same paging schedule but compensated at the LATAM band; Liam Hayashi has cross-training rights as Head of Field Operations.
