# Atlas Global Mobility Policy

Atlas's 60,000 employees span four regions. Mobility — temporary assignments, permanent transfers and remote-from-anywhere arrangements — is governed by this policy. Owned by Maya Schwartz with input from regional People Leads.

## Mobility types

| Type | Duration | Compensation source |
|---|---|---|
| Short-term assignment (STA) | 3-12 months | Home country payroll + per diem |
| Long-term assignment (LTA) | 12-36 months | Host country payroll, tax equalisation |
| Permanent transfer | Indefinite | Host country bands fully |
| Cross-border remote | <30 days/year | Home, with compliance check |

## Triggers

Common Atlas mobility scenarios:

- Engineering deep-dive support (e.g., a Marcus Ortiz NORAM Logix engineer spending 6 months in Dublin with Priya Anand's EMEA team to share architectural pattern work).
- Customer Services field surge for a major deployment (Aisha Okonkwo regularly rotates EMEA CSMs into NORAM to support Halcyon Logistics scale-out windows).
- Field Operations cross-training (Liam Hayashi runs an annual 90-day APAC-to-LATAM rotation to seed LATAM operators with Atlas FieldOps deep expertise).
- Career-development LTAs for L5+ talent who need cross-region exposure to advance to L6.

## Sponsorship and visa

Atlas Mobility (a small team within Maya Schwartz's org) handles all visa and right-to-work casework through Fragomen (NORAM/EMEA) and Crown World Mobility (APAC/LATAM). Employees are not expected to navigate immigration alone.

## Tax equalisation

For LTAs, Atlas equalises tax burden so an employee is no better and no worse off than they would have been on home-country tax for the assignment duration. Annual reconciliation through PwC GMS. STAs use a simpler tax-protection regime.

## Family support

- Spousal job-search support (3 months Lee Hecht Harrison or local equivalent).
- Schooling allowance for dependants up to USD 24,000/year, capped at age 18.
- Two annual home leaves for LTAs.
- Pet relocation up to two animals, capped at USD 6,000.

## Cultural integration

Every LTA includes a Berlitz cultural orientation and language access. APAC inbound LTAs are paired with a local buddy through Liam Hayashi's regional buddy registry.

## Failed assignments

A failed LTA (early return at company request or mutual agreement) is supported with a guaranteed home-country role at equivalent level. Failed assignments are non-disciplinary — Atlas treats failure as a system signal, not a personnel signal.
