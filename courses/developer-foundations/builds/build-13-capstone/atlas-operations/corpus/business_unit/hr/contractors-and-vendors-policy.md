# Contractors and Vendors Policy

Atlas engages contingent workforce — independent contractors, agency contractors, vendor-supplied staff augmentation — across all five business units. This policy ensures legal classification, consistent treatment and protection of restricted data. Owned by Maya Schwartz with vendor-management leadership.

## Classifications

| Type | Definition | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| Independent contractor (1099 / equivalent) | Self-employed, project scoped | Specialist short engagements (e.g., a hardware safety auditor for an Atlas E-220 line) |
| Agency contractor | Employed by staffing agency | Engineering surge (Marcus Ortiz's NORAM teams) |
| SOW-based vendor | Vendor delivers an outcome | Implementation partners for Atlas BuildingHub deployments |
| Embedded contractor | Vendor staff embedded in Atlas team | Rare; requires Maya Schwartz approval |

## Classification audit

Each new contractor engagement is reviewed against the relevant jurisdiction's worker-classification test (e.g., IRS 20-factor, EU country tests, CCPA-equivalent). The classification is recorded in Workday's Contractor Module. Misclassification risk is monitored by Dr Sara Vance's compliance team.

## Onboarding

- Background check appropriate to data access (Atlas BG package level 2 for any contractor with Restricted data access).
- Atlas Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1` acknowledgment, Data Handling `POL-DATA-v3.1` training, Incident Response `POL-INCIDENT-v2.0` awareness.
- SSO account scoped to project duration.
- Equipment: Atlas-managed laptop for any Restricted data access.

## Compensation and benefits

Contractors are not eligible for Atlas employee benefits (medical, equity, learning budget, parental leave). Contractor day rates are benchmarked separately and reviewed annually.

## Time limits

- Independent contractors: 12 months maximum continuous engagement (extendable with classification re-audit).
- Agency contractors: 24 months. Beyond 24 months requires conversion to FTE or termination — long agency engagements create classification risk in many jurisdictions.

## Conversion to FTE

Converting a high-performing contractor to FTE is encouraged. Process mirrors external hire (panel interview, offer with band placement) but the contractor's existing knowledge is recognised in level placement.

## Customer data and contractors

Contractors engaged on customer accounts (Norvale Energy, Halcyon Logistics, Meridian Bank, Cresta Health Network, Talos Steelworks) must complete the customer's required clearances. For Cresta Health Network engagements this includes HIPAA training; for Meridian Bank, the financial-services pre-clearance set. Aisha Okonkwo's CSM-Ops team coordinates customer-required clearance per engagement.

## Termination

Contractor engagements end on the SOW end date or with seven days' notice. Termination for cause is immediate.
