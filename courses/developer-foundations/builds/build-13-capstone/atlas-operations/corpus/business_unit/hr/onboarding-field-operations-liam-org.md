# Onboarding Deployment Guide — Field Operations (Liam Hayashi org)

Operational guide for HRBPs onboarding into Liam Hayashi's APAC-led Field Operations org. The org dispatches technicians and field engineers globally across all Atlas product families, with primary site coverage for Atlas FieldOps customers including Talos Steelworks.

## Pre-boarding (T-14 to T-0)

- HRBP confirms regional placement (APAC, NORAM, EMEA or LATAM). Liam's org is APAC-led but operates globally.
- Site induction packets prepared.
- PPE order placed per `safety-policy-field-operations.md`.
- Equipment: Atlas-rugged tablet for field app, Atlas-managed laptop.

## Day 1

- Welcome by regional lead. Liam Hayashi attends Day 1 by video for all NORAM/EMEA/LATAM new hires; in person for APAC.
- `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`, `POL-DATA-v3.1`, `POL-INCIDENT-v2.0` modules.
- Safety briefing: walk through `safety-policy-field-operations.md` line by line.
- Driving-credential check, defensive-driving cert verified.

## Week 1

- Atlas FieldOps app training (product cert level 1).
- Product certs for the customer sites the technician will cover: Talos pod → Atlas FieldOps, Norvale Energy pod → Atlas E-220, Halcyon Logistics pod → Atlas Logix, Cresta pod → Atlas BuildingHub.
- Shadow three site visits — never solo until Day 60 minimum.
- Read `RB-FieldOps-Sync-004` (FieldOps offline sync corruption remediation post `INC-2028-0034`). This is mandatory because every FieldOps technician needs to recognise sync corruption symptoms and stop work appropriately.

## Days 30 / 60 / 90

- Day 30: shadow + assist on five site visits.
- Day 60: lead one site visit with shadow support.
- Day 90: solo authorisation granted by regional lead + Liam Hayashi sign-off.

## Customer-specific addenda

- **Talos Steelworks**: heat-stress training, FR PPE certification.
- **Cresta Health Network**: HIPAA awareness, hospital-environment infection-control briefing.
- **Norvale Energy**: high-voltage safety + arc-flash certification.
- **Halcyon Logistics**: warehouse-traffic awareness, forklift-pedestrian protocols.

## Cross-region context

Liam Hayashi's safety culture and on-call rotation patterns are the global Atlas standard. NORAM, EMEA and LATAM Field Operations regions implement his playbooks with regional adaptation. Aisha Okonkwo's CSM org partners closely on customer-facing communication.

## Common pitfalls

- Solo work before Day 90.
- Skipping pre-job safety briefing logging.
- Underestimating customer-specific clearance renewal cadence.
- Not exercising stop-work authority because of perceived schedule pressure.
