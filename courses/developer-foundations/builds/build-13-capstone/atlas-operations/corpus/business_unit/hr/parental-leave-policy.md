# Atlas Parental Leave Policy

Owned by Maya Schwartz. Atlas provides parental leave equally to all caregivers regardless of region of employment, gender, or pathway to parenthood (birth, adoption, fostering, surrogacy).

## Standard provisions

- **Primary caregiver:** 24 weeks paid, 16 additional unpaid.
- **Secondary caregiver:** 12 weeks paid, 12 additional unpaid.
- **Pregnancy loss:** 2 weeks paid bereavement.
- **Adoption support stipend:** USD 5,000 per child.

## Return-to-work

- Phased return (60% then 80% then 100%) available across first 8 weeks back, full pay throughout.
- Travel restrictions during first 12 weeks back (optional).

## Regional supplements

EMEA: matched against the higher of Atlas policy or statutory minimum.
APAC: same.
LATAM: same.

## Process

Notify your manager and HRBP at least 12 weeks before expected start of leave. HRBP coordinates handover plan. Maya Schwartz signs off final plan if expected leave is >20 weeks.
