# EMEA Benefits Schedule 2028

Atlas EMEA benefits framework. Country-level statutory provisions take precedence where Atlas standard does not exceed them. Owned by Maya Schwartz with regional HRBPs.

## Medical

- Country-by-country private medical: BUPA UK, Allianz DE, Generali FR, Laya Healthcare IE, Vitality NL.
- Atlas pays 100% employee, 60% dependant in all EMEA jurisdictions.
- Statutory health system remains primary in single-payer jurisdictions; Atlas private cover is supplemental.

## Pension

- UK: 8% employer, 5% employee minimum (Atlas matches up to 8%/3% under salary sacrifice).
- DE: statutory contribution + Atlas DC supplemental 5% of base.
- FR: statutory retraite complémentaire (AGIRC-ARRCO) + Atlas PER 3% of base.
- IE: Atlas DC 8% with 5% employee match.
- NL: Atlas DC scheme aligned to industry market.
- ES, IT: statutory plus Atlas DC 4% supplemental.

## Life and disability

- Life cover: 4x base salary, Atlas-paid.
- Long-term income protection: 75% replacement to age 65.
- Critical illness lump-sum: 1x base.

## Time off

- 25 days PTO floor + statutory bank holidays (varies by country).
- France: RTT days for cadre roles.
- Sabbatical eligibility after 5 years (1 year earlier than NORAM).
- Right to disconnect per `working-time-policy-emea.md`.

## Family

- Parental leave per `parental-leave-policy.md` with EMEA statutory floor.
- Childcare voucher / equivalent in jurisdictions where tax-efficient.
- Fertility / IVF support up to EUR 20,000 lifetime.

## Mental health

- Atlas EAP via Spring Health (UK, IE) and local providers elsewhere.

## Learning

- Per `learning-budget-policy.md`: EUR 1,500 / 2,800 / 4,200 by level.
- Tuition / advanced-study support discretionary, manager-approved.

## EMEA-specific

- Cycle-to-work or equivalent transport benefit in UK, IE, NL, DE.
- Commuter-allowance in jurisdictions where statutory.
- Meal vouchers in FR, IT.

## Country-statutory floor

If Atlas standard is below statutory floor in a country, the statutory floor applies. Maya Schwartz reviews annually with each country lead.
