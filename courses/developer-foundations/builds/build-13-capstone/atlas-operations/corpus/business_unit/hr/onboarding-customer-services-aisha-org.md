# Onboarding Deployment Guide — Customer Services (Aisha Okonkwo org)

Operational guide for HRBPs onboarding into Aisha Okonkwo's Customer Success org. The org owns CSM coverage across Atlas's customer base, with named accounts including Norvale Energy (primary CSM Priya Anand by exception — engineering-led), Cresta Health Network (Aisha herself as primary), Meridian Bank (Dr Sara Vance secondary), Halcyon Logistics (Marcus Ortiz secondary), Talos Steelworks (Liam Hayashi field-led).

## Pre-boarding (T-14 to T-0)

- HRBP confirms account assignment. Aisha's office maintains the account-pod registry.
- Equipment ships. SSO provisioning.
- Pre-read: Customer Success Playbook, Atlas Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`.
- Customer-specific NDA acknowledgments where the new CSM will touch Cresta Health Network (HIPAA) or Meridian Bank (financial-services pre-clearance).

## Day 1

- Welcome by Aisha Okonkwo (she attends every new CSM Day 1).
- `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1` and `POL-DATA-v3.1` modules.
- Account-pod intro: shadow CSM, technical AM, support partner.

## Week 1

- Atlas product certifications (level 1) for the account's products. For a Cresta-Health-pod CSM, this is Atlas BuildingHub and Atlas Aura. For a Meridian-Bank-pod CSM, Atlas Ledger.
- Customer health framework training.
- Shadow three customer calls.
- Read at least one post-mortem touching the account's product family. For Cresta pod, read `RB-BldHub-Firmware-003` covering `INC-2028-0019` (BuildingHub firmware rollback).

## Days 30 / 60 / 90

- Day 30: first solo customer call (typically a check-in, not a steering committee).
- Day 60: full pod handover for one named subaccount. Buddy steps back.
- Day 90: contribute to next-quarter Account Plan for one named account. Aisha reviews Day-90 plans personally for new CSMs covering Tier-1 customers (Cresta Health Network, Meridian Bank).

## Customer-specific addenda

- **Cresta Health Network** new CSMs complete HIPAA awareness within 14 days and Cresta's customer-mandated background recheck.
- **Meridian Bank** new CSMs complete the financial-services pre-clearance set; trading restrictions per `code-of-conduct-supplement-conflicts.md`.
- **Norvale Energy** new CSMs (rare; the account is largely engineering-led under Priya Anand) shadow Priya's team for two weeks.

## Cross-region context

Liam Hayashi's APAC field operations org partners closely with Aisha's CSM org. Joint pods exist for Talos Steelworks and for FieldOps deployments globally. Aisha and Liam co-own the customer-health methodology.

## Common pitfalls

- Treating product certs as optional.
- Skipping the post-mortem reading because "I'm in CSM not engineering" — knowing the failure modes is what makes a CSM credible.
- Not declaring customer-mandated background-check renewals (annual).
