# Deployment · Norvale Energy E-220 Turbine Fleet

Customer-specific deployment record for Norvale Energy's Atlas E-220 turbine fleet. Owned by Priya Anand.

## Customer profile

- **Customer:** Norvale Energy
- **Region:** EMEA
- **CSM:** Priya Anand
- **Initial fleet contract:** 2022-04-30
- **Current fleet:** 12 Atlas E-220 turbines across 3 sites

## Sites and units

| Site | Units | First commissioned | Last major service |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rotterdam Wind 1 | 4 | 2022-09 | 2027-11 |
| Antwerp Wind 2 | 4 | 2023-04 | 2028-02 |
| North Sea Wind 12 | 4 | 2024-10 | 2028-04 |

## Recent operational events

- INC-2027-0142 (Q4 2027) — turbine cooling regression affected 3 of the 12 units during a cold-snap window. Remediated via RB-E220-Cooling-001 in 38 days.
- 2028-01-10 — service-credit settlement (EUR 1.2M issued, matching customer-side production impact estimate).
- 2028-02-22 — annual maintenance complete at Antwerp Wind 2.
- 2028-04-15 — annual maintenance complete at North Sea Wind 12.

## Service contract

- Full-service maintenance contract (annual maintenance + 24x7 emergency response).
- Repair-for-Life cost model in effect.
- 5-year TCO ~22% lower than next-best alternative.

## Joint governance

- Joint engineering steering committee chaired quarterly by Priya Anand.
- Annual operations review co-located with the Norvale plant engineering leadership.
- Emergency response paged through Norvale's 24x7 desk to Atlas's EMEA on-call rotation.
