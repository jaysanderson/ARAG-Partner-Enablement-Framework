# Case Study · Halcyon Logistics Atlas Logix

**Customer:** Halcyon Logistics
**Region:** NORAM (with select APAC operations)
**Primary CSM:** Marcus Ortiz (Principal Architect)
**Years as Atlas customer:** 4

## Background

Halcyon Logistics is a 3PL (third-party logistics) provider operating across NORAM and selected APAC routes. Atlas Logix powers their routing and shipment optimisation engine.

## What Atlas does for Halcyon

- Atlas Logix production deployment routing ~3M shipments per quarter.
- 47 × Atlas FieldOps devices in use by field-service teams.
- Engineering steering review monthly with Marcus Ortiz.

## Recent operational events

- INC-2027-0188 (Q4 2027 - Q1 2028) — Atlas Logix routing engine memory leak. Halcyon-side throughput impact estimated at ~3% additional fleet miles over a 12-week window. Atlas issued service credits. Remediated via patch 4.7.3 then full upgrade to 4.8.
- INC-2028-0034 (Q1 2028) — FieldOps offline sync corruption affected 12 of Halcyon's 47 field devices. Remediated via runbook RB-FieldOps-Sync-004 in 1 week.

## Why Halcyon stays with Atlas

1. Marcus Ortiz's personal involvement in both incidents.
2. Atlas Logix routing-cost optimisation has saved Halcyon ~7% on annual fleet costs vs the prior in-house system.
3. Service-credit policy clarity — Atlas issues credits proactively without dispute.

## Expansion roadmap

- Atlas Logix Phase 2 expansion (APAC routes) — planned Q4 2028.
- Atlas Pulse for warehouse energy management at three Halcyon distribution centres — under evaluation.

## Compliance posture

- NIST 800-53 r5 — Halcyon does federal-government shipping for selected DoD partners; the routing engine satisfies federal compliance.
- No HIPAA or EU AI Act applicability.
