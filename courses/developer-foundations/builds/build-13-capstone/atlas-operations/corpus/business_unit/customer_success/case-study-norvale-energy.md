# Case Study · Norvale Energy E-220 Fleet

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Region:** EMEA
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand (VP Engineering — strategic-tier engineering-led relationship)
**Years as Atlas customer:** 6

## Background

Norvale Energy operates utility-scale renewable energy generation in EMEA. The Atlas E-220 turbine fleet is the cornerstone of their generation capacity portfolio. Norvale is also the lead customer for the Atlas Pulse pilot.

## What Atlas does for Norvale

- 12 × Atlas E-220 turbines across three EMEA sites.
- Full-service maintenance contract (annual maintenance + emergency response).
- Joint engineering steering committee meeting quarterly.

## Recent operational events

- INC-2027-0142 (Q4 2027) — turbine cooling regression affected three sites during a cold-snap window. Atlas-side remediation in 38 days. Runbook RB-E220-Cooling-001 applied. Norvale-side production impact estimated at €1.2M; Atlas issued service credits matching.

## Why Norvale stays with Atlas

1. Engineering-led relationship. Priya Anand (VP Engineering) is the customer-facing executive, not a salesperson. Norvale's plant engineering team trusts the technical conversation.
2. Repair-for-Life cost model. Norvale's 5-year TCO on the E-220 fleet is ~22% lower than the next-best alternative because of resolution and maintenance economics.
3. Sustainability alignment. Atlas's Carbon-Negative by 2030 commitment maps to Norvale's own ESG reporting.

## Expansion roadmap

- Atlas Pulse — 4 unit pilot in Q3 2028, expansion to 12 units planned for 2029.
- Atlas Grid pilot — Q3 2028 conditional on Pulse pilot success.
- Atlas FieldOps for plant maintenance teams — early conversation, on roadmap for 2029.

## Compliance posture

- EU AI Act — Pulse smart-grid optimisation features require AI Act applicability statement, owned by Dr Sara Vance.
- GDPR — controller-processor delineation refreshed annually.
- Audit findings — AF-2027-014 (Norvale-related, INC-2027-0142 follow-up) closed Q1 2028.
