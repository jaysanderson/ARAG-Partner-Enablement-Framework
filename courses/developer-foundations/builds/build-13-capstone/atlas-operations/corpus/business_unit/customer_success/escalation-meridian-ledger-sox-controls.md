# Escalation · Meridian Bank Ledger SOX Controls Refresh

**Customer:** Meridian Bank
**Product:** Atlas Ledger
**Primary CSM:** Dr Sara Vance
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Janelle Pham, Meridian Chief Auditor
**Status:** Closed — refreshed control set in production 2028-05-30

## Trigger

Meridian Bank's external auditor flagged a control gap in the Atlas Ledger SOX §404 attestation. Specifically: the existing change-management evidence trail did not record per-environment promotion approvals at sufficient granularity to satisfy the auditor's new evidence threshold.

This was not an Atlas product defect — it was an auditor expectation drift in 2028.

## What Dr Sara Vance did

As the primary CSM on Meridian (an unusual arrangement reflecting the customer's deeply compliance-led relationship), Dr Sara Vance handled this escalation end-to-end:

- Day 1: Acknowledge to Janelle Pham, commit to remediation plan within 10 business days.
- Day 4: Internal workshop with Marcus Ortiz (Atlas Ledger product owner) on evidence-emission feature.
- Day 8: Atlas Ledger 2028.5 control-evidence enhancement feature scoped.
- Day 10: Remediation plan delivered to Janelle Pham.

## Delivery

- 2028-04-12: Atlas Ledger 2028.5 enhanced control-evidence emission shipped.
- 2028-05-04: Meridian's auditor accepts the enhanced evidence pack.
- 2028-05-30: Meridian's SOX §404 attestation refresh passes clean.

## Outcomes

- Meridian SOX attestation clean for FY2028.
- Atlas Ledger 2028.5 control-evidence emission shipped to all Ledger customers.
- Janelle Pham's quote in her internal report: "Atlas's customer-success function and compliance function operate as a single team. That is rare and valuable."
