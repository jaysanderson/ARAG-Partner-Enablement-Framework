# Escalation · Norvale FieldOps Rollout Sequencing Dispute

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Product:** Atlas FieldOps
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Field Ops partner:** Liam Hayashi
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Aaron Kim, Norvale Senior Plant Operations Manager
**Status:** Closed — agreed phased rollout starting Q3 2028

## Trigger

Norvale's FieldOps procurement was negotiated for Q4 2028 install at three EMEA plant sites simultaneously. Aaron Kim, Norvale's senior plant operations manager (EMEA fitter background, recently promoted), formally requested a phased rollout instead — one site, evaluate, then the rest. He cited concern about FieldOps's offline-sync history (INC-2028-0034) and the criticality of plant maintenance reliability at Norvale's lead site.

## Why this required escalation handling

The dispute was operational, not commercial — but the sequencing decision would slip Norvale's revenue recognition window for the FieldOps deal. Atlas commercial team initially pushed back on the phased approach. Aaron Kim escalated through Priya Anand.

## What Priya did

Priya Anand sided with the customer on the operational question. Specifically:

1. Validated Aaron Kim's concern as professionally legitimate.
2. Brought Liam Hayashi (Head of Field Operations, FieldOps domain owner) into the conversation.
3. Recommended the phased approach to Atlas's revenue committee, accepting the revenue-timing impact.
4. Worked with the commercial team to restructure the FieldOps contract to recognise revenue at each site go-live.

## Resolution

- 2028-03-15: Phased rollout plan agreed.
- 2028-06-15: Site 1 (Rotterdam) FieldOps install complete.
- 2028-07-15: Site 1 evaluation complete — clean.
- 2028-09-01: Sites 2 and 3 go-live.

## Outcomes

- Norvale Aaron Kim's confidence in FieldOps deepened.
- Atlas restructured FieldOps commercial templates to support site-by-site recognition by default.
- Aaron Kim's quote: "Priya could have argued with me. Instead she asked questions, listened, and made the call. That is who I want as my customer success partner."
