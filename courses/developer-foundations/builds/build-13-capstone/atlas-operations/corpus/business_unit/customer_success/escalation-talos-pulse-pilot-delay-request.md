# Escalation · Talos Pulse Pilot Delay Request

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Product:** Atlas Pulse (8-unit pilot)
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Hiroshi Tanaka, Talos VP Operations
**Status:** Closed — pilot delayed by one quarter to Q4 2028

## Trigger

Talos's planned Atlas Pulse 8-unit pilot was scheduled for Q3 2028. In May 2028, Hiroshi Tanaka requested a one-quarter delay citing internal capacity constraints — Talos's plant engineering team had a major furnace re-lining project consuming bandwidth through August.

## Why this landed as a formal escalation

Pulse Q3 deliveries were already manufactured and committed at Atlas's APAC production schedule. Re-allocating them carried inventory cost. Hiroshi Tanaka asked for the delay to be granted without service-credit penalty.

## What Liam did

Liam Hayashi treated this as a relationship-investment moment:

1. Granted the delay request without contractual pushback.
2. Worked with Marcus Ortiz on the inventory-redistribution plan (units re-allocated to Norvale's Q3 expansion).
3. Used the freed Q3 capacity to bring forward a no-cost Atlas FieldOps health-check at all 47 Talos FieldOps devices.
4. Memo'd Hiroshi Tanaka explicitly: "no penalty, no commercial consequences."

## Outcomes

- Pulse pilot delayed to Q4 2028 install / Q1 2029 evaluation.
- Talos furnace re-lining project completed on time without distraction.
- Inventory re-allocation absorbed cleanly via Norvale's expansion order.
- Hiroshi Tanaka's confidence in Atlas as a true partner (not a vendor-extracting-revenue-on-schedule) reinforced.
- Talos confirmed Atlas Grid follow-on commitment.
