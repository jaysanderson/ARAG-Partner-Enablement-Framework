# Escalation · Talos Steelworks Grid Evaluation Data Residency

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Product:** Atlas Grid (evaluation phase)
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Compliance partner:** Dr Sara Vance
**Executive sponsor (customer-side):** Kenji Sato, Talos Chief Information Officer
**Status:** Closed — evaluation proceeding under revised data-residency commitment

## Trigger

During Talos's Atlas Grid evaluation in April 2028, Kenji Sato escalated through Liam Hayashi a concern that the default Grid cloud-telemetry architecture routed data through a NORAM region. Talos's internal data-residency policy required APAC-resident processing for all generation-asset telemetry.

## What Liam did

Liam Hayashi rated this as a P1 sales-blocking customer-success escalation. The action plan:

- Day 1: Acknowledge to Kenji + commit to written response within 5 business days.
- Day 2: Bring Dr Sara Vance in for compliance scoping (APAC data-residency expectations).
- Day 3: Engage Marcus Ortiz (product owner) on the architecture-change feasibility.
- Day 5: Written response delivered confirming an APAC-resident telemetry option for the Grid evaluation, available no later than 2028-06-15.

## Resolution

- 2028-06-12: APAC-resident telemetry option shipped to Talos evaluation environment.
- 2028-06-18: Kenji Sato confirms data-residency posture acceptable.
- Grid evaluation proceeds. Decision-window: Q4 2028.

## Outcomes

- Talos Grid evaluation retained on calendar.
- APAC-resident telemetry now offered as a standard option on the Atlas Grid product — driven by the Talos escalation.
- Kenji Sato's quote: "Liam-san didn't argue our policy — he solved for it."
