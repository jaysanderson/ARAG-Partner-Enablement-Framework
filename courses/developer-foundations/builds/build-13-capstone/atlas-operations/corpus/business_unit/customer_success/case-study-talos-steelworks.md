# Case Study · Talos Steelworks

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Region:** APAC
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Atlas tenure:** 7 years

## Background

Talos Steelworks operates one of the largest steel-production facilities in APAC. Atlas E-220 (12 turbines) + Atlas FieldOps (47 devices) + soon Atlas Pulse (8 units Q3 2028).

## Recent operational events

- INC-2027-0142 (Q4 2027) — turbine cooling regression. One Talos site affected. Remediated via RB-E220-Cooling-001 (2028-01-22).
- INC-2028-0034 (Q1 2028) — FieldOps offline sync corruption. 47 devices affected. Remediated via RB-FieldOps-Sync-004 in 1 week.

## Why Talos stays with Atlas

- Liam Hayashi's regular APAC visits + Japanese-language documentation availability.
- Repair-for-Life cost model materially below competitor pricing for heavy-industry equipment.
- Engineering-led trust via Priya Anand's annual visit.

## Expansion roadmap

- Atlas Pulse 8 units pilot Q3 2028 → expansion to 12 units 2029.
- Atlas Grid (energy distribution) under evaluation.

## Compliance posture

- No EU AI Act or HIPAA applicability.
- ISO 27001 compliance aligned with Atlas Repair-for-Life programme.
