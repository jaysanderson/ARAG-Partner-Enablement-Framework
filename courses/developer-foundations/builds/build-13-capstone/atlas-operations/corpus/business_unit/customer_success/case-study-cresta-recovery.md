# Case Study · Cresta Health Network Relationship Recovery

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Region:** EMEA
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Programme:** Post-INC-2028-0019 customer-trust rebuild

## Why this case study exists

Following INC-2028-0019, Cresta's NPS dropped sharply (52 in Q1 2028 measurement). Aisha Okonkwo's response — over Q2 and Q3 2028 — has been adopted as a template for customer-trust rebuild scenarios across the Atlas customer success function.

## The action plan

1. **Acknowledge the dip without spinning.** Cresta's NPS drop was named and acknowledged in writing, no defensive framing.
2. **Monthly facilities-team check-ins.** Aisha personally led monthly working sessions with Eleanor Brightwell and her facilities engineering team through end of Q2 2028.
3. **Quarterly executive standup.** Eleanor Brightwell + Priya Anand + Aisha — quarterly cadence, no slides, conversation-led.
4. **Joint HIPAA audit-prep support.** Atlas committed to a co-built audit-evidence pack with Cresta CISO Marcus Whitfield (see `escalation-cresta-hipaa-audit-prep.md`).
5. **No new-business pitch.** Aura was on hold until Eleanor reopened the conversation herself in April 2028.

## Sequence of recovery indicators

- 2028-02: NPS = 52.
- 2028-05-22: Eleanor approves the Aura pilot (Atlas's first signal of restored trust).
- 2028-06-12: Cresta's external HIPAA audit passes with zero findings.
- 2028-06 NPS = 64 (lifted 12 points).
- 2028-09 NPS = 71 (lifted further 7 points; back into healthy range).

## Quote attributed to Eleanor Brightwell

> "Aisha didn't sell — she listened. That's why I'm in this room."

## Quote attributed to Marcus Whitfield (CISO)

> "The partnership has matured visibly since INC-2028-0019."

## Lessons captured

- Customer trust dips can be inflection-points: handled badly they accelerate churn; handled well they deepen the relationship.
- The CSM's job in a trust-dip is to listen, not to sell.
- Compliance partnership (Dr Sara Vance's involvement) is a relationship-deepener, not just a feature.
