# Customer Success Runbook · Quarterly Business Review Cadence

**Owner:** Aisha Okonkwo (Director of Customer Success)
**Region:** Global
**Applies to:** All Atlas Tier-1 and Tier-2 customers

## Pre-QBR (T-30 days)

1. Pull customer-success health metrics: NPS trend, incident history, deployment milestones, expansion pipeline.
2. Pull product-side update from each owning engineering team.
3. Pull compliance posture summary from Dr Sara Vance.
4. Draft a 4-section QBR brief (operational, strategic, commercial, roadmap).

## QBR meeting (T-0)

| Time | Section | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 0-15 min | Customer operational update | Customer-side host |
| 15-30 min | Atlas operational update | CSM |
| 30-45 min | Strategic discussion | Customer + Atlas executive sponsors |
| 45-60 min | Roadmap and next-quarter commitments | CSM + customer host |

## QBR composition per customer type

- **Engineering-led (Norvale):** Priya Anand chairs. No slides; conversation-led. Customer's engineering leadership present.
- **Compliance-led (Meridian):** Dr Sara Vance chairs alongside Janelle Pham. Compliance-posture summary is the main artefact.
- **Operations-led (Halcyon, Cresta, Talos):** CSM chairs. Operational scorecard is the main artefact.

## Post-QBR (T+7 days)

1. QBR notes circulated to all attendees.
2. Action items entered into the customer-success tracking system.
3. Atlas-side commitments translated into trackable engineering or operational tasks.
4. Next-quarter QBR scheduled.

## Adjusting cadence

- Tier-1 customers: quarterly always.
- Tier-2 customers: quarterly default; semi-annual if customer requests.
- Post-incident trust-rebuild: monthly check-in for 90 days, layered on top of the quarterly QBR.

## Anti-patterns

- Slide-heavy QBRs. The customers Atlas wants don't want PowerPoint.
- Sales-led QBRs disguised as customer-success conversations. Atlas's customer success function does not sell.
- Atlas-side absences. The CSM must be there in person (or video) for every QBR.
