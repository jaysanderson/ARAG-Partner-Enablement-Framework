# Atlas Board Compliance Briefing — Q3 2028

**Prepared for:** Board Audit Committee
**Prepared by:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)
**Reporting period:** 2028-Q3

## Executive summary

Atlas's compliance posture in Q3 2028 strengthened materially. The HIPAA-impact-assessment process born from AF-2028-007 cleared its first full quarter. The Atlas Ledger SOX posture remained zero-finding at Meridian Bank. EU AI Act applicability work delivered customer-facing statements for Norvale Energy and Cresta Health Network. Two minor audit findings opened (AF-2028-025, AF-2028-040); both remediations are in flight on target.

## Material changes

- POL-AIUSE-v1.0 effective 2028-05-01. Adoption complete across all five business units. Spot audit (AF-2028-055) identified two non-registry LLM tools in NORAM engineering; closed in Q4.
- POL-SUBPROC-v1.0 effective 2028-04-01. Sub-processor registry published; customer notice obligations met.
- POL-XBORDER-v1.0 effective 2028-04-30. SCC framework in place across EMEA customer base.

## Active customer compliance contexts

- **Cresta Health Network**: HIPAA-impact-assessment process operating; DPIA refresh in flight (AF-2028-040).
- **Norvale Energy**: EU AI Act applicability statement delivered; NIS2 supply-chain RFC under Council review.
- **Meridian Bank**: SOX §404 zero-finding maintained; ITGC automation RFC under Council and CFO review.
- **Halcyon Logistics**: Per-record access logging remediation in flight (AF-2028-025).
- **Talos Steelworks**: APAC data-residency rehearsal-script remediation closed (AF-2028-031); FieldOps classification RFC under Council review.

## Forward outlook

Q4 2028 will close the remaining open findings except AF-2028-046 (Atlas Ledger pen-test backlog) which targets Q1 2029. The Atlas Customer Trust Portal RFC is the largest forward investment under consideration.
