# RFC — NIS2 Applicability for Atlas Pulse at Norvale Energy

**Status:** Draft for Atlas Compliance Council review
**Author:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)
**Co-author:** Priya Anand (VP Engineering)
**Target decision date:** 2028-Q4

## Question

What are Atlas's obligations under the EU NIS2 Directive when Atlas Pulse is deployed to Norvale Energy, an energy-sector essential entity?

## Background

NIS2 applies to essential entities in the energy sector. Norvale Energy is in scope as a customer. The directive imposes risk-management measures and incident-reporting obligations on the essential entity, and increasingly anticipates that the entity's ICT supply chain (including Atlas Pulse as a smart-grid optimisation provider) is itself robust to the same standards.

## Atlas's position

Atlas is not directly in NIS2 scope as a regulated entity. However, contractual flow-down from Norvale Energy can effectively impose NIS2-aligned obligations on Atlas Pulse delivery.

## Proposed posture

1. Atlas Pulse maintains the POL-SECBASE-v2.0 control baseline, which exceeds NIS2 Article 21 minimums.
2. Atlas Pulse incident reporting via POL-INCIDENT-v2.0 satisfies the 24-hour early-warning and 72-hour full-notification expectations Norvale would need to pass through.
3. Atlas executes an addendum to the Norvale Energy MSA acknowledging NIS2-supply-chain alignment as a soft commitment.

## Customer impact

- Norvale Energy gains a defensible NIS2 supply-chain assurance from Atlas.
- Future EMEA energy-sector customers benefit from the same template.

## Open questions

- Does NIS2 contractually obligate Atlas's sub-processors as well? Resolution: yes — flow-through via POL-SUBPROC-v1.0.
- Should Atlas pursue voluntary NIS2-aligned certification? Recommendation: defer to 2029 once ENISA scheme matures.
