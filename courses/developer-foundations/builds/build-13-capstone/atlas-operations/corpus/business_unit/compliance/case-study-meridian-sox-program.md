# Meridian Bank SOX §404 Program — 2028 Year in Review

**Prepared by:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)
**Audience:** Atlas Board Audit Committee, Atlas CFO, Meridian Bank engagement team

## Headline

Atlas Ledger maintained a zero-finding SOX §404 posture across all three Meridian Bank audit cycles in 2028 — the third consecutive year of clean audits.

## What we delivered

For each Meridian Bank audit cycle, Atlas delivered:

- The Atlas Ledger SOC 2 Type II report.
- Atlas-side ITGC documentation refreshed quarterly.
- Atlas penetration test summary (2028 cycle delivered 2028-Q2).
- Atlas incident registry summary (no SOX-material incidents).
- Quarterly access-review attestations covering Atlas Ledger administrative interfaces.

## What didn't go to plan

- **AF-2028-018** (Q1 access review cadence slip): 14-day window miss. No inappropriate access found. Root cause: handoff between Atlas Compliance and Meridian IT liaison. Remediated by 30-day advance calendaring and KPI tracking.

- **AF-2028-046** (Q4 pen-test recommendation backlog): 7 Medium-severity recommendations open past the 90-day target. Burndown on track for Q1 2029 closure.

- **AF-2028-037** (Q3 Grid SOX-pilot documentation freshness): documentation lag, no control gap. Quarterly refresh cadence established.

## What we'll change

The RFC for Atlas Ledger SOX ITGC automation, currently under Council and CFO review, addresses the root-cause class of all three findings: ITGC tasks tracked in calendar tools rather than as enforceable control checkpoints. If approved, the 2029 audit cycles will operate against a measurably stronger control plane.

## Meridian Bank feedback

Meridian Bank's external SOX auditor (Tier 1 firm) verbally noted Atlas Ledger as one of the more mature vendor-side SOX postures observed in the year. The Meridian Compliance Officer expressed appreciation for Atlas's transparency on AF-2028-018 and AF-2028-046 in advance of formal audit observation.
