# Atlas EU AI Act Readiness Program — 2028 Year in Review

**Prepared by:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO) and Priya Anand (VP Engineering)
**Audience:** Atlas Board, Atlas Compliance Council, EMEA customer advisory group

## Programme objective

Establish Atlas's product-by-product EU AI Act applicability position and operationalise the obligations for in-scope products by the 2028 effective milestones.

## Scope

Eight Atlas products: Atlas E-220, Atlas Pulse, Atlas BuildingHub, Atlas Logix, Atlas FieldOps, Atlas Grid, Atlas Aura, Atlas Ledger. Four are AI Act applicable at the Limited Risk tier; four are not applicable.

## What we shipped in 2028

- **Atlas EU AI Act Applicability Position 2028.** Published Q1; reviewed annually.
- **POL-AIUSE-v1.0.** Effective 2028-05-01. Operationalises product AI obligations and governs internal AI use.
- **Customer-facing applicability statements.** Delivered to Norvale Energy (Pulse) and Cresta Health Network (BuildingHub) in Q2 2028.
- **Algorithmic impact assessments.** Authored and signed for Atlas Pulse (Priya Anand), Atlas BuildingHub (Aisha Okonkwo), Atlas Logix (Marcus Ortiz), Atlas Grid (Marcus Ortiz).
- **Human-oversight UX patterns.** Embedded in Atlas BuildingHub and Atlas Logix; in flight for Atlas Pulse (AF-2028-015 remediation).

## Lessons learned

1. **In-UI transparency is the right interpretation.** AF-2028-015 surfaced the gap between training-only disclosure and in-UI disclosure for Norvale's Pulse pilot. Engineering took the lesson and is baking transparency into the design system.

2. **AI Act intersects with HIPAA at Cresta.** The RFC for EU AI Act Article 6 applicability to Atlas BuildingHub formalised the analysis. Limited Risk holds.

3. **Algorithmic impact assessments are not one-off.** Annual cadence is real work; the Q1 2029 cycle should reuse the Q1 2028 templates aggressively.

## Forward — 2029

- Atlas Pulse UI transparency release (AF-2028-015 closure).
- Monitor European Commission 2029 guidance on healthcare-adjacent AI for any Cresta-relevant reclassification.
- NIS2 supply-chain RFC enactment for Norvale.
