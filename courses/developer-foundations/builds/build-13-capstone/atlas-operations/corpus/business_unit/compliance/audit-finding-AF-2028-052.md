# Audit Finding AF-2028-052 · Whistleblower Hotline Acknowledgement Lag

**Auditor:** Atlas internal audit (NORAM Compliance)
**Audit cycle:** Q4 2028
**Status:** Closed
**Severity:** Minor
**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO)
**Co-owner:** Maya Schwartz (VP People)

## Finding

POL-WHISTLE-v1.0 commits Atlas to a 5-business-day triage on each report received via the anonymous hotline. The audit sampled the 23 reports received in 2028 and found 4 reports where triage was completed in 7-9 business days. All 4 reports were ultimately found to be employment-relations matters routed to HR (Maya Schwartz) for handling, none safety- or compliance-critical.

The cause was a vacation-coverage gap in the Compliance triage rotation.

## Affected stakeholders

- Internal Atlas employees who used the hotline. No specific reporter identified to the audit; reporter identities remain protected per POL-WHISTLE-v1.0.

## Remediation

- Triage rotation rebuilt with a primary and a deputy at all times.
- Vacation-coverage protocol documented.
- Quarterly self-audit of triage-time-to-acknowledgement.

## Closure criteria

- Rotation rebuilt: Yes.
- Documentation published: Yes.
- One quarterly self-audit clean: Yes.

## Closed

2028-12-22 — Dr Sara Vance accepted closure with Maya Schwartz co-signing.
