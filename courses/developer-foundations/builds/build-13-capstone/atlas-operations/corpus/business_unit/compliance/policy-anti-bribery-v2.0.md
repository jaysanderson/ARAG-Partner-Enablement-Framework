# Atlas Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy v2.0 (POL-ABAC-v2.0)

**Owner:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Effective:** 2028-02-15
**Review:** Annual
**Applies to:** All Atlas employees, contractors, partners, channel resellers

## Standards adopted

Atlas operates to the most restrictive of the U.S. FCPA, the UK Bribery Act 2010, and applicable EU and APAC anti-corruption regimes. A practice prohibited by any one is prohibited globally.

## Core prohibitions

- No facilitation payments, anywhere, ever.
- No gifts or hospitality to government officials beyond de minimis (USD 25) without CCO pre-approval.
- No gifts or hospitality between Atlas Sales and customer procurement decision-makers (Norvale Energy, Halcyon Logistics, Meridian Bank, Cresta Health Network, Talos Steelworks) exceeding USD 100 in aggregate per fiscal year without VP Sales + CCO approval.

## Third-party due diligence

- Channel partners and resellers are subject to integrity due diligence at onboarding and biennially.
- POL-VENDOR-v1.0 controls apply to procurement intermediaries.

## Books and records

Per SOX §404 and Atlas's own controls (see Atlas SOX §404 Position), all transactions are recorded accurately. Atlas Ledger is the system of record. No off-book accounts, period.

## Training

Annual completion required for all employees with customer-facing or procurement duties. Maya Schwartz tracks; Compliance audits.

## Reporting

The Atlas ethics hotline accepts anonymous reports. The CCO investigates with HR and, where appropriate, outside counsel. No retaliation.
