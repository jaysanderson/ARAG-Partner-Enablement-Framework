# Proposal — Cresta Health Network BAA Refresh and HIPAA Audit-Support Bundle

**Prepared by:** Dr Sara Vance (CCO) and Aisha Okonkwo (Director of Customer Success)
**Customer sponsor:** Cresta Health Network CCO
**Atlas commercial sponsor:** VP Sales (Atlas BuildingHub account)
**Date:** 2028-Q4

## Background

Cresta Health Network's Atlas BuildingHub deployment has been the proving ground for Atlas's HIPAA-relevant operating model in 2028. The post-INC-2028-0019 work matured into the AF-2028-007 closure, the AF-2028-028 follow-up, and the in-flight AF-2028-040 GDPR DPIA refresh. The 2026 BAA standard does not reflect Atlas's current operational maturity; the proposed BAA refresh is a natural next step at contract anniversary.

## What we propose

1. **2026 BAA refreshed to 2028 standard.** Atlas's standard refresh per the relevant RFC. Includes HIPAA-impact assessment commitment, 30-day Tier 1 sub-processor notice, 24-hour confirmed-PHI-exposure breach notification, and quarterly Atlas-side incident-registry summary.

2. **Annual HIPAA audit-support package.** Atlas delivers a pre-staged audit package at each Cresta HIPAA audit cycle. Saves Cresta compliance team estimated 4-6 weeks per cycle.

3. **DPIA refresh facilitation.** Aisha Okonkwo and Atlas Compliance co-author the 2028 DPIA refresh closing AF-2028-040.

4. **Continued joint regulator-engagement protocol.** Atlas maintains the protocol used through the 2028 incident arc.

## Commercial terms

The BAA refresh itself carries no incremental cost; it is an obligation Atlas would meet at contract anniversary regardless. The annual audit-support package and DPIA-refresh facilitation are a fixed-fee bundle priced in the deal addendum.

## Customer outcomes

- Cresta's HIPAA audit posture documentably improves.
- Cresta's compliance team workload during audit cycles reduces materially.
- Atlas BuildingHub renewal narrative remains strong.
