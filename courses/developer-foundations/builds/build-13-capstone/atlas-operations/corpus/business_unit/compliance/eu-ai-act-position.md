# Atlas EU AI Act Applicability Position (2028)

**Author:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer)
**Co-author:** Priya Anand (VP Engineering)
**Status:** Approved by Atlas Compliance Council

## Summary

This document is Atlas Global Industries' applicability position on the EU AI Act for each in-market Atlas product. Position is reviewed annually and republished to customers in EMEA at contract renewal.

## Product-by-product applicability

| Product | AI Act applicability | Risk tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Atlas E-220 | Not applicable | — | Cooling firmware is deterministic; no ML / AI in scope. |
| Atlas Pulse | Applicable | Limited risk | Smart-grid optimisation features use ML. Documented. |
| Atlas BuildingHub | Applicable | Limited risk | HVAC scheduling uses ML. Documented. HIPAA implications also reviewed (Cresta Health Network). |
| Atlas Logix | Applicable | Limited risk | Routing optimisation uses ML. Documented. Marcus Ortiz signs off. |
| Atlas FieldOps | Not applicable | — | No ML / AI in scope. |
| Atlas Grid | Applicable | Limited risk | Energy distribution optimisation uses ML. |
| Atlas Aura | Not applicable | — | Building automation control plane only. |
| Atlas Ledger | Not applicable | — | Financial ledger; no ML / AI in scope. |

## Limited-risk obligations (where applicable)

- Transparency to data subjects about AI involvement.
- Annual algorithmic impact assessment.
- Human oversight of automated decisions where a customer designates them as material.
- Data quality and governance commitments documented in the customer-data agreement.

## Customer impact

- **Norvale Energy** (EMEA) — Atlas Pulse pilot in scope. Applicability statement delivered Q2 2028.
- **Cresta Health Network** (EMEA) — Atlas BuildingHub in scope. Applicability statement reviewed annually as part of HIPAA review.
- Other EMEA customers — applicability assessed at contract.

## Renewal cadence

Position renewed annually. Last revision 2028-Q1; next revision Q1 2029.
