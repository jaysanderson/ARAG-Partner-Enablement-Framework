# Case Study · Cresta Aura 11% HVAC Energy Reduction

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Industry:** Healthcare (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Atlas products:** Atlas Aura, Atlas BuildingHub

## The headline

Across Cresta's four-campus Atlas Aura reference deployment (live since 2026), HVAC energy spend is down 11% year-over-year (calendar 2027 vs 2026 baseline). Independently verified by Cresta's facilities-economics team. This is the headline number powering the Q2 2028 7-campus Aura expansion proposal.

## What the number includes

- HVAC compressor energy.
- Chilled-water plant energy.
- Air-handling-unit fan energy.
- Auxiliary-loop pumps.

What it excludes: lighting, IT, clinical equipment. Aura's optimisation scope is HVAC-specific in Cresta's deployment.

## Why it works

- Aura's optimisation profile tunes set-points and load curves per zone, informed by Cresta's clinical-use-of-space data via the BuildingHub integration.
- The integration depth is non-trivial — Aisha Okonkwo's CS team spent the first quarter post-deployment co-tuning with Cresta's facilities engineers.

## Implementation lessons

- The first two campuses delivered 6% reduction in year 1, climbing to 11% in year 2 as the model adapted. Customers should expect a ramp.
- Clinical-area set-point constraints are non-negotiable. Aura's optimisation respects clinician-set comfort bands as a hard constraint.

## Compliance posture

- HIPAA — not in scope (EMEA deployment).
- EU AI Act — Aura's optimisation features in the limited-risk tier, with documentation maintained by Dr Sara Vance.

## Why the 11% matters commercially

- Cresta CFO board approval for the 7-campus expansion (proposal-cresta-aura-expansion.md) was contingent on the verified 11% figure.
- The number is now the reference point in pitches to peer healthcare networks across EMEA.
- It also strengthens the Cresta BuildingHub + Pulse + FieldOps cross-sell story (the cumulative Cresta footprint is now four products).

## What this case shows

- A measurable, customer-verified outcome figure is the most durable expansion lever an AE has.
- Year-2 results beat year-1; framing pilot expectations to acknowledge this protects credibility.
