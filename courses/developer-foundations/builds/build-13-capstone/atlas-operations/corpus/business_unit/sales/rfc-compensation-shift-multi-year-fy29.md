# RFC · Compensation Shift Toward Multi-Year Deals FY29

**Status:** Draft for comment.
**Authors:** VP Sales, CFO.
**Reviewers:** Sales leadership, Maya Schwartz (VP People), all AEs.

## Problem statement

The current AE comp plan accelerates on year-1 contract value but flat-lines on multi-year TCV. The Norvale Multi-Year MSA Refresh (proposal-norvale-multi-year-msa-refresh.md) and the Halcyon FieldOps three-year renewal (proposal-halcyon-fieldops-renewal.md) both required AE-time investment proportional to multi-year scope but comp accrued only on year-1. This is creating a disincentive to pursue long-term deals.

## Proposed change

Restructure year-2-onwards multipliers:

- Year-1 contract value — 1.00x comp multiplier (no change).
- Year-2 contract value — 0.50x comp multiplier (up from 0.20x).
- Year-3 contract value — 0.30x comp multiplier (up from 0.10x).
- Year-4+ contract value — 0.15x comp multiplier (up from 0.05x).

For the Norvale five-year MSA, this would roughly double the cumulative AE comp on the deal.

## Funding

- Margin model holds because multi-year deals reduce churn risk and lower customer-acquisition-cost amortisation per year.
- Annual escalator economics (pricing-renewal-uplift-policy.md) remain unchanged.

## Why now

- Three of the five strategic customers (Norvale, Halcyon, Talos) have multi-year deals either in flight or imminent.
- The cross-product expansion playbook (case-study-cross-product-expansion-playbook.md) implies AE-relationship continuity over 3-5 years; comp should align.

## Out of scope

- CSM comp (handled separately by VP Customer Success).
- Sales-director comp (handled separately by VP Sales).
- Specialist overlay comp (rfc-vertical-specialist-overlay.md).

## Open questions

1. Should the new multipliers apply retroactively to deals already booked in FY28, or only to deals booked in FY29 forward?
2. Should pilot-pricing deals (pricing-pilot-program-framework.md) qualify for the new multi-year multipliers if they convert?
3. How does the new model interact with strategic-customer-override compensation?

## Risks

- AE behavior may over-rotate to multi-year structures even when shorter terms better suit the customer.
- Year-2 multiplier may distort renewal-cycle incentives if a customer's renewal team interprets the AE's preference for multi-year as pressure.

## Decision deadline

Comments by 31 July 2028. Decision at the FY29 comp-planning cycle in Q4.
