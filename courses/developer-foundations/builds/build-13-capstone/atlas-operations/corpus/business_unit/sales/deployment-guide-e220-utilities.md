# Sales Deployment Guide · Atlas E-220 in Utilities

**Audience:** AEs and sales engineers pursuing Atlas E-220 turbines in utility-sector prospects.
**Reference customers:** Norvale Energy, Talos Steelworks.

## When this guide applies

- Prospect is a generation utility, captive industrial generator, or large facility operator.
- Prospect has multi-site generation footprint or growth roadmap.
- Prospect operates in EMEA, APAC, NORAM, or LATAM (E-220 is sold globally).

## Reference customer details

- **Norvale Energy:** 12 turbines across three EMEA sites. 99.94% trailing-12-month uptime. Six-year relationship (case-study-norvale-e220-six-year-relationship.md). Primary CSM Priya Anand.
- **Talos Steelworks:** 12 turbines across two APAC production sites, expanding to 20 (proposal-talos-e220-third-site.md). 99.91% trailing-12-month uptime. Primary CSM Liam Hayashi.

## Lead messaging

- Repair-for-Life programme — the strongest single TCO differentiator vs Vendor A.
- Operational reliability across the strategic-customer reference fleet — the 99.94% and 99.91% numbers carry weight.
- Engineering-executive ownership of incidents — Priya Anand's personal involvement in INC-2027-0142 is part of the pitch.

## Engineering ownership

- E-220 product owner: Priya Anand.
- Architecture consultation: Marcus Ortiz (cooling-loop architecture; site-utility integration).

## Operational lessons priced in

INC-2027-0142 (Q4 2027 cooling regression) is the most serious E-220 incident in Atlas's history. RB-E220-Cooling-001 is the remediation runbook. The post-incident specification includes a pre-deployment cooling-validation step now standard at every new E-220 deployment. Reference this proactively in proposals.

## Commercial mechanics

- Per-unit list + annual maintenance per regional book.
- Volume discount tiers documented per region.
- Strategic-customer overrides apply at Norvale and Talos (pricing-strategic-customers-2028.md).
- Multi-product bundle discount when E-220 lands with Pulse / Grid / FieldOps at the same customer.

## Implementation services sizing

- Per-turbine deployment: 80-110 person-days depending on site-utility integration depth.
- Per-site commissioning: 40 person-days fixed regardless of unit count.

## Compliance posture

- EU AI Act — not applicable (no AI features).
- NIST 800-53 r5 — applies for US federal customers (pricing-public-sector-supplement.md).
- GDPR — generally not in scope for generation equipment.

## What to avoid

- Pitching against Vendor A on per-unit list price; Atlas's win is TCO, not unit price.
- Pitching without engaging Priya Anand if the prospect's fleet size will exceed 20 units (custom pricing required).
