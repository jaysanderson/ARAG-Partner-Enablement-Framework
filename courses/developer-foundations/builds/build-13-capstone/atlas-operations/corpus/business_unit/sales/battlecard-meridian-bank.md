# Battlecard · Meridian Bank

**Customer:** Meridian Bank
**Industry:** Financial Services (NORAM)
**Primary CSM:** Dr Sara Vance (Chief Compliance Officer — also serves as named technical contact; financial-services customers typically engage with compliance leadership)
**Atlas tenure:** 3 years

## Current footprint

- Atlas Ledger production deployment across 14 markets.
- Atlas Grid pilot (energy distribution for data-centre energy management) — Phase 2.

## Key contacts at Meridian

- Chief Risk Officer — primary economic buyer (SOX §404 and GDPR exposure live in his org).
- Head of Cloud Engineering — technical buyer.
- VP Internal Audit — compliance buyer; engages with Dr Sara Vance directly.

## Strategic context

Meridian Bank is the most compliance-sensitive customer in Atlas's NORAM book. Every product deployment goes through their internal audit team. Atlas Ledger has held SOX §404 certification across three audit cycles without finding. Dr Sara Vance is the engineering executive Meridian trusts; she's the relationship-keeper.

## Competitive landscape

- Atlas Ledger competitors: Workday Financials, SAP S/4. Atlas wins on Ledger's separation-of-duties controls and the SOX §404 hardened audit posture.
- Atlas Grid for data centres: novel category; primary competitor is in-house power management. Atlas wins on operational visibility.

## Open expansion opportunities

- Atlas BuildingHub for Meridian's branch-network energy management — interest expressed.
- Atlas FieldOps for branch service teams — early conversation.

## Risks

- The 2027 Atlas Logix memory leak (INC-2027-0188) affected Meridian's Atlas Grid pilot (shared routing engine). Remediation was successful but the incident generated an audit finding (AF-2028-001, closed Q2 2028).
- GDPR controller-processor delineation needs annual refresh with Meridian Bank Internal Audit. Dr Sara Vance owns.

## Proof points

- 3 years of Atlas Ledger production with zero SOX §404 findings.
- Atlas Grid pilot is the first non-utility deployment of the product.
- INC-2027-0188 remediation closed Atlas-side in 12 weeks.
