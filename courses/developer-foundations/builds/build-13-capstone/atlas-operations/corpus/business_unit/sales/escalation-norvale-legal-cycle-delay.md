# Escalation · Norvale Legal Cycle Delay on Multi-Year MSA

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Industry:** Utilities (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Escalation owner:** VP Sales (EMEA)
**Status:** Open as of Q2 2028.

## Summary

Norvale's procurement legal team is signalling a 12-week, rather than the historical 6-9-week, review cycle on the Norvale Multi-Year MSA Refresh (proposal-norvale-multi-year-msa-refresh.md). The slip threatens the Q3 2028 close target and risks compounding into the Pulse-Grid promo deadline (30 September 2028; pricing-pulse-grid-bundle-promo-q3.md).

## What's driving the slip

- Norvale's General Counsel reorg in Q1 2028 has reduced senior bandwidth on Atlas's account.
- The five-year horizon of the MSA is itself triggering deeper-than-usual review.
- Norvale's procurement team has asked for a year-3 customer-side opt-out clause that did not exist in the legacy MSA. This clause has been agreed in principle but the legal drafting is the cycle-blocker.

## What Atlas has tried

- Priya Anand personally engaged Norvale's COO to seek expedited GC attention. Status: scheduled.
- Account executive ran a parallel-path attempt to pre-clear the Pulse-Grid promotion (proposal-norvale-pulse-expansion.md) onto the legacy MSA so the promo deadline is independently met. Status: legal complexity argues against this.

## Recommended next steps

1. Reframe the Pulse expansion deal to land on the legacy MSA terms even if the MSA refresh slips. Atlas accepts the 3% escalator on Pulse rather than the 1.5% the new MSA would have delivered. Trade-off accepted by VP Sales.
2. Continue MSA refresh cycle in parallel with the Pulse expansion close.
3. If MSA slips into Q4, the Grid POC (proposal-norvale-grid-poc.md) and FieldOps pilot (proposal-norvale-fieldops-pilot.md) absorb the same trade-off.

## Decision needed

VP Sales endorsement of the recommended dual-track approach. Dr Sara Vance compliance-side endorsement that running the Pulse expansion under the legacy MSA does not create EU AI Act documentation gaps (assessment in flight).

## Risk if not resolved

- Q3 close of Pulse expansion slips into Q4. Atlas misses the Pulse-Grid promo loyalty layer for this customer. Year-1 contract value impact: approximately EUR 70k.
- Risk of customer-side perception that Atlas's commercial process is rigid. Priya Anand's personal relationship mitigates but does not eliminate.
