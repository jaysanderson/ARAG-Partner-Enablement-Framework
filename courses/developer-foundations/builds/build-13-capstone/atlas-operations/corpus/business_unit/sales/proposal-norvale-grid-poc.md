# Proposal · Norvale Energy Atlas Grid POC

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Industry:** Utilities (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Drafting:** EMEA account executive, reviewed by Marcus Ortiz for Grid architecture scope.

## Executive summary

Norvale Energy has signalled intent to evaluate Atlas Grid as the distribution-side companion to the existing Atlas E-220 generation fleet and the Atlas Pulse storage units (currently in expansion pilot). This proposal scopes a six-month, single-region POC at Norvale's Karlsruhe distribution node.

## Why now

- The Pulse pilot is meeting the agreed acceptance criteria three months ahead of plan.
- Norvale's Chief Sustainability Officer is now actively sponsoring distribution-side modernisation in pursuit of the Carbon-Negative by 2030 alignment.
- Existing E-220 fleet uptime (99.94% trailing 12 months) gives the engineering buyer high confidence in Atlas reliability.
