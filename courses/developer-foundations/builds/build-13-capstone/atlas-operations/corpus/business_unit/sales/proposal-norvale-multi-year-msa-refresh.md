# Proposal · Norvale Multi-Year MSA Refresh

**Customer:** Norvale Energy
**Industry:** Utilities (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Priya Anand
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

The Atlas-Norvale master services agreement expires 31 December 2028. This proposal refreshes the MSA for a five-year term (2029-2033), consolidating the E-220 fleet, Pulse expansion, Grid POC, and forthcoming FieldOps pilot onto a single contract.

## Commercial framing

- Five-year multi-product framework
- Annual price escalator: 1.5% (negotiated down from the standard 3% in recognition of strategic-customer status per POL-PRICING-v4.0).
- Volume-cumulative discount mechanism: as Norvale's annual Atlas spend grows past EUR 5M, a 2-point tier discount applies to all incremental orders.
- Service-credit framework retained from the legacy MSA, including the customer-success-budget treatment used for the INC-2027-0142 credits.

## Why a five-year framework

- Norvale's grid-modernisation programme runs to 2033.
- Single MSA reduces friction on each subsequent SOW; current 6-9 week legal cycle drops to ~2 weeks for amendments under the new MSA.
- Locks Atlas as default vendor across generation + storage + distribution + field operations.

## Risks

- The five-year horizon is unusually long for Norvale procurement. Mitigation: include a customer-side opt-out clause at year-3 conditional on Atlas missing two consecutive annual performance reviews.
