# RFC · Vertical Specialist Overlay FY29

**Status:** Draft for comment.
**Authors:** VP Sales.
**Reviewers:** All AEs, all CSMs, Priya Anand, Dr Sara Vance.

## Problem statement

Atlas Sales is currently organised by geography (NORAM AE team, EMEA AE team, APAC AE team, LATAM AE team). Vertical specialisation lives implicitly in the strategic-customer CSM assignments but is not formalised in the AE org. As Atlas pursues new logos in banking (extending the Meridian story), healthcare (extending the Cresta story), heavy industry (extending the Talos story), and 3PL (extending the Halcyon story), the per-vertical knowledge gap at the AE layer is widening.

## Proposed change

Introduce a vertical-specialist overlay role per major vertical:

- **Banking & Financial Services specialist** — supports AEs pitching Atlas Ledger. Dr Sara Vance's compliance perspective is the natural mentor.
- **Healthcare specialist** — supports AEs pitching Atlas BuildingHub + Aura into healthcare. Aisha Okonkwo's deployment perspective is the natural mentor.
- **Heavy Industry specialist** — supports AEs pitching Atlas E-220 + Pulse into heavy industry. Liam Hayashi's APAC operational perspective is the natural mentor.
- **3PL specialist** — supports AEs pitching Atlas Logix + FieldOps into 3PL. Marcus Ortiz's architecture perspective is the natural mentor.
- **Utilities specialist** — supports AEs pitching Atlas E-220 + Pulse + Grid into utilities. Priya Anand's engineering perspective is the natural mentor.

Specialists do not own quota. They are commissioned via uplift on the deals they support.

## Why now

- New-logo pipeline in each vertical exists but conversion velocity lags strategic-customer expansion velocity.
- Deployment-guide assets (deployment-guide-ledger-banking.md, deployment-guide-buildinghub-healthcare.md, deployment-guide-e220-utilities.md, deployment-guide-fieldops-large-fleet.md, deployment-guide-pulse-grid-bundle.md) are now sufficient for specialist onboarding.

## Out of scope

- AE re-organisation (geography-based teams stay).
- Quota reallocation (specialists are overlay, not primary).

## Open questions

1. Should specialists be hired from external industry incumbents or developed internally from senior AEs?
2. How does the specialist overlay interact with the proposed shadow-CSM model (rfc-csm-account-coverage-model.md)?
3. Should specialists hold pricing-approval authority for their vertical's deals?

## Risks

- Overlay role can become a "second-guess" layer if the AE-specialist relationship is not well-defined.
- Specialists may be drawn into deal-execution work at the expense of cross-deal pattern-finding.

## Decision deadline

Comments by 31 August 2028. Decision at the Q4 Sales Leadership Forum.
