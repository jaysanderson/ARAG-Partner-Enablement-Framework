# Case Study · Talos E-220 Cooling Incident Handling

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Industry:** Heavy Industry (APAC)
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Atlas products:** Atlas E-220

## The headline

INC-2027-0142 (Q4 turbine cooling regression) affected one Talos production site. The Atlas response, executed concurrently across Talos and Norvale, took 38 days from open to close and did not interrupt the active Pulse pilot conversation at Talos. The incident handling is the reference for managing a multi-customer engineering crisis without losing commercial momentum.

## What happened operationally

- Cooling-loop sensor degradation in a specific batch of E-220 turbines.
- One Talos site affected; production capacity unaffected (Atlas-side service team intervened before customer-visible production impact).
- Priya Anand (VP Engineering) personally owned the response across all affected customers.
- RB-E220-Cooling-001 published 14 days after incident open; full remediation across the Talos fleet complete at day 30; full closure (including post-incident hardening at Norvale) at day 38.

## What happened commercially

- Service credit issued from CS budget — no sales-side concession.
- The active Talos Pulse pilot conversation continued without disruption; the pilot expansion proposal landed Q1 2028.
- Liam Hayashi led the Talos exec briefing; the Talos COO commented on the "clean separation" between Atlas's engineering response and the commercial relationship.

## What this case shows

- Engineering-executive ownership compresses incident-closure cycles. 38 days vs the industry average of 90+.
- The Atlas service-credit framework (POL-PRICING-v4.0 §7) is well-understood by customers; issuing the credit from the CS budget rather than as a sales discount preserves the commercial economics of subsequent expansion.
- An incident on one product does not have to derail negotiation on an adjacent product if the CSM holds the narrative.

## What we learned for runbook design

- The post-INC-2027-0142 RB-E220-Cooling-001 added a pre-deployment cooling-validation step now applied at every new E-220 deployment, including the in-flight proposal-talos-e220-third-site.md scope.
