# Proposal · Talos Atlas E-220 Third-Site Expansion

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Industry:** Heavy Industry (APAC)
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Talos is commissioning a third production site (Site C) and has invited Atlas to bid on the on-site generation fleet. This proposal scopes 8 × Atlas E-220 turbines plus associated implementation services.

**Year 1 total before discount: EUR 4,212,000.**
**Volume discount: 16% (10-unit cumulative effect across existing + new fleet).**
**Final Year 1 contract value: EUR 3,538,080.**
