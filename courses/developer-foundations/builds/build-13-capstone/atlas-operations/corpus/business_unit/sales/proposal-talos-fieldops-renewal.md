# Proposal · Talos Steelworks FieldOps Renewal

**Customer:** Talos Steelworks
**Industry:** Heavy Industry (APAC)
**Primary CSM:** Liam Hayashi
**Status:** Renewal cycle Q3 2028.

## Executive summary

Two-year renewal of Atlas FieldOps for Talos' 640 plant-maintenance and yard-operations devices across two production sites. Adds a third site (Yard C) and an Atlas FieldOps managed-service tier.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Year 1 cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| FieldOps device licenses (existing) | 640 | 1,843,200 |
| FieldOps device licenses (Yard C) | 180 | 518,400 |
| Managed-service tier | 1 | 168,000 |
| Implementation services (Yard C) | — | 64,000 |

**Year 1 total before discount: EUR 2,593,600.**
**Discount: 16%.**
**Year 1 contract value: EUR 2,178,624.**

## Operational lessons priced in

INC-2028-0034 (FieldOps offline-sync corruption) closed via RB-FieldOps-Sync-004 — Talos was one of the affected accounts (referenced in the Q1 2028 incident debrief). The managed-service tier added in this renewal is partly a response to Talos' expressed preference for Atlas-led operations of FieldOps after the incident.
