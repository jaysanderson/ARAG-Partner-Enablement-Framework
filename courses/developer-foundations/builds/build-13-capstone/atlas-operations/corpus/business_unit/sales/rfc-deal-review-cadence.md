# RFC · Deal-Review Cadence Restructure

**Status:** Draft for comment.
**Authors:** VP Sales.
**Reviewers:** Sales directors, sales operations, Priya Anand, Marcus Ortiz, Dr Sara Vance, Aisha Okonkwo, Liam Hayashi.

## Problem statement

The current deal-review cadence (weekly forecast call, monthly strategic-deal review, quarterly QBR) was designed when Atlas had a single-product portfolio. With 8 products and 5 strategic customers carrying multi-product portfolios, the weekly call is overflowing with cross-product context-switching and the monthly review is too coarse for proposals like proposal-norvale-multi-year-msa-refresh.md and proposal-talos-e220-third-site.md that require engineering-executive input.

## Proposed change

Restructure into three cadences:

1. **Weekly forecast call.** AE + sales director only. Forecast confidence, deal-stage movement. No strategic discussion.
2. **Bi-weekly strategic-deal review.** Engineering executive + CSM + AE + sales director. Cross-product proposals; pricing exceptions; engineering-scope sign-off. Replaces the monthly review.
3. **Quarterly customer business review.** Customer-side and Atlas-side senior leadership. Unchanged.

## Why now

- The bi-weekly cadence aligns with the typical proposal-development cycle (1-2 weeks from initial scope to AE-ready draft).
- The engineering-touched lane in the proposed deal-desk redesign (rfc-deal-desk-redesign.md) would feed naturally into the bi-weekly forum.

## What changes for engineering executives

- Priya Anand currently attends ad-hoc deal calls; the bi-weekly forum would replace ~6 ad-hoc touches per month with a single structured one.
- Same for Marcus Ortiz, Dr Sara Vance, Aisha Okonkwo, Liam Hayashi.

## Out of scope

- Customer-facing meeting cadence (QBRs unchanged).
- Sales-leadership offsites.

## Open questions

1. Should the bi-weekly forum be per-region or global?
2. Should the Pulse-Grid promo Q3 2028 deadline (pricing-pulse-grid-bundle-promo-q3.md) be handled in a dedicated cadence until 30 September 2028, or absorbed into the standard bi-weekly?
3. How are escalation cases handled outside the bi-weekly window?

## Risks

- Bi-weekly cadence may still be insufficient for time-pressured promo windows.
- Engineering executives may feel under-engaged on deals that fall between bi-weekly slots.

## Decision deadline

Comments by 30 June 2028. Decision at the Q3 Sales Leadership Forum.
