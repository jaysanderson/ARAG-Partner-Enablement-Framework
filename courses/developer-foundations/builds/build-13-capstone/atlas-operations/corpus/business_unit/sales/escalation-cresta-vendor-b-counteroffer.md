# Escalation · Cresta Vendor B Counteroffer on Aura Expansion

**Customer:** Cresta Health Network
**Industry:** Healthcare (EMEA)
**Primary CSM:** Aisha Okonkwo
**Escalation owner:** VP Sales (EMEA) + VP Customer Success.
**Status:** Open as of Q2 2028.

## Summary

Vendor B has tabled a counteroffer to the in-flight proposal-cresta-aura-expansion.md, addressing the Cresta CFO directly with a price approximately 9% below Atlas's 16%-discounted Year-1 contract value. The counteroffer arrives in a window when Cresta's CIO and facilities-economics team are otherwise supportive of Atlas. Risk: CFO drives a re-open of the commercial conversation that delays close past the Cresta board-approved estate-modernisation budget deadline (31 December 2028).

## What Atlas has

- Verified Cresta 11% HVAC energy reduction over 12 months (case-study-cresta-aura-energy-reduction.md).
- Demonstrated incident-response credibility from INC-2028-0019 (case-study-cresta-bldhub-firmware-recovery.md) + Atlas-funded firmware uplift SOW (proposal-cresta-bldhub-firmware-uplift.md).
- Aisha Okonkwo's 6-year operational relationship.
- Multi-product bundle discount already applied; Atlas is at the strategic-customer floor.

## What Atlas cannot do

- Match Vendor B's 9%-below price without triggering POL-PRICING-v4.0 §3 floor and requiring CFO sign-off plus engineering executive (Priya Anand) approval.
- Move below 16% discount without consuming the year-2 escalator headroom.

## Recommended next steps

1. CSM-led conversation with Cresta CFO. Frame: Atlas's TCO over the 7-campus rollout vs Vendor B's headline year-1 price. Use the case-study-cresta-aura-energy-reduction.md numbers.
2. Engineering-executive (Priya Anand) outreach to the Cresta CIO, reinforcing the post-INC-2028-0019 confidence and BuildingHub firmware stability commitment.
3. Reaffirm Atlas's no-surcharge stance on HIPAA-style deployments (irrelevant in EMEA but a structural commitment) versus Vendor B's history of bundled surcharges.
4. If a price concession is unavoidable: trade discount for multi-year commitment (Cresta currently on annual). The renewal-uplift-policy.md treatment makes this defensible.

## Decision needed

- VP Sales authorisation of any below-floor concession (CFO sign-off chain).
- VP Customer Success endorsement of trading multi-year commitment for discount.

## Risk if not resolved

- Cresta close slips past 31 December 2028. Year-1 contract value EUR 2.0M at risk.
- Worse: Atlas loses the 7-campus expansion outright to Vendor B. The 4-campus reference deployment remains but the lighthouse momentum is lost.
