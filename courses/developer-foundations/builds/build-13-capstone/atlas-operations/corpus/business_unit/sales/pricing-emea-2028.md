# Atlas EMEA Pricing & Discount Framework 2028

This document governs EMEA list pricing and approved-discount thresholds for all Atlas products. Governed by Atlas Pricing & Discount Policy `POL-PRICING-v4.0`.

## Atlas E-220 turbine

- **List price (EMEA):** EUR 425,000 per unit + EUR 18,000 annual maintenance.
- **Typical discount:** 8-12% for orders of 4-10 units. 14-18% for orders of 10+ units.
- **Volume threshold:** Above 20 units, custom pricing — approval from Priya Anand required.

## Atlas Pulse

- **List price (EMEA):** EUR 185,000 per unit + EUR 12,000 annual maintenance.
- **Typical discount:** 10-15%.
- **Pilot pricing:** 30% off for first 4 units in a verified pilot deployment (max 12 months).

## Atlas BuildingHub

- **List price (EMEA):** EUR 28,000 per site-controller + EUR 4,800 per zone-extender.
- **Typical discount:** 12-18%.
- **HIPAA-deployment surcharge:** None (Atlas does not surcharge regulated deployments — competitive differentiator).

## Atlas Logix

- **List price (EMEA):** EUR 195,000 base + EUR 0.04 per shipment routed.
- **Typical discount:** 15-22%.
- **Volume threshold:** Above 1M shipments/month, custom pricing.

## Atlas FieldOps

- **List price (EMEA):** EUR 240 per device per month.
- **Typical discount:** 10-15% on annual prepay.

## Atlas Ledger

- **List price (EMEA):** EUR 380,000 base + EUR 28,000 per market.
- **Typical discount:** 12-18%.
- **SOX-compliant deployment:** No surcharge; competitive differentiator vs Workday Financials.

## Approval matrix

| Discount level | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 15% | Account executive |
| 15-22% | Sales director |
| 22-28% | VP Sales + CFO sign-off |
| 28%+ | Sales VP + CFO + Priya Anand (for engineering products) / Dr Sara Vance (for compliance-touched products) |

## Service credits

Service credits for incidents (e.g., INC-2027-0142 issued €1.2M to Norvale; INC-2027-0188 to Halcyon) come out of the dedicated customer-success budget — not the sales discount pool.

## Compliance

Atlas pricing transparency required by Atlas Code of Conduct `POL-CONDUCT-v3.1`. All discount approvals logged in the Atlas approval system.
