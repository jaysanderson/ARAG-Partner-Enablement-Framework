# Atlas LATAM Pricing & Discount Framework 2028

Governs LATAM list pricing and approved discount thresholds. Anchored on `POL-PRICING-v4.0`. LATAM is Atlas's smallest sales region; pricing is set to win incremental footprint without eroding the global price book.

## Atlas E-220 turbine

- **List price (LATAM, USD):** 455,000 per unit + USD 20,000 annual maintenance.

## Atlas Pulse

- **List price (LATAM, USD):** 195,000 per unit + USD 13,000 annual maintenance.

## Atlas Grid

- **List price (LATAM, USD):** 175,000 per unit + USD 9,000 annual maintenance.
- LATAM Grid pilots are encouraged — see Halcyon LATAM yard-power proposal as the reference shape.

## Atlas BuildingHub

- **List price (LATAM, USD):** 30,000 per site-controller + USD 5,000 per zone-extender.

## Atlas Aura

- **List price (LATAM, USD):** 200,000 site license.

## Atlas Logix

- **List price (LATAM, USD):** 200,000 base + USD 0.044 per shipment routed.

## Atlas FieldOps

- **List price (LATAM, USD):** 260 per device per month.

## Atlas Ledger

- Not currently sold in LATAM.

## LATAM-specific notes

- All LATAM deals are quoted in USD; FX risk handled at contract layer.
- Customs and shipping windows can slip — implementation services carry a 6-week buffer baseline.
- Local-language documentation (Portuguese, Spanish) provided as part of implementation services at no extra charge.

## Approval matrix

LATAM follows global thresholds (POL-PRICING-v4.0). Strategic-customer overrides apply only to Halcyon LATAM (Marcus Ortiz CSM ownership).
