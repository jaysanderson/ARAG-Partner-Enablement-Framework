# Proposal · Meridian Bank FieldOps Back-Office Tech Pilot

**Customer:** Meridian Bank
**Industry:** Financial Services (NORAM)
**Primary CSM:** Dr Sara Vance
**Status:** Submitted Q2 2028.

## Executive summary

Meridian's branch technology team currently lacks a unified field-service platform for ATM, branch-IT, and HVAC technicians. This proposal pilots Atlas FieldOps across the Northeast US branch network — 220 devices, three branch districts.

## Scope

| Component | Quantity | Pilot cost (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas FieldOps device licenses (220 devices, 6 months) | 220 | 316,800 |
| Implementation services | — | 110,000 |
| Atlas Ledger work-order adapter | — | 48,000 |

**Pilot total: USD 474,800.**
**Pilot discount: 22%.**
**Final pilot value: USD 370,344.**

## Why now

- Meridian's CIO has earmarked branch technology modernisation as a FY29 priority.
- Atlas Ledger is already deeply embedded at Meridian (now extended into Phase 2 Grid + Multi-Market Add-On). The Ledger work-order adapter lets FieldOps inherit Meridian's existing chart of accounts.

## Engineering coordination

- Liam Hayashi — FieldOps platform owner.

## Compliance posture

- SOX §404 — adapter design preserves Ledger's audited control surface.
- NIST 800-53 r5 — FieldOps platform's existing NIST coverage will be re-affirmed for Meridian's specific deployment shape (Dr Sara Vance leads).
- GDPR — not applicable in NORAM scope.

## Risks

- Branch change-management is conservative at Meridian. Pilot scope deliberately limited to three districts to keep change-control complexity manageable.
