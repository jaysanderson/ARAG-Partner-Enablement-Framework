# Annual Renewal Uplift Policy

Defines the annual price escalator applied at each renewal cycle. Anchored on POL-PRICING-v4.0 §9.

## Standard escalator

- 3.0% annual price increase, applied to the per-unit license rate and per-device subscription rate at each renewal anniversary.
- Maintenance line: separately indexed at 2.5%.

## Strategic-customer reductions

- Strategic customers (Norvale, Halcyon, Meridian, Cresta, Talos) may negotiate down to 1.5% annual escalator, subject to multi-year commitment.
- Below 1.5% requires VP Sales + CFO approval.

## Per-deal precedent

- Norvale Multi-Year MSA Refresh — 1.5% escalator agreed (five-year framework).
- Halcyon Logistics FieldOps Renewal 2028 — 2.0% escalator (three-year).
- Talos FieldOps Renewal — 2.0% escalator (two-year).
- Cresta Aura Expansion — 2.5% escalator (multi-year forward; no MSA refresh required).
- Meridian Ledger Multi-Market Add-On — 2.5% escalator (FY29 onward).

## Mechanics

- Escalator applied to net renewal price (after any active discounts), not gross list.
- Bundle add-on discounts persist across renewals as long as the qualifying product set remains in place.
- If a product is dropped from the customer's footprint mid-cycle, the bundle tier recalculates at the next renewal anniversary.

## Non-negotiables

- Volume-cumulative thresholds, once earned, persist across renewals.
- Service-credit handling (CS budget, not sales discount pool) is not affected by escalator mechanics.
- Multi-year discount cannot be combined with a fresh pilot-pricing discount on a previously-piloted product within the same SOW.

## AE handling

AEs must surface the escalator value to the customer at proposal stage rather than at signature. Hiding the escalator inside fine print is a violation of POL-CONDUCT-v3.1 and will be treated as a sales-ethics issue.
