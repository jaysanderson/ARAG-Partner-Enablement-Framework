# Public-Sector Pricing Supplement

Atlas's public-sector engagements operate under a different approval chain (Dr Sara Vance for compliance-applicability sign-off) and a slightly tighter discount frame. Anchored on POL-PRICING-v4.0 §8.

## Eligibility

A deal is treated as public-sector if any of the following hold:

- Customer is a US federal agency, state, or local government.
- Customer is an EU institution or member-state public body.
- Customer is a public health network within an OECD member state.
- Customer is a state-owned utility or transport operator.

## Discount frame

| Discount level | Authority |
|---|---|
| 0-10% | Account executive (no change) |
| 10-18% | Sales director |
| 18-25% | VP Sales + Dr Sara Vance |
| 25%+ | VP Sales + CFO + Dr Sara Vance |

The Dr Sara Vance sign-off is mandatory and exists to ensure compliance-side obligations (NIST 800-53 r5, EU AI Act, GDPR, public-procurement transparency) are not silently traded away.

## Atlas Repair-for-Life programme

Public-sector procurement officers tend to weight TCO heavily. Atlas Repair-for-Life is the strongest commercial differentiator vs Vendor A and Vendor B in public-sector RFPs. AE materials should lead with TCO.

## Reference patterns

- Atlas E-220 federal deployment under NIST 800-53 r5: standard pricing, no surcharge.
- Atlas Ledger SOX-compliant deployment under SOX §404: standard pricing, no surcharge. The Meridian Bank Atlas Ledger deal is the reference even though Meridian is private-sector — the compliance posture is the relevant precedent.
- Atlas FieldOps public-sector device tier: USD 270/device/month, with annual prepay required.

## Excluded products

- Atlas Aura is not currently sold into public-sector under this supplement (data-residency questions outstanding).

## Approvals discipline

Dr Sara Vance maintains a live log of approved public-sector exceptions. AE must reference the most recent precedent before quoting.
